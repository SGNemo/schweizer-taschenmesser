# Word list

`wordlist-eff-large.json` is the **EFF Large Wordlist for Passphrases** (7776 words) by the
Electronic Frontier Foundation, https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/07/new-wordlists-random-passphrases,
licensed under **CC BY 3.0 US** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/). The dice numbers were
dropped; the words are unchanged.
