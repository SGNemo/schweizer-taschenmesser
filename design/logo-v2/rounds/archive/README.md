# Archive – exploration rounds

Sheets (`sheet.png`), parameter files (`variants.mjs`, `*/params.json`) and the layers of rounds 1–8 of the logo v2 design with the maintainer, plus `icon-v1-NOTES.md`, the decision log of the previous logo (clownfish tile C12 and wordmark Q2; its workshop `design/icon/` was removed, see git history).

They are records, not runnable: they were rendered with the master of their time. Rounds 2–6 use Inter and Nunito letter outlines and rounds 1–4 the head, bands and line directions, which the final master no longer contains (`git log -- design/logo-v2/mark.mjs design/logo-v2/wordmark.mjs` has them). In rounds 2–8 the transparent mark was cut off 12 units at the fan tail's back edge (fixed in the final master; the tiles were never affected).
