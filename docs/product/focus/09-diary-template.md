# Focus audit 2 – phase 0: 3-day usage diary (template)

Purpose: evidence base for the second focus audit. One line per entry, write it right after the moment, not afterwards. Keep it rough; "no idea" is a valid answer. Do not put real names, amounts, addresses or secrets in it – describe things in general terms ("a bill", "a call").

Use for 3 ordinary days (no need to change habits). Also note moments when you did **not** open the app but wanted to (paper, other app, memory).

| Time | Wanted (goal) | Did (app / other app / paper / nothing) | Slowed or annoyed me | Feeling 1–5 (1 bad, 5 good) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 08:10 | e.g. "see what is due today" | opened home, scrolled | too many cards | 3 |

Optional daily footer (3 lines, 1 minute):
1. The one moment the app helped most today.
2. The one moment it was in the way most today.
3. Something I wanted to do and could not (or did elsewhere).

Hand-over: paste the table into the chat, or tell the points by voice-to-text. Partial data is fine; the audit can also start from the answers to the four questions.
