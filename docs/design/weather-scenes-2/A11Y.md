# Weather round 2 – accessibility, motion, cost

Numbers come from `mockups/measure.mjs` (Chromium, the mockup drawing; it writes `web/.screens/weather-scenes-2/measure.md`). The product repeats them as tests in package A and D.

## 1. Contrast of text on the picture

**Rule of the drawing:** under text stands only what belongs to the sky's own tone family. Left of the quiet edge every far layer is mixed 85 % toward the sky's lower colour, the massif included; no single tree, no building, no bolt, no cloud bank stands there; fog bands start right of it; in the dark theme the day clouds are dark. Chips, pills, the module row and the gear keep their own opaque backing (round 1). On the phone the picture carries only the temperature and the detail line, and the crop is scaled so the summits stay below them.

**Tests in the product:** `contrast.test.ts` gets every sky key × theme against the hazed layer tones (not only against the gradient stops), the deck tones, and the phone crop; limit 4.5 : 1.

## 2. What the picture shows is also a word

The scene stays `aria-hidden`. Every level it draws has a text beside it:

| In the picture | As text |
| --- | --- |
| cloud 0–4, veil | the weather word (klar … bedeckt, Schleierwolken), "Bewölkung n %" in the rest line |
| rain, snow by level | the word (leichter Regen · Regen · starker Regen …), the amount on the hour cards |
| thunder 1–3, hail | leichtes Gewitter · Gewitter · starkes Gewitter (mit Hagel) |
| fog 1–2 | Nebel · dichter Nebel, "Sicht n km" |
| wind 0–3 and its direction | the wind chip ("Wind 46 km/h SW"), from level 2 "windig", level 3 "Sturmböen" |
| snow cover, frost, heat | Schnee liegt · Frost · Hitze, "Schneehöhe" in Mehr Werte |
| how sunny | "Sonnenschein n h", the UV chip |
| the moon between clouds, its phase | the moon chip and the card "Mond & Himmel" |

| the curve | one sentence per series |
| the chosen day or hour | the stepper's label, the head's detail line, the checked radio, one polite announcement |

## 3. Motion

- Five seconds after opening on "now", then still; never when a day or an hour is chosen (200 ms cross-fade of opacity instead); none with `prefers-reduced-motion`, `data-motion="reduce"`, "Standbild", "Schlicht", "Ruhig", "Modulfarben: Aus", a hidden page, or in a small version. Only `transform` and `opacity`.
- **Bolts never move.** The glow at the cloud brightens once (1.2 s up, 1.2 s down, from second 1). WCAG 2.3.1: one brightening in five seconds, against a limit of three per second, on an area of about 1 % of the stage. WCAG 2.2.2: everything that moves ends by itself within five seconds. FOCUS: it is part of the opening answer, it is under the existing switch "Bild im Wetter-Kopf", and it never repeats.
- At most **9 animated groups** per scene (the mockup's heaviest scene has 6); the still frame after the motion equals the picture drawn without motion.

## 4. Ceilings

| | Round 1 | Round 2 | Mockup, heaviest state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Elements in the scene SVG | ≤ 220 | **≤ 220** | 194 |
| Markup | ≤ 48 KB | **≤ 64 KB** after the skylines and the staffel are drawn once and repeated with `<use>` | 83.0 KB, unoptimised |
| Animated groups | ≤ 9 | **≤ 9** | 6 |
| First paint of the scene, phone profile (4× CPU) | – | **within one frame** | 20.2 ms (the frame floor) |
| Main thread during the 5 s of motion | ≤ 60 ms | **≤ 60 ms** | product measure (package D) |
| At rest | about 0 ms, no long task | same | – |

Why the markup ceiling rises: six mountains with ribs, six kinds of trees and the cloud banks are the detail the maintainer asked for; the paint time did not rise with it. Package A lands at or under 64 KB or names in its PR what it could not fold.

## 5. Keyboard, pointer, screen reader

| Control | Keys | Size | Name / state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| day stepper `‹` `›` | Tab, Enter / Space | 44 × 44 px | "Einen Tag zurück" / "Einen Tag weiter"; inactive at the ends (`aria-disabled`, still focusable) |
| "Heute", "Jetzt", "Ganzer Tag" | Tab, Enter / Space | ≥ 44 px high | visible text |
| days (radio group "Tag wählen") | one Tab stop; ← → ↑ ↓ choose; Home / End | row ≥ 44 px | each radio: day, word, high / low; checked |
| hours (radio group "Stunde wählen") | one Tab stop; ← → choose; the row scrolls the focused card into view | card ≥ 44 px wide and high | each radio: time, word, temperature; checked |
| curve select "Anzeigen" | native | ≥ 44 px high | label "Anzeigen" |
| "Mehr Werte" | Enter / Space | ≥ 44 px high | `aria-expanded` |

- **Focus:** the app's 2 px focus ring on the row or card (the radio itself is visually hidden, not `display: none`); focus stays on the control used; nothing traps it.
- **Checked is not colour alone:** tint, a 2 px frame and a check mark.
- **Swipe** on the picture has button equivalents right under it (WCAG 2.5.1); it does not start at the screen edges.
- **Announcements:** one polite sentence per change of the moment, none on first render; the rain preview is not a live region (it would speak on every refresh).
- **Reading order** equals the visual order of [CONCEPT-INFO §1](CONCEPT-INFO.md); headings: place (h1), then one h2 per section.

## 6. Checklist for the packages' accessibility review

1. Every text on the picture ≥ 4.5 : 1 in every sky and both themes, desktop and phone (test, plus eyes on the six scenes of the PR).
2. The scene is `aria-hidden`; nothing is told by the picture alone (table in §2).
3. No motion under any off switch; the glow runs once; nothing blinks.
4. Every control reachable and usable with the keyboard alone; visible focus; targets ≥ 44 px.
5. The chosen day and hour are announced and marked without colour.
6. 200 % zoom and 320 px width: no clipped text in the head, the stepper, the cards.
