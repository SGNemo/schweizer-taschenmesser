## 0. In one paragraph

The scenario is not a planning problem – the planner already distributes everything honestly – it is an **intake** problem: getting things out of the head and out of the mailbox without decisions, and without seeing the pile. Today the many-lines sheet asks a type per line, turns a payment reminder into a booking and stops the batch for it (U01, U03, U04); a mail needs three taps and a dialog per thing, loses the intent and leaves no way back (U07–U09); „automatic“ invoices hide behind two settings (U10); the planner page opens with the whole waiting list (U12). The target keeps every surface and changes four things, all for every user: **F1** the many-lines sheet becomes calm (targets as text, no selects, no booking, the pile hidden behind it, one closing line); **F2** „In den Plan“ on a mail row, a local filter „Persönlich“, a folder batch with a preview, a link back to the mail; **F3** a recognised invoice mail shows payee, amount and date with one verb, a payment reminder raises the dunning level of the invoice that is already open instead of making a second one; **F4** the planner folds what waits and names today's next step. Measured: five things on the PC 53 → 42 s with no detour and nothing of the pile visible beside the sheet, two mails into the plan 19 → 4 s, the planner page from 29 rows to the board only. Two things stay open by design and are said so: the home screen still shows „Jetzt wichtig“ before the „+“ (S1's abort criterion; the pile is covered only once the sheet is open), and the phone's „in passing“ path keeps its 1 tap per thing (37 s for three things) unless the maintainer pulls the explicit mode forward (Q1 d: 30 s).

## 2. New functions

Each: problem in the scenario → idea → how it applies to all users → form → focus checklist 1–9 → rule conflicts → smallest first step → effort. Variants minimal / medium / large with a recommendation for F1 and F2 (the two with a real design choice); F3 and F4 have one sensible size.

### F1 The many-lines sheet becomes calm (U01, U02, U03, U04, U05)

**Problem.** „Mein Kopf läuft über und es ist gefühlt zu schwer, alles aufs Blatt zu bekommen.“ As soon as the sheet holds a second line it shows a 12-entry select per line, turns a money line into a booking and asks for a second „Alle speichern“, and the „Jetzt wichtig“ cards stay visible around it.

**Idea (medium, recommended).** Nothing to discover: the **same sheet**, the moment it holds more than one line, takes a calm shape – the hint „Eine Zeile = ein Eintrag.“ gains the new sentence „Sortiert wird später.“ (German and English); each line shows its target as **text** („ToDo“, „Rechnung · 13. Okt. · Datum angenommen“ – an invoice needs a date, the parser's 14-day assumption and its hint chip stay –, „Termin · Do 9 Uhr“), a tap on the text opens the choice (the select is gone from the first view); no line books money (a money line with an invoice word becomes an invoice, one without becomes a ToDo with the amount in its title and a tap-able chip „Als Ausgabe buchen“ that opens the single sheet with the line prefilled); a reminder line („Mahnung Stadtwerke 87,40 € zahlen“) goes through the same open-invoice lookup as F3 and reads „Mahnung zu Rechnung Stadtwerke, fällig 17.9. · vermerken“ instead of making a second invoice (two equally good candidates: the line stays a ToDo „Mahnung Stadtwerke prüfen“ – nothing asks while unloading); a line with a read date shows the date, nothing asks a question while the person is unloading (deadline marking happens in the sorter or on the board, U14); the sheet gets an **opaque backdrop the moment it opens** (every sheet, single or many lines; the page stays mounted, nothing jumps while typing) – on the phone it covers the page anyway, on the PC the „Jetzt wichtig“ cards are no longer visible beside it (the measurement reads the backdrop's opacity – today `rgba(0,0,0,0.45)` – and counts the cards behind a translucent one); „Alle speichern (5)“ closes with one line: „5 Dinge gespeichert. Sortiert wird später. · Ansehen · Rückgängig“ („Ansehen“ opens the ToDo page's inbox sorter, or the planner when it is on). The single-line sheet is unchanged (Enter saves, the booking draft and „Buchen“ stay).

### F2 „In den Plan“ from a mail (U07, U08, U09, U17)

**Problem.** „E-Mails, die ich beantworten oder lesen muss, die aber untergehen … will über die App diese Mails finden und in den Planner bekommen.“ Today a mail becomes a ToDo in three taps and a dialog, named after its subject, with a web link in its note that carries the person's own address; there is no way from the plan back into the mail, no view for personal mail, and no batch.

**Idea.** One action **„In den Plan“** on a mail row and in the thread view (with the planner off it reads „Als ToDo“). The mail module calls `saveCapture('plan', …)` (`'todo'` while the planner is off) – the same module-free layer `LinkDialog` uses with its six types today; `plan` becomes a seventh caller-side type for mail, offered only while `availableTypes` lists it (planner and ToDos on). The title carries name and topic – „Anna Beispiel · Wochenende?“ (Q3; a verb only when the heuristics are sure, e.g. „bezahlen“ for an invoice mail, which F3 handles instead) – `estimateMin: 15`, a soft due when the text names a date, and `link: { kind: 'mail', id: '<thread id>', label: subject }` (a thread id, never an address or the device-local account key). The ToDo's note keeps the mail's web URL **without** `?authuser=<address>` (U17), so the link opens on every device; on the desktop the plan row and the ToDo row offer „Zur Mail“ (in-app, the mail page learns `?open=<thread>` and treats the id as untrusted input: format check, looked up only in the local cache or the provider's own account, no other effect), elsewhere the link opens the web URL as today. The mail row shows a quiet „im Plan“ text from the module's own `link` row, written with the returned id as a link of type `todo` (`LINK_TYPES` has no `plan`; what the person sees is a ToDo, so no schema change). **Cross-device duplicate guard:** the ToDo id is deterministic, `mail-<hash(thread id)>` – the Gmail thread id alone (unique inside a mailbox; a collision between two mailboxes is negligible and would end in „schon im Plan“, never in data loss), so no address and no device-local account key enters any synced id – passed as an optional `id` in `SaveOptions` of `targets/index.ts`. The write is **create-if-absent** through `createMany([{ data, id }])`, the repo's only primitive that skips an existing id, tombstones included (`repo.create` would overwrite and revive, so it is not used): an empty result means the entry exists, **both stages stop** (no `upsert` of the planner item, nothing reset), the `SavedCapture` carries `existed: true` and no undo, only the device's own `link` row is added. A live row says „schon im Plan“; a tombstone (the person's own earlier „Rückgängig“, or a done and deleted ToDo of a thread that got a new reply) says „War im Plan · Wieder einplanen“ and that verb restores the row and runs the second stage. These guarantees hold once the first device's entry has synced; two devices that tap the same mail before a sync both create the same id and converge by field LWW, each undo removing only rows its own tap created. Toast „Im Plan: Mi · Rückgängig“. **Finding the mails (medium):** a local filter chip **„Persönlich“** in the mail list – no AI, no count, no badge – keeps threads whose sender is a person: no `List-Unsubscribe`/`Precedence: bulk` header and not a Gmail bulk category. The onboarding scan already reads `List-Unsubscribe` (`connectors/google/mail.ts:77`, `MailMeta.bulk`); the thread list fetch (`gmail.ts:239`, `metadataHeaders: ['From','Subject']`) gets the same headers. Gmail is the only provider today; the rule is header-based so a later provider inherits it. **Batch (medium):** „Alle in den Plan…“ on a folder or the filter → a preview list with tick boxes (personal senders ticked, bulk and already-linked threads unticked), one „In den Plan (8)“, one undo. **Large:** the automation template „Mail mit Label ‚Antworten‘ → in den Plan“ – this **reverses a decision** (`decisions/automation.md`: mail only reports, mail events carry no title or id so the rule log keeps nothing from a mail), needs a thread id and a sender name in the event, runs only while the app runs with mail notifications on, and would write names into ToDos without a look; if ever, only as `suggest` with a preview.

```
┌ Posteingang · Persönlich ────────────── Alle in den Plan… ┐           ┌───────────────────────────────┐
│ Anna Beispiel · Wochenende?             07:40  [In den Plan] │         │ Anna Beispiel · Wochenende?  15 Min│
│ Max Muster · Fotos vom Ausflug      17. Sept.  im Plan · Mi  │         │ Mail · ⋯ Erledigt · Später · Zur Mail│
│ Hausverwaltung · Termin Ablesung   28. Sept.  [In den Plan] │         └───────────────────────────────┘
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**For all users.** Any mail, any folder; the filter needs no label and no filter chat.

**Form.** Mail module (row action in `rowActions.ts`, thread action next to „Aus dieser Mail anlegen“, filter chip, folder head action, the `?open=` route, the preview list on the importer's `ImportPreview` pattern), `core/mail/heuristics.ts` (personal/bulk from the headers already read by the scan), `core/mail/prefill.ts` (title rule, URL without address), `LinkDialog.tsx` (`extra.link`), planner page (`link.kind === 'mail'` → „Zur Mail“ beside „Zur Notiz“), ToDo row (open a mail link in the app on the desktop). Contract: one additive optional `id` in `SaveOptions` (`quickCapture/targets/index.ts`) with create-if-absent, announced in CHATS.md as a hotspot row; the bus event `plan.requested` stays as it is (a bus emit returns nothing, so the mail module could not write its link row from it – the capture layer returns the ids). Unit tests: two devices with different account keys produce the same id; an existing live id stops both stages and registers no undo; a tombstoned id offers the restore path.

**Focus checklist.** 1 Stimulus: one more row action (on hover on the PC, in the ⋯ menu and as swipe on the phone, like „Archivieren“), „im Plan“ and „Persönlich“ as muted text, no counts; 2 Off switch: the filter chip and the batch are switchable in the mail settings („Persönlich anbieten“, „Alle in den Plan anbieten“, both on), the template off; the single row action is a plain row action like „Archivieren“; 3 Next step: the toast names the day; 4 Wording: „Anna Beispiel · Wochenende?“, never „unbeantwortet seit“, never a count of unanswered mails; 5 Interruption: ToDo and link row exist after one tap; 6 Tests: title rule (unit; „Re:“, bulk senders, two mails of one person stay two topics), deterministic id, create-if-absent and the „schon im Plan“ path (unit), `isolation.test.ts` stays green, path B of the use-case script (2 taps for two mails); 7 Readability: „Mail“ is a word in the row; 8 Clicks: 3 taps + dialog → 1 tap per mail; 9 Removed instead: the address in the stored URL (U17) and the ToDo's dependence on a web link for its source.

**Rule conflicts.** Modules never import each other: the mail module writes through the capture target layer, as `LinkDialog` does today. **Decision change:** „Mail → Nemo entries“ (`decisions/features.md`, 2026-11-02) made the capture sheet's live preview the rule („Vorschau-Pflicht“, save only on confirm); the one-tap row action replaces the preview with the title it will write visible in the row's hover/menu text, the toast and undo – that is a change the maintainer decides (Q2), the batch keeps a real preview, and „Aus dieser Mail anlegen“ keeps the sheet. Privacy: the ToDo title (name and subject) and the link label are ordinary ToDo data – synced, backed up, readable by the local API, MCP and the AI import like any typed ToDo; the mail body never leaves the sealed cache except what the person ticks into a note themselves (share target, „Anfang der Mail als Notiz“); nothing of this reaches the assistant (it receives schemas, never entries), but a person who uses MCP or the import hands a model their own ToDo titles – Q3 therefore also offers the variant (b) in which the title is the sender's name only and the link label is the fixed word „Mail“ (`taskLinkSchema.label` is required); the name itself is still synced under every variant, the body never. Mail cache, `link` table and thread bodies stay `local: true`. A **batch** writes many names at once – therefore the preview with tick boxes, never a silent batch. Sync: nothing new. `accounts` untouched.

**Gebaut anders:** „In den Plan“ is a quiet button that appears when pointing at the row (PC); on touch it is in ⋯ and a swipe – no standing button per row; the row mark reads „Im Plan“ without the day (the toast names the day); „Persönlich“ and „Alle in den Plan…“ stand above the list (with search and „Aktualisieren“), not in a folder head.

**Smallest first step.** `LinkDialog` passes `extra.link`, the URL loses the address (U17), the thread view gets „In den Plan“, the plan row says „Mail“ and offers „Zur Mail“: 2 taps per mail (open, button).

### F3 „Stadtwerke Musterstadt · 84,30 € · fällig 15.11. · Anlegen“ – invoices and payment reminders from the list (U10, U03 for mail)

**Problem.** „Mahnungen, Rechnungen, alles irgendwie am liebsten automatisch, aber trage jetzt eben alles einzeln ein.“ The heuristics already find payee, amount and date; the result feeds a widget count and a suggest-notice behind two settings. A reminder for an invoice that is already open would create a second one.

**Idea.** A mail the heuristics read as an invoice with a certain amount shows one muted second line with the values that will be written and one verb: „Stadtwerke Musterstadt · 84,30 € · fällig 15.11. · **Anlegen**“. One tap writes the invoice through the quick-capture `invoice` target (payee, amount, due, note with the address-free URL; id `mail-<hash(message id)>` – the **message**, because a reminder usually answers in the same thread – create-if-absent as in F2) and ends in „Rechnung angelegt · Rückgängig“; the existing `plannable` source puts it into the plan with a hard due („Stadtwerke Musterstadt bezahlen · 10 Min · Frist 15.11.“ – the plan row says „Rechnung“, invoices have no `links`, so it does not say „Mail“). **Payment reminder:** when the words say Mahnung/Zahlungserinnerung/Zahlungsaufforderung the target layer first looks for an **open invoice** (a module-free lookup in `quickCapture/targets`, the layer that already knows the invoice fields) in this order: the reference or invoice number from the mail; else a normalised payee prefix („Stadtwerke“ ⊂ „Stadtwerke Musterstadt GmbH“, also after „im Auftrag von“) with an amount **at or above the open rest** (`payments[]` counted) within a fee tolerance; several candidates → the oldest overdue, and with two equally good ones the preview opens with a picker. Found → the line reads „Mahnung zu Rechnung Stadtwerke, fällig 17.9.
