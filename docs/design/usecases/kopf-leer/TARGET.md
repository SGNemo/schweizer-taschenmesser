## 0. In one paragraph

The scenario is not a planning problem – the planner already distributes everything honestly – it is an **intake** problem: getting things out of the head and out of the mailbox without decisions, and without seeing the pile. Today the many-lines sheet asks a type per line, turns a payment reminder into a booking and stops the batch for it (U01, U03, U04); a mail needs three taps and a dialog per thing, loses the intent and leaves no way back (U07–U09); „automatic“ invoices hide behind two settings (U10); the planner page opens with the whole waiting list (U12). The target keeps every surface and changes four things, all for every user: **F1** the many-lines sheet becomes calm (targets as text, no selects, no booking, the pile hidden behind it, one closing line); **F2** „In den Plan“ on a mail row, a local filter „Persönlich“, a folder batch with a preview, a link back to the mail; **F3** a recognised invoice mail shows payee, amount and date with one verb, a payment reminder raises the dunning level of the invoice that is already open instead of making a second one; **F4** the planner folds what waits and names today's next step. Measured: five things on the PC 53 → 42 s with no detour and none of the pile visible, two mails into the plan 19 → 4 s, the planner page from 29 rows to the board only; the phone's „in passing“ path keeps its 1 tap per thing (37 s for three things), a later mode that keeps the field open would bring it to 30 s.

## 2. New functions

Each: problem in the scenario → idea → how it applies to all users → form → focus checklist 1–9 → rule conflicts → smallest first step → effort. Variants minimal / medium / large with a recommendation for F1 and F2 (the two with a real design choice); F3 and F4 have one sensible size.

### F1 The many-lines sheet becomes calm (U01, U02, U03, U04, U05)

**Problem.** „Mein Kopf läuft über und es ist gefühlt zu schwer, alles aufs Blatt zu bekommen.“ As soon as the sheet holds a second line it shows a 12-entry select per line, turns a money line into a booking and asks for a second „Alle speichern“, and the „Jetzt wichtig“ cards stay visible around it.

**Idea (medium, recommended).** Nothing to discover: the **same sheet**, the moment it holds more than one line, takes a calm shape – the hint reads „Eine Zeile = ein Eintrag. Sortiert wird später.“; each line shows its target as **text** („ToDo“, „Rechnung · ohne Frist“, „Termin · Do 9 Uhr“), a tap on the text opens the choice (the select is gone from the first view); no line books money (a money line with an invoice word becomes an invoice, one without becomes a ToDo with the amount in its title and a tap-able chip „Als Ausgabe buchen“ that opens the single sheet with the line prefilled); a line with a read date shows the date, nothing asks a question while the person is unloading (deadline marking happens in the sorter or on the board, U14); the page behind the sheet is covered on the phone and hidden on the PC (the strip and figures behind the sheet are not rendered while it is open, not just dimmed – the measurement counts them); „Alle speichern (5)“ closes with one line: „5 Dinge gespeichert. Sortieren geht später. · Ansehen · Rückgängig“ („Ansehen“ opens the ToDo page's inbox sorter, or the planner when it is on). The single-line sheet is unchanged (Enter saves, the booking draft and „Buchen“ stay).

**For all users.** The same sheet, the same parser, every entry point (FAB, top-bar „Erstellen“, hotkey window, Android tile); nothing depends on the planner (with the planner off „Ansehen“ opens the inbox sorter).

**Form.** Core quick capture only: `quickCapture/ui/CaptureForm.tsx` (multi rows), `parser/index.ts` (invoice words, „bezahlt“/„gekauft“ as spend words), `targets/index.ts` (the batch result carries the count and the place for the toast), strings. No module, no contribution, no table, no new mode.

**Focus checklist.** 1 Stimulus: five selects and the confirmation box leave the first view; the page behind the sheet is not rendered – measured as „visible pile elements outside the sheet“ (path A), target 0; 2 Off switch: nothing appears by itself and nothing is an aid in the sense of the guideline – it is the existing sheet; a device-local switch for a later mode would live in Einstellungen → Schnellerfassung next to the other sheet options; 3 Next step: one closing line with one verb; 4 Wording: „Eine Zeile = ein Eintrag. Sortiert wird später.“ (the sheet's own words today), no counts of what is open, no „noch“; 5 Interruption: the draft survives a killed app (`draft.ts`, C04) – on the phone the person who pockets the phone comes back to the same lines; 6 Tests: parser words (unit, corpus + six sentences), multi rows as text (component test), path A of the use-case script (taps, detours, pile elements), `capture-multi.spec.ts` gains the money-line case; 7 Readability: targets as words, no colour for state, the field stays out of the reading aid like every input; 8 Clicks: 3 taps → 2 for five things; 9 Removed instead: the per-line select and the second „Alle speichern“ leave the many-lines path.

**Rule conflicts.** Finance „booked only after an explicit confirmation“: the many-lines path never books (Q1); the single sheet and `$` keep „Buchen“. Vault excluded as before (`targets.test.ts`). Capture adapters stay module-free. No sync or privacy change (the lines are ToDos as typed by hand). The share target is **not** part of F1: it already shows one line and keeps the whole shared text in the note (U17 says what that means for a shared mail).

**Smallest first step.** U03 + U04 + U05 (package 1): 2 taps and no detour.

### F2 „In den Plan“ from a mail (U07, U08, U09, U17)

**Problem.** „E-Mails, die ich beantworten oder lesen muss, die aber untergehen … will über die App diese Mails finden und in den Planner bekommen.“ Today a mail becomes a ToDo in three taps and a dialog, named after its subject, with a web link in its note that carries the person's own address; there is no way from the plan back into the mail, no view for personal mail, and no batch.

**Idea.** One action **„In den Plan“** on a mail row and in the thread view (with the planner off it reads „Als ToDo“). It writes through the quick-capture target `plan` (the module-free path `LinkDialog` uses today, which returns the created ids and one undo), with a title that carries name and topic – „Anna Beispiel · Wochenende?“ (Q3; a verb only when the heuristics are sure, e.g. „bezahlen“ for an invoice mail, which F3 handles instead) – `durationMin: 15`, a soft due when the text names a date, and `link: { kind: 'mail', id: '<account>/<thread>', label: subject }`. The ToDo's note keeps the mail's web URL **without** `?authuser=<address>` (U17), so the link opens on every device; on the desktop the plan row and the ToDo row offer „Zur Mail“ (in-app, the mail page learns `?open=<thread>`), elsewhere the link opens the web URL as today. The mail row shows a quiet „im Plan“ text from the module's own `link` row, written with the returned id. **Cross-device duplicate guard:** the ToDo id is deterministic, `mail-<hash(account, thread, 'todo')>` (additive optional `id` on the capture target's first stage; `upsert` semantics, like automation's `auto-<hash>`), so a second desktop that offers the action again overwrites instead of doubling; its own `link` row follows on the first tap there. Toast „Im Plan: Mi · Rückgängig“ (undo removes ToDo, planner item and link row). **Finding the mails (medium):** a local filter chip **„Persönlich“** in the mail list – no AI, no count, no badge – keeps threads whose sender is a person: not a Gmail bulk category and no `List-Unsubscribe`/`Precedence: bulk` header (the header fetch is new, so the signal works for any provider, not only Gmail's label). **Batch (medium):** „Alle in den Plan…“ on a folder or the filter → a preview list with tick boxes (personal senders ticked, bulk and already-linked threads unticked), one „In den Plan (4)“, one undo. **Large:** the automation template „Mail mit Label ‚Antworten‘ → in den Plan“ – this **reverses a decision** (`decisions/automation.md`: mail only reports, mail events carry no title or id so the rule log keeps nothing from a mail), needs a thread id and a sender name in the event, runs only while the app runs with mail notifications on, and would write names into ToDos without a look; if ever, only as `suggest` with a preview.

│ Anna Beispiel · Wochenende?             07:40  [In den Plan] │         │ Anna Beispiel · Wochenende?  15 Min│
│ Max Muster · Fotos vom Ausflug      17. Sept.  im Plan · Mi  │         │ Mail · ⋯ Erledigt · Später · Zur Mail│
│ Hausverwaltung · Termin Ablesung   28. Sept.  [In den Plan] │         └───────────────────────────────┘
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**For all users.** Any mail, any folder, any provider behind `MailProvider`; the filter needs no label and no filter chat.

**Form.** Mail module (row action in `rowActions.ts`, thread action next to „Aus dieser Mail anlegen“, filter chip, folder head action, the `?open=` route, the preview list on the importer's `ImportPreview` pattern), `core/mail/heuristics.ts` (personal/bulk from the headers; today only Gmail's `CATEGORY_PROMOTIONS` is known), `core/mail/prefill.ts` (title rule, URL without address), `LinkDialog.tsx` (`extra.link`), planner page (`link.kind === 'mail'` → „Zur Mail“ beside „Zur Notiz“), ToDo row (open a mail link in the app on the desktop). Contract: one additive optional field on the capture target's first stage (`id`), announced in CHATS.md as a hotspot row (`quickCapture/targets/types.ts`); the bus event `plan.requested` stays as it is (a bus emit returns nothing, so the mail module could not write its link row from it – the capture target returns the ids).

**Focus checklist.** 1 Stimulus: one more row action (on hover on the PC, in the ⋯ menu and as swipe on the phone, like „Archivieren“), „im Plan“ and „Persönlich“ as muted text, no counts; 2 Off switch: the filter chip and the batch are switchable in the mail settings („Persönlich anbieten“, „Alle in den Plan anbieten“, both on), the template off; the single row action is a plain row action like „Archivieren“; 3 Next step: the toast names the day; 4 Wording: „Anna Beispiel · Wochenende?“, never „unbeantwortet seit“, never a count of unanswered mails; 5 Interruption: ToDo and link row exist after one tap; 6 Tests: title rule (unit; „Re:“, bulk senders, two mails of one person stay two topics), deterministic id and duplicate guard (unit), `isolation.test.ts` stays green, path B of the use-case script (2 taps for two mails); 7 Readability: „Mail“ is a word in the row; 8 Clicks: 3 taps + dialog → 1 tap per mail; 9 Removed instead: the address in the stored URL (U17) and the ToDo's dependence on a web link for its source.

**Rule conflicts.** Modules never import each other: the mail module writes through the capture target layer, as `LinkDialog` does today. Privacy: the ToDo title (name and subject) and the link label are ordinary ToDo data – synced, backed up, readable by the local API, MCP and the AI import like any typed ToDo; the mail body never leaves the sealed cache; nothing of this reaches the assistant (it receives schemas, never entries), but a person who uses MCP or the import hands a model their own ToDo titles – Q3 therefore also offers the name-only title. Mail cache, `link` table and thread bodies stay `local: true`. A **batch** writes many names at once – therefore the preview with tick boxes, never a silent batch. Sync: nothing new. `accounts` untouched.

**Smallest first step.** `LinkDialog` passes `extra.link`, the URL loses the address (U17), the thread view gets „In den Plan“, the plan row says „Mail“ and offers „Zur Mail“: 2 taps per mail (open, button).

**Variants.**

**Effort.** minimal S · medium M (one package together with F3) · large +M (own decision, own package).

### F3 „Stadtwerke Musterstadt · 84,30 € · fällig 15.11. · Anlegen“ – invoices and payment reminders from the list (U10, U03 for mail)

**Problem.** „Mahnungen, Rechnungen, alles irgendwie am liebsten automatisch, aber trage jetzt eben alles einzeln ein.“ The heuristics already find payee, amount and date; the result feeds a widget count and a suggest-notice behind two settings. A reminder for an invoice that is already open would create a second one.

**Idea.** A mail the heuristics read as an invoice with a certain amount shows one muted second line with the values that will be written and one verb: „Stadtwerke Musterstadt · 84,30 € · fällig 15.11. · **Anlegen**“. One tap writes the invoice through the quick-capture `invoice` target (payee, amount, due, note with the address-free URL) and ends in „Rechnung angelegt · Rückgängig“; the existing `plannable` source puts it into the plan with a hard due („Stadtwerke Musterstadt bezahlen · 10 Min · Frist Do“ – the plan row says „Rechnung“, invoices have no `links`, so it does not say „Mail“). **Payment reminder:** when the words say Mahnung/Zahlungserinnerung/Zahlungsaufforderung the target layer first looks for an **open invoice with the same payee and amount** (a module-free lookup in `quickCapture/targets`, the layer that already knows the invoice fields); found → the line reads „Mahnung zu offener Rechnung Stadtwerke · Mahnung vermerken“ and one tap sets `dunningLevel + 1` and `dunnedAt` = mail date on that invoice (no second amount in invoices or in the plan); not found → a new invoice with `dunningLevel: 1`. When a value is missing (no date, two amounts) the tap opens today's preview dialog instead. The automation template stays for people who want the notice. Duplicate guard across devices as in F2 (deterministic id `mail-<hash(account, thread, 'invoice')>`).

**For all users.** Every invoice-like mail in any folder; the line is text, no colour, never a count.

**Form.** Mail module (list row, `heuristics.ts` adds a `dunning` flag from its invoice regex), `core/mail/prefill.ts`, quick-capture: additive optional `CaptureFields.dunning?: { level: number; date: string }` mapped by the invoice adapter to `dunningLevel`/`dunnedAt` (both fields exist in `modules/invoices/schema.ts`), the open-invoice lookup and the „raise instead of create“ path in the target layer. Setting in the mail module: „Rechnungen in der Liste erkennen“ (on).

**Focus checklist.** 1 one muted line per invoice mail (typically 1–3 rows in a list) – justified by the 3 taps + dialog it removes; 2 switch above; 3 one verb; 4 „fällig 15.11.“, after the date „Frist war 15.11.“ in warning tone, never red in the mail list; „Mahnung vermerken“, not „Mahnstufe 1“; 5 the invoice exists after the tap, undo in the toast; 6 unit tests for the dunning flag, the certainty rule and the open-invoice match (payee + amount, case and whitespace tolerant), e2e „Anlegen“ once → „Rechnung angelegt“, a second time → „angelegt“ stays, path B of the use-case script; 7 text, no icon-only; 8 3 taps + dialog → 1 tap; 9 Removed instead: the invoice count in the inbox widget („n Rechnungsmails in x Tagen“) becomes „n Rechnungen zum Anlegen“ pointing at the same list – the old element gets a verb, no new one.

**Rule conflicts.** Money is created only on a tap, with the values visible before the tap and undo after it (`decisions/automation.md`: mail offers `suggest`, never a silent `create` – unchanged). Everything local (heuristics). Mail cache stays sealed; the invoice stores payee, amount, date, reference – what a person types by hand. Invoices get no new field.

**Smallest first step.** The dunning flag in `heuristics.ts` and the additive `CaptureFields.dunning` + adapter mapping (S); the open-invoice match next (S); then the list line.

**Effort.** M (together with F2 one package „mail → plan“).

### F4 The planner shows less and names the next step (U12, U13)

**Problem.** „Mich überfordert die Anzahl, die ich noch umsetzen muss, und notiere nichts.“ The planner page opens with the board and every waiting thing, each with a reason line.

**Idea.** „Wartet auf freie Zeit“ is folded by default (`GroupedList` `defaultClosed`), the per-row reason moves behind „Warum hier?“ like on the board; the head detail line names the next step – a full day: „Heute: Stadtwerke bezahlen · dann ist der Tag voll“; a day without time: „Heute ist frei. Mi: 1 h 24 frei.“ (two texts for two cases, the same in ANALYSIS U13). What the folded head says is **Q4**: (a) „Wartet auf freie Zeit · 16“ – the count the wording glossary allows („Wartet noch · 5 Dinge“), the overload sentence („12 h 40 mehr zu tun, als Zeit frei ist“) only when the group is opened and in the once-a-week toast as today; (b) count and overload sentence in the head as decided in CONCEPT §7. For this scenario the number is the problem, so (a) is recommended; the figure stays one tap away and the toast rule is unchanged, so honesty is kept. Fold state stays device-local and remembered (exists).

**For all users / form.** Planner page only (`routes/PlannerPage.tsx:577`, `:690-703`), three strings. No contract change; `planner.spec.ts` opens the group before it reads waiting rows.

**Focus checklist.** 1 fewer rows and words on the first screen (path D: rows, words, scroll); 2 the fold is the person's choice and remembered; 3 the detail line is the next step; 4 „Der Rest wartet.“, never „noch 16“; 5 –; 6 path D of the use-case script; 7 the figure keeps its words; 8 one tap to see what waits; 9 Removed: the reason line per waiting row.

**Rule conflicts.** Q4 (a) moves the overload figure out of the folded head – a wording change to a decided line (CONCEPT §7, `decisions/plan.md` „Calm by construction“), therefore a question, not a package default.

**Effort.** S.

### Not proposed (and why)

- A new module „Eingang“ or a brain-dump module: the ToDo inbox, the sorter and the capture are that place; a module would import from two others.
- Cloud AI reading the mailbox for „needs answer“: mail never reaches a model (`core/mail/isolation.test.ts`); the local model may help on request only (package 5 of mail) and is desktop-only. The filter „Persönlich“ is header logic, no model.
- Counters („12 unbeantwortet“), badges on the mail folder, a „Jetzt wichtig“ card for mails: stimulus; the scenario's problem is the number.
- Making the planner or mail module on by default: a maintainer decision (U16), out of scope.
- Mail on Android/PWA: a platform and Google-review decision (`docs/STATUS.md`, Google OAuth „Testing“ note), out of scope; the share target already takes a shared mail as one line.

## 6. Fragen an Sven (Empfehlung zuerst)

1. **Mehrzeiliger Zettel und Geld:** (a) **Mehrere Zeilen buchen nie** – eine Geldzeile mit Rechnungswort wird Rechnung, sonst ToDo mit Betrag und Chip „Als Ausgabe buchen“ (öffnet den Einzel-Zettel vorbelegt); Buchungen weiter nur einzeln oder mit `$` _(Empfehlung: kein zweiter Schritt im Abladen, die Finanzregel bleibt unberührt)_ · (b) Buchungen bleiben im Stapel, der eine Knopf nennt sie („Speichern · davon 1 Buchung 87,40 €“) · (c) keine neue Form, nur die drei kleinen Fixes.
2. **Mail → Plan, Umfang:** (a) **mittel** – Zeilenaktion „In den Plan“, Filter „Persönlich“ (lokal, ohne Zahl), „im Plan“-Marke, „Zur Mail“, „Alle in den Plan…“ mit Vorschau, F3 „Anlegen“ mit Mahnungs-Abgleich _(Empfehlung: ein Paket, braucht keinen eigenen Filter im Postfach)_ · (b) minimal – nur Knopf in der geöffneten Mail · (c) groß – zusätzlich Automation „Label → Plan“ als Vorschlag; das kehrt die Entscheidung „Mail meldet nur, Ereignisse ohne Titel“ um.
3. **Titel aus der Mail:** (a) **„<Absendername> · <Betreff>“** (Name und Thema; Verb nur, wenn sicher) _(Empfehlung: zwei Mails von Anna bleiben zwei Themen)_ · (b) „<Absendername> antworten“, Betreff nur lokal (nichts vom Inhalt in Sync, Backup, Daten-API) · (c) nur der Betreff wie heute.
4. **Kopf der eingeklappten Wartegruppe im Planer:** (a) **nur „Wartet auf freie Zeit · 16“**, der Überlast-Satz erst beim Aufklappen und im Wochen-Hinweis wie bisher _(Empfehlung für dieses Szenario: die Zahl ist das Problem, die Ehrlichkeit bleibt einen Tipp entfernt)_ · (b) Zahl und Überlast-Satz im Kopf wie in CONCEPT §7 entschieden.
