# Use case „Kopf leer“ – analysis (phase 1: play-through, measurement, friction)

Branch `design/usecase-kopf-leer`, 2026-11-08. Scenario by the maintainer (verbatim in the prompt, condensed here): _overwhelmed, mails pile up, invoices run over; wants everything „aufs Blatt“ and out of the head – mails to answer or read that sink, a filter already categorises the inbox but it is still too much, wants to find those mails in the app and get them into the planner; reminders and invoices „am liebsten automatisch“, today everything is typed in one by one; some of it on the phone while walking through the flat (rubbish, tidying); then the sheer number of things to answer stops him from writing anything down._ Target picture: [TARGET.md](TARGET.md). Council rounds 1–3 (2026-11-08) corrected U04, U06, U07, aligned the fix ideas with TARGET and added U17. Measurement script: [`web/e2e/usecases/kopf-leer.spec.ts`](../../../../web/e2e/usecases/kopf-leer.spec.ts). Rules obeyed: root `CLAUDE.md`, [FOCUS-GUIDELINES](../../FOCUS-GUIDELINES.md), [MODULE-IDENTITY](../../MODULE-IDENTITY.md), planner concept [plan/CONCEPT.md](../../plan/CONCEPT.md) (§4 `plannable`, §10 decisions). Nothing here is code.

## 1. Steps of the scenario („Ablauf“)

| #   | Step                                                          | Intent                                                                    | Expected way (a person who does not know the app)                                                 | Abort criterion                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| S1  | Open the app, find the place to unload                        | „alles aufs Blatt“, without first seeing the pile                         | One obvious entry point for free text, many lines, no decisions                                   | The person sees the pile first, or has to pick a type per line                    |
| S2  | Unload five things at once (PC)                               | Rubbish, tidying, a reply to the landlord, a deadline, a payment reminder | Type, Enter, done; the app sorts: ToDo, ToDo, ToDo, ToDo with deadline, invoice                   | Something lands in the wrong place, a line is lost, or the person has to confirm  |
| S3  | Find the mails that need an answer, get them into the planner | the filter labels already exist, the list is still too long               | A view „zu beantworten“ (or the filter's label), one tap per mail → it is in the plan             | Mail is not available on the device; three or more steps per mail; intent is lost |
| S4  | Invoice / payment reminder from a mail                        | „am liebsten automatisch“                                                 | The app proposes the invoice from the mail; one tap confirms; it is in the plan with its deadline | Manual retyping; the reminder is not recognised as money with a deadline          |
| S5  | Phone, walking through the flat                               | „Müll runter“, „zusammenräumen“ – two or three things in passing          | One tap, type, Enter – and the field is ready for the next thing                                  | The sheet closes after each thing, or opening it takes more than one tap          |
| S6  | Look at the result                                            | relief: it is on paper now, the plan says what is first                   | One figure („heute 3 Dinge“), the rest folded                                                     | The planner shows the whole backlog with a reason per row                         |
| S7  | The number overwhelms, nothing more gets written              | the feeling, not a click path                                             | The app never shows the count of open things while the person is unloading                        | Red figures, „überfällig“, counters in the way of the field                       |

## 4. What already works (keep)

- Multi-line capture („Eine Zeile = ein Eintrag“), one undo for the batch, drafts survive a killed app (C04).
- The planner's honesty: nothing is „überfällig“, the overload is a number, every move has a reason behind „Warum hier?“ on the board.
- „Aus dieser Mail anlegen“ with a preview and the backlink under the thread; the mail cache is local, sealed, never synced; the local model only on request.
- Phone: one tap to the field, Enter saves, Android launcher shortcut and quick-settings tile „Erfassen“, share target.
- The inbox sorter („Eingang sortieren“, one thing at a time) and „Später“ everywhere.
