# Use case „Wochenende retten“ – target picture (phase 2)

Branch `design/usecase-wochenende`, 2026-11-14. Analysis and friction ids: [ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md) (W01–W22). Rules: root `CLAUDE.md`, [FOCUS-GUIDELINES](../../FOCUS-GUIDELINES.md), [FOCUS-WORDING](../../FOCUS-WORDING.md), [MODULE-IDENTITY](../../MODULE-IDENTITY.md), [DESIGN-SPEC §4a](../../DESIGN-SPEC.md), planner [CONCEPT](../../plan/CONCEPT.md) (§3 engine, §4 `plannable`, §6c instructions, §10 decisions), [DATA-FLOWS](../../../legal/DATA-FLOWS.md). ## 0. In one paragraph

The pieces exist – batch capture, the planner with its field, mail actions, time tracking, reminders – but a first-time person on a Saturday morning meets them in the wrong order: the setup never offers the planner, Sunday has no time, the break is planned like a chore, the deadline loses its day or its time, and in the evening „Neu verteilen“ keeps everything on today. The target adds **one entry „Tage einteilen“** that runs the existing pieces in the person's order (dump → time per day → preview → take), and five small, general extensions: **day parts and a break block** in the planner, **„Rest verteilen“** for the evening, **a deadline time** with reminders that fire once, a **„Beantwortet?“** hint on the mail row, and a **„Tagesende“** the plan does not fill past. Nothing reads, counts or rates leisure, sleep or health; the app keeps only what the person types.

## 2. New functions

Each: problem → idea → for all users → form → focus checklist 1–9 → rule conflicts → smallest first step → effort; three sizes with a recommendation.

### F1 „Tage einteilen“ – the first morning without knowing modules (W01, W02, W03, W08, W09, W10, W13)

- **Problem:** the setup ends on the overview without the planner; time per day is 16 taps away; Sunday is „frei“; the empty head contradicts itself.
- **Idea:** one sheet with three steps, built only from existing parts: (1) **„Was steht an?“** – the batch field of the quick capture (one line = one thing); (2) **„Wie viel Zeit hast du?“** – today and the next two days as rows with chips „keine Zeit · 2 h · 4 h · 6 h · ganzer Tag“ („frei“ stays the word for free time, never for a day without time) (figures through `core/i18n/format`; writes planner day rows of those three days, never the weekday defaults); (3) **Vorschau** – the planner preview („Neu im Plan“, per day) → „Übernehmen“ / „Zurück“. One undo for everything: „Eingeplant: Sa, So, Mo · Rückgängig“.
- **Reached from:** the welcome card as a **secondary** button „Tage einteilen“ beside the primary „Einrichtung starten“ (one primary per view); after either path the card is gone like after „Später“ – the setup stays reachable in the settings and the overview checklist. Also from the planner's empty state (its one action) and the planner ⋯. It switches on `planner` (+ `todos` via `requires`) only on tap and never switches anything off (W02).
- **For all users:** a weekday person gets the same sheet (three days from today); nothing is weekend-specific; the setup questions stay as they are.
- **Form:** planner module UI (`modules/planner/components/DaySetup.tsx`), uses `saveCaptures` (quick-capture targets) and `setAvailable` + the preview of `actions.ts`; the welcome card links to `/planner?start=1` (no new contribution, no contract change).
- **Focus 1–9:** (1) one sheet, three steps, one primary button per step; replaces the 16 stepper taps; (2) the entry is a button, never opens by itself; (3) each step names one next action; (4) „Das reicht für heute.“ (the planner head) stays the voice, no counters; (5) a closed sheet keeps the dump as a draft (capture draft exists); (6) unit: chips → minutes; e2e: paths A and C of the script; (7) chips with text, no colour meaning; (8) 3 taps for three days; (9) **removes**: the stepper as the first way (stays for fine steps), the contradicting empty head (W13).
- **Rule conflicts:** none; the welcome card is core setup (docs/rules/core-privacy.md: no data in the setup progress, never a nag) – the change is one secondary link.
- **Smallest first step:** chips in the day sheet (W08/W09), the head sentence (W13).
- **Effort:** minimal S (chips + head + link) · **mittel M (the three-step sheet) – recommended** · groß L (a daily „Morgen“ routine with the sheet every morning – no: a nag risk, FOCUS 2).

### F2 Day parts, the break block and estimates (W05, W11, W12)

- **Problem:** „erst Pflicht, nach der Pause Haushalt“ cannot be said; „Zocken“ is planned like a chore; every captured thing counts 30 min and is a main item, so a day holds three.
- **Idea:** an optional **Tagesabschnitt** per plan item – „Vormittag · Nachmittag · Abend“ (row ⋯; field: „Duschen nachmittags“, „Bude abends“). Inside a day the order becomes: tier (pinned, deadline, rest – as today) → part (without part first, then Vormittag, Nachmittag, Abend) → today's keys. Days are still chosen by today's rules; a part never moves a unit to another day. With a window, the window is split into three equal thirds and a unit is timed in its third; one that does not fit there takes the next free slot later in the window, else it has no time (as today). A **break** is a blocker with an optional part or time; the row ⋯ of a plan item offers „Als Pause“ with a length (1 h · 2 h · 3 h; stored as `asBreakOn` on the planner row: the engine never plans it as a unit and turns it into a blocker of that length on that day), the quick capture reads lines starting with „Pause“ or „Freizeit“ as a blocker while the planner is on (day = the typed date ?? today, length = the typed range or duration ?? 1 h, a time only when typed; written through the existing planner capture target to the day's blockers, one undo). A bare „Zocken“ stays a ToDo – the app does not guess what is leisure. A break shows its own length like any blocker; there is **no total, no rating, no leisure sum across days, no widget**. **Estimates:** the existing choices (5, 15, 30, 60 Min; `TaskEditor.tsx:50`) are offered on the plan row ⋯ („dauert 15 Min“) and as the field sentence „Duschen 15 Min“ (the `duration` op exists) – below 30 min a thing is already not a main item (`engine.ts:78`).
- **For all users:** parts and breaks are optional; without them nothing changes; the weekday person uses „Vormittag“ for deep work.
- **Form:** planner only: `part?` and `asBreakOn?` on the planner's own item row (`manifest.version` + migration) and `part?` on `core/plan` `PlanItem`/`PlanBlocker`; engine comparator as above; `windows.ts` thirds. **No change to `contributions.plannable`** (no source would deliver a part).
- **Focus 1–9:** (1) parts and „Als Pause“ behind ⋯, the board unchanged; (2) nothing set = today's behaviour; (3) unchanged; (4) a break never with a sum or a judgement; (5) stored; (6) engine fixtures: determinism, fixpoint, „a part never changes the day“, thirds overflow; (7) part as text, no colour; (8) one tap via ⋯; (9) removes the need for a window just to order a day.
- **Rule conflicts:** none (planner-internal data, field-wise sync unaffected; no `aiSchema`).
- **Smallest first step:** estimates on the row ⋯ and „Pause …“ lines.
- **Effort:** minimal S (estimates + „Pause“ lines) · **mittel M (parts in the engine, „Als Pause“) – recommended** · groß L (time-of-day scheduling with drag – no: the planner stays a day planner, CONCEPT §0).

### F3 „Rest verteilen“ in the evening (W18, W19)

- **Problem:** at 21:00 „Neu verteilen“ keeps today's things on today; the person moves each one with two taps.
- **Idea:** a run **„Rest verteilen“** built like the existing „alles von heute auf morgen“ (`core/plan/instructions.ts` `moveAll`, Z. 207–216): today's available time goes to 0 and every open unit of today gets `earliest = tomorrow`; a pinned unit and a hard due of today stay. Shown as the existing preview („Verschoben · Sa → Mo …“) → „Übernehmen“; undo toast „Auf die nächsten Tage verteilt. · Rückgängig“ (FOCUS-WORDING „Replan“). Reached from: a quiet text link in the planner head from 17:00 (pattern of „Woche prüfen“), one text action in the „Wochenlast“ widget from 17:00, and „Tag abschließen“ on the ToDo widget when the planner is on (it then runs this instead of „Auf morgen schieben“). An optional notification **„Rest verteilen?“** at 20:00, **off by default**, `soft: true`, under „Ruhige Erinnerungen“, once a day, never repeated, before the quiet hours.
- **For all users:** a weekday person gets the same; without the planner „Tag abschließen“ stays as it is.
- **Form:** planner action `restToTomorrow` (the ops of `moveAll`); the field also reads „Rest verteilen“/„Rest auf morgen“; the ToDo widget asks over the event bus (`plan.restRequested`), no module import.
- **Focus 1–9:** (1) one text link after 17:00 only; (2) evening link under `focus.eveningWrapUp`, notification default off; (3) one action; (4) no count of what was left anywhere; (5) one undo; (6) unit: op list, fixpoint; e2e: path F; (7) –; (8) 1–2 taps instead of 2 per thing; (9) removes the planner-less „Auf morgen schieben“ path while the planner is on.
- **Rule conflicts:** none; deterministic (ops are a pure function of today's units).
- **Effort:** minimal S (op + field + head link) · **mittel M (+ widget + „Tag abschließen“ + optional notification) – recommended** · groß L (automatic at a set time – no: nothing moves without a tap, CONCEPT §1).

### F4 Deadlines with a time, Sunday evening, Monday morning (W04, W06, W20, W21)

- **Problem:** „bis So 18:00“ loses the day or the time; „Mo 7:30 krankmelden“ is an appointment that takes plan time, and as a reminder it would repeat under today's rule.
- **Idea:** (a) **fix W20** (bug): a due phrase wins over the parser's date. (b) An optional **time on a hard due**: `due.time?: 'HH:mm'` in the contract, the planner's own item row and `dueTime?` on the ToDo; the engine still plans by day (unchanged), the row shows „Frist So 18:00“, and a deadline with a time offers **one** reminder („1 Std vorher“, default off, per deadline) – sent by the ToDo module itself from `dueDate` + `dueTime` (no stored calendar row), so it is gone when the ToDo is done, deleted or moved. (c) Parser: „bis/spätestens + day (+ time)“ makes a ToDo with a deadline (not an event). A dated, timed line becomes a **reminder** only with an explicit trigger – „erinnere mich“ (exists), „falls nötig“, „ggf.“ – never by guessing a verb; „Fr 20 Uhr Kino mit Anna“ stays an appointment. (d) Every reminder made by the quick capture or the planner field carries **„einmal“** (calendar field `once`, shown on the reminder row): exactly one notification at its time – no lead, no staggered stage, no repeat; reminders set in the calendar dialog keep the global repeat rule (decision Q4 a). The title is what the person typed minus date, time and „erinnere mich“ (as today); „falls nötig“/„ggf.“ stay in the title; the app adds no word about health. These reminders use only the existing notification and push flows (DATA-FLOWS `push`: title and note like every calendar reminder, only with push on), no new flow.
- **For all users:** every deadline can carry a time; nothing changes for deadlines without one.
- **Form:** contract extension, additive (`PlannableItem.due.time?`), planner item schema and todos schema (`manifest.version` + migrations), calendar event field `once?` (+ migration, `modules/calendar/notifications.ts` sends only the main notification for it), ToDo field `dueRemind?` (todos' own notifications), parser rules.
- **Focus 1–9:** (1) a time only where typed; (2) „1 Std vorher“ is opt-in per deadline; (3) –; (4) „Frist So 18:00“, after it „Frist war So 18:00“ (glossary pattern), never red; (5) –; (6) parser corpus incl. counter-lines, migration tests, `limits.test.ts`/`policy.test.ts` unchanged; (7) –; (8) 0 extra taps when typed; (9) removes the note line „Uhrzeit: 18:00“ for deadlines.
- **Rule conflicts:** formats: times `HH:mm` local (holds); data shape change → migrations; health: no field, category or inference from words like „krank“ or „Arzt“ – a word only picks event vs reminder.
- **Smallest first step:** W20 bug + „bis So 18:00“ as a ToDo deadline (time kept as a note line, as today).
- **Effort:** minimal S (bug + deadline parser) · **mittel M (+ `due.time`, `once`, trigger words) – recommended** · groß L (deadline-aware hourly scheduling – no).

### F5 Reply things and the „Beantwortet?“ hint (W17)

- **Problem:** „WhatsApp antworten“, „Mails beantworten“, „Team lesen“ are three vague things; a reply made from the mail row never learns it was answered.
- **Idea:** (a) **„Antworten an …“** as one ToDo with a checklist (exists): the capture line „Antworten: Anna, Vermieter, Team“ makes one ToDo „Antworten“ with three checklist items (only with the word and a colon). (b) **Hint on the mail row, never automatic:** when the person opens or refreshes the mail list (computed in the list path of `useMailbox` only; the background new-mail check `notifyService` never writes the flag), a thread that was put „In den Plan“ and whose **last message** carries the label `SENT` shows „Beantwortet? · Erledigt“ on its mail row; „Erledigt“ asks the ToDo to close over the bus (`todo.doneRequested` with the ToDo id only) and shows „Erledigt · Rückgängig“. The flag lives only in mail's device-local cache (`local: true`, `dataApi: false`), is derived from the last message's labels in `summarize` (today's thread labels are the union of all messages, `gmail.ts:188`), never from the background new-mail check (`notifyService`), never synced, backed up as a field of the ToDo, or sent to an AI. WhatsApp stays a plain thing (no connector, no link).
- **For all users:** the checklist line works for anyone; the hint only with mail on; with the planner off it is the same hint on the mail row.
- **Form:** quick-capture parser rule (a); mail module (connector `summarize` keeps `lastLabelIds`, row hint, setting „Beantwortet anzeigen“ in the mail settings – a mail-only display switch, default on); one bus event; DATA-FLOWS `google` and data-flows-hinweise „Mail → ToDo“ name the device-only flag.
- **Focus 1–9:** (1) one hint on one mail row; (2) mail setting, default on (display only, no notification); (3) –; (4) „Beantwortet?“ is a question, no pressure; (5) –; (6) unit: last-message rule; e2e: seed thread with a sent last message; (7) –; (8) 1 tap with undo; (9) removes the manual search for which replies are done.
- **Rule conflicts:** privacy promise „no new field on the synced side“ (data-flows-hinweise) – kept: no field on the ToDo or plan row.
- **Effort:** **minimal S (a) and mittel M (b) – both recommended, one package** · groß L (reply drafts from the plan – no, out of scope).

### F6 „Tagesende“ – a sleep window the plan respects (W22)

- **Problem:** nothing tells the plan „nichts nach 23 Uhr“; the window is no limit.
- **Idea:** two optional settings per weekday group (Mo–Fr, Sa, So): **„Tagesbeginn“** and **„Tagesende“**. With a Tagesende a day's capacity is capped: `raw = min(available, minutes from start to Tagesende) − blockers` with start = Tagesbeginn ?? window start, and for today the later of that and the current time (an explicit engine input, never read from a clock inside); without Tagesbeginn or window the cap binds only today; „0:00“ means end of day (1440), in `core/plan/capacity.ts`, in the one pass the engine already makes; `windows.ts` clamps the window end to the Tagesende, so no session is timed after it – so what does not fit moves on like any overflow (reason „passt nicht vor dem Tagesende“), and the fixpoint holds. Fragmentation by breaks may still leave a unit without a time (as today). In F1 step 2 a quiet text link „Tagesende festlegen“ (folded, „keins“ preselected) sets it for the three shown days only (`dayEnd?` on the planner day row and `PlanDay`, undone with the rest); the lasting per-group setting lives on the planner settings page. **No notification:** from 30 min before the Tagesende the planner head says „Bald ist Tagesende.“ (switch: `focus.eveningWrapUp`; in-app only – an OS notification would fall into the quiet hours 22–07 and be moved to the morning). Optional and separate: a habit „Ins Bett bis 23:30“ (habits exist; streaks off by default; only named in the user docs).
- **For all users:** a shift worker sets nothing or a late end; nothing is about sleep in the data – it is a plan limit.
- **Form:** planner settings (two fields per group), `dayEnd?` on the day row + migration, `capacity.ts` cap, `windows.ts` clamp, reason sentence in `explain.ts`, head line.
- **Focus 1–9 (strict):** (1) one head line, only near the end; (2) settings empty by default, head line under `focus.eveningWrapUp`; (3) –; (4) no „zu spät“, no deficit, no counter, no score, no history of bedtimes; (5) –; (6) engine fixtures „unit after Tagesende moves“ + fixpoint; (7) –; (8) set once; (9) removes the need to type windows on the settings page.
- **Rule conflicts:** none; no sleep or health data (only two times the person chose for the plan).
- **Effort:** minimal S (Tagesende cap) · **mittel M (+ Tagesbeginn, F1 row, head line) – recommended** · groß L (sleep tracking – never).

### Not proposed (and why)

- WhatsApp access or reading messages – no connector, and none is wanted (A3).
- Counting or limiting gaming time, a „weniger zocken“ goal, a leisure total – against FOCUS 4 and A5; the break block is enough.
- Any health field, symptom, „krank“ category or inference from titles – A4; the app keeps the typed title only.
- A shared Google OAuth client (W15) – an own decision (cost, Google verification, privacy text); named, not planned here.
- Auto-enabling time tracking from a „Arbeitszeit“ line (W14) – modules are switched only by the person.

## 5. Fragen an Sven (Empfehlung zuerst) – answered 2026-11-14: Q1 a, Q2 a, Q3 a, Q4 a; decisions in [`docs/decisions/features-wochenende.md`](../../../decisions/features-wochenende.md), packages in [PACKAGES.md](PACKAGES.md)

1. **Einstieg „Tage einteilen“:** (a) **Blatt in drei Schritten, erreichbar über die Willkommenskarte und den leeren Planer** · (b) nur ein Profil „Tage einteilen“ in der Einrichtung · (c) nur Chips im Tagesblatt und ein besserer Kopfsatz.
2. **Reihenfolge am Tag:** (a) **Tagesabschnitte Vormittag/Nachmittag/Abend, optional je Ding, Pause als Blocker** · (b) nur Reihenfolge-Hinweise („zuerst“, „danach“) · (c) Uhrzeiten über Zeitfenster.
3. **Schlafrhythmus:** (a) **„Tagesende“ im Planer, Gewohnheit „Ins Bett bis …“ nur als Hinweis auf Bestehendes** · (b) nur als Gewohnheit · (c) gar nicht.
4. **Wie laut Sonntag/Montag:** (a) **nur, was du selbst einträgst, einmal zur Uhrzeit, ohne Wiederholen; Fristen-Erinnerung „1 Std vorher“ nur auf Wunsch** · (b) wie heute: eine selbst gesetzte Erinnerung wiederholt bis „Erledigt“ (30 Min, max. 6) · (c) nur die Karte in der App, keine Benachrichtigung.
