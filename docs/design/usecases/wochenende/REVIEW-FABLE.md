## The four questions, short

1. **Does it fit the Saturday morning?** First use, brain dump, Sunday, evening and Monday morning: yes, and the first three are built. Order: carried by typing order plus a break; parts are too much effort per thing (R2). Sleep: the weakest part – the person gets one sentence, the engine work behind it rarely acts (R4). Missing: phone, work time, and mail for a real first-time person (R7, R10).
2. **Smallest functions?** F1 and F3 yes. F2, F4, F6 no: typing order, the window, the day minutes, the row choice in the preview and „Rest verteilen“ already carry large parts (R2, R4, R5, R6).
3. **Focus rules:** hold. No leisure total or widget, no sleep or health data, no notification for the Tagesende, the rest reminder is off by default, wording is calm. One gap: the open „Zocken“ ToDo (R3).
4. **Package plan:** order and hotspots are sound and serial where they must be. Cuts: 4 and 5 are too big, 6 too heavy, 7 too early (R2, R4, R5, R7). Targets: mostly realistic, three rows need correcting and the ratchet comes too late (R9).
