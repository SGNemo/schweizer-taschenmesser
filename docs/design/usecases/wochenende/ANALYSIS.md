# Use case „Wochenende retten“ – analysis (phase 1: play-through, measurement, friction)

Branch `design/usecase-wochenende`, 2026-11-14, `develop` @ `35499492`. Scenario (verbatim in the maintainer's prompt, condensed, told about „the person“): _Saturday morning, first use of the app; lay out Saturday, Sunday and the next days; log work time, answer WhatsApp, check and answer mails (find the important ones, search one, take invoices from them), after a gaming break tidy the flat, shower, gather the bottle deposit; what is left gets spread over other days „per Klick“; less gaming, more doing; by Sunday everything answered and the team stuff read, so there is a calm moment on Sunday evening (a possible sick note on Monday morning); fix the sleep rhythm._ Target picture: [TARGET.md](TARGET.md), packages: [PACKAGES.md](PACKAGES.md). Measurement script: [`web/e2e/usecases/wochenende.spec.ts`](../../../../web/e2e/usecases/wochenende.spec.ts). Known findings are only referenced: [kopf-leer](../kopf-leer/ANALYSIS.md) (K…), [round 5](../../../qa/round-5.md), [round 6](../../../qa/round-6/FINDINGS.md). Nothing here is code.

## 4. What already works (keep)

- The batch of twelve lines saves in one tap, never books, and closes calmly („12 Dinge gespeichert. Sortiert wird später.“) – kopf-leer F1 carries here.
- „So 19 Uhr erinnere mich …“ is a reminder at 19:00 on Sunday; reminders never block plan time.
- Mail: „Persönlich“, the invoice line „Anlegen“ and „In den Plan“ are one tap each (kopf-leer 3a/3b) – once Gmail is connected.
- The planner field already understands „Sa 6 Std“, „So frei“, „Sa 14 bis 16 Uhr Zocken“ (a blocker with times), „schieb X auf Mo“, „alles von Sa nach Mo“ – it only is not offered (W09, W12).
- Nothing in the app counts, rates or locks leisure; the focus settings default to calm (no streaks, no morning digest).
