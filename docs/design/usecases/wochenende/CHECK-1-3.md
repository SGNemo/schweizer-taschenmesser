Checked: #870, #880, #888 as merged on `develop` @ `4065bd22`, against [TARGET](TARGET.md), the package prompts (`git show e5f14e183:docs/design/usecases/wochenende/PACKAGES.md`), the decisions and [FOCUS-GUIDELINES](../../FOCUS-GUIDELINES.md). Paths A, B, C, F, G were walked by hand-written Playwright steps with screenshots, not with the measuring script. „Observed“ below means seen in that app; „read“ means code or test only.

## Verdict

Packages 1–3 do what their lists ask, with one real bug: the first tap of the whole use case could end in the library. That and two small gaps are fixed here; five points are for the running packages or for Sven.

**Focus rules.** One primary button per view: observed on the card (1 of 3), each sheet step, both previews; the empty planner has the field's „Eintragen“ as primary and „Tage einteilen“ as secondary. Nothing opens by itself: observed at first start and at 21:00. No count of what is left: the rest preview holds no digit, the ToDo card says „Was heute offen ist, lässt sich auf die nächsten Tage verteilen.“ Calm wording: nothing against FOCUS-WORDING found. Undo: observed for the sheet, a quick pick and „Rest verteilen“. Touch: chips are 32 px high with a 44 px hit area (`ui/Patterns.module.css:399-402`).

**Counter-scenarios.** Weekday with office hours (observed, 1920 px): the events block, see C6. Planner off (observed, 1920 px): „Später“ writes no module row, no planner link, `/planner` leads to the library, a „Pause …“ line stays a ToDo, no „Rest verteilen“ on the overview. No mail module: every walk ran without it.
