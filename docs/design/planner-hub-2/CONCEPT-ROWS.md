# Planner hub 2 – concept: what a row can do (ideas 4, 2, 5, 8)

Frame and link contract: [CONCEPT](CONCEPT.md). Looks and measures: [DESIGN](DESIGN.md), `handoff/`. Words: [COPY](COPY.md).

## Idea 4 – expand instead of jumping

**Behaviour.** (1) A tap on a row's title (Enter on the row) opens a compartment under the row; the title no longer navigates. (2) The compartment shows what the thing's own module mounts there – its **plan panel** – and ends with „Ganz öffnen“ (the old jump). (3) What he does in the panel is the module's own action with the module's own undo toast. (4) When the thing is done at its source, the row leaves with the board's next reading, as today. (5) One compartment is open at a time; opening another closes the first; Esc closes and returns focus to the title.

**Contract.** `contributions.planPanel?: () => Promise<{ default: ComponentType<PlanPanelProps> }>` with `PlanPanelProps = { id: string; compact: boolean; onDone?: () => void }`. Mounted only by `core/modules/PlanPanelHost` (lazy, error boundary, Skeleton while loading). Rules on the type: reads and writes only the module's own data; the id is untrusted (unknown → the panel's own „Gibt es nicht mehr“); no network unless the person taps something that says so (mail „Senden“); never rendered for `accounts`. A module without a panel gets the core default: title, the source's `detail`, links, „Ganz öffnen“. A carrier ToDo (its first `origin` link is a mail thread) shows its target's panel first and its own checklist under it – the one case where the host mounts two.

| Panel | Shows | Lets him do | Package |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ToDos | note (first 3 lines), checklist | tick checklist lines; „Erledigt“ is the row's tick | 4a |
| Listen | open items (≤ 8, then „n weitere“), sections as small headers | tick items; „Fertig eingekauft“ when none is open | 4a |
| Rechnungen | payee, amount (`SensitiveValue`), deadline, reminder line | the existing `PayStep` inline: day, sum, „Bezahlt“ | 4b |
| Abos | name, price, deadline | new small step „Gekündigt am …“ / „Läuft weiter“ (the module's first cancel step) | 4b |
| Budgets (pile member, no row of its own) | category, spent / frame as words | nothing; „Zum Budget“ | 2 |
| E-Mail (desktop) | sender, subject, the newest message's first lines, rendered by mail from the device cache | „Antworten“ opens mail's own composer in the compartment (review, „Senden“, the 10 s window stay); „Beantwortet? · Erledigt“ | 4c |
| Kalender (a blocker row) | time, place | idea 9's two proposals | 9 |
| Notizen, Merkliste, Personen, Unterlagen, Routinen | default panel (Routinen: its steps, 5a) | Merkliste: „Im Browser öffnen“, „Erledigt“ | 4a |

**States.** closed · loading (Skeleton, 2 rows) · open · open and acting (the panel's button is busy) · gone („Gibt es nicht mehr“ + „Aus dem Plan nehmen“) · unavailable (module off or not on this device: „Auf diesem Gerät nicht verfügbar“, mail on the phone: plus „Am PC beantworten“ as plain text) · error (the module's write failed: its message stays in the panel, nothing closed).

**Data.** None. The open row is page state (not stored; a reload closes it).

**Risk.** Mail content in the planner's DOM: it is rendered by mail's component, never passed through planner code, never in the planner's strings, search or copy path (`copyInput` test grows). A panel that throws must not take the sheet with it (boundary + test). Two ways to finish (row tick and panel) for ToDos: the panel has no „Erledigt“ of its own.

**Target (script).** Flow B: module switches 10 → 0 on the desktop, `doneInPlanner` 0 → 10 of 10 (phone: 6 of 10, the four mails stay a desktop thing); per-thing handles for paying: 3 → 2.

**Not part of it.** No second detail page, no editing of titles, dates or amounts in the panel (that is „Ganz öffnen“), no reply draft generated by anything, no panel in the week band, the 4-week grid or the widget.

## Idea 2 – collective rows (piles)

**Behaviour.** (1) Things from one area that wait without a day can stand as **one row**: „Finanzen checken · 3 Rechnungen · 2 Fristen · 1 Budget“, „E-Mails durchgehen · 4 warten“. (2) It is offered, not done: the first time an area has ≥ 3 single rows in „Noch ohne Tag“, a quiet line there says „6 Dinge aus Finanzen · Als eine Zeile zeigen“. His tap sets `planner.piles.money`; the same switch is in the settings; the line is not shown again after ✕. (3) The pile row behaves like any row: a day, „fest“, moving. It has no tick; its lead is the area's glyph. (4) Expanding it (idea 4) lists its parts: each member as a compact row with its own action or tick, each part with „In Rechnungen ansehen“ (a jump into the filtered view). (5) A member he gave a day or a lock himself stays a row of its own and is not counted. (6) When nothing waits, an unplaced pile is simply not there; a placed one reads „Finanzen checken · nichts wartet“ and is ticked off like a done thing (kept until the day passes, never „Noch offen“).

**Contract.** `contributions.pile?: () => Promise<{ default: PileSource }>`; `PileSource = (ctx: { today: string }) => Promise<Pile[]>`; `Pile = { area: 'money' | 'mail'; id: string; count: number; label: (n: number) => string; to: string; due?: string; members?: string[] }`. `members` are item keys (`<module>:<id>`) of plannable rows the module owns or made (mail names the ToDos it created); they fold into the pile; a pile without members (budgets over their frame, the mail queue) is a count and a route. Core groups by `area`; the planner's own plannable source hands them over as kind `pile`, id `pile-<area>`. Titles of the two areas are the planner's strings. Routes: `/invoices?view=open`, `/subscriptions?view=notice`, `/budgets?view=over`, mail: the queue route of `feat/mail-find-overview` – each param untrusted, an allow-listed word, read once and removed from the URL (pattern `invoices/returnTo.ts`).

| Module | Pile | Members |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rechnungen | open invoices in the horizon, incl. those in „Frist vorbei“ | yes |
| Abos | cancellation deadlines within 14 days | yes |
| Unterlagen | deadlines within 14 days | yes |
| Budgets | budgets over their frame this month | no |
| E-Mail | the waiting queue (count + route; the queue is `feat/mail-find-overview`'s) plus ToDos made from a mail | the ToDos, by key |

„Frist vorbei“ (planner hub R2) stays as it is while the switch is off. With it on, the group's rows are members and the group is not drawn; the pile's meta carries „Frist vorbei“ as text.

**States.** offered · on (row unplaced / placed / fest) · expanded · nothing waits · a part's module off (that part is missing, said nowhere) · all parts off (no row).

**Risk.** A count is a figure that can grow: it is muted text in the meta, never a badge, never coloured, and falls as he works. Late money must not get quieter: „Frist vorbei“ stays in the pile's meta and the planner head's sentence is unchanged.

**Target (script).** Flow B `rowsBefore` 10 → 2; with idea 4 the rest of flow B as above.

**Not part of it.** No pile for ToDos, lists or routines (lists are one row already), no notification, no count on the widget or the home screen, no automatic day for a pile.

## Idea 5 – bundles with one start point

Two things carry steps, and both look the same in the plan.

**5a · a thing with steps (the source owns them).** `PlannableItem.steps?: { id: string; title: string; done: boolean }[]` (≤ 40, display and „Los“ only, not part of the input hash) and `PlannableSource.onStepDone?(id, stepId, done)`. Sources: ToDos (checklist), Listen (open items), Routinen (today's steps). (1) The row shows „Haushalt · 5 Schritte“, later „· 2 von 5“. (2) Expanded, the steps are tick lines. (3) ⋯ and the panel offer **„Los“**: the focus page (`/planner/focus/:unit`) in step mode shows the title small, **the next open step large**, „Fertig · weiter“, „Überspringen“, „Pause“; after the last step „Alles durch.“ and the row's own „Erledigt“. (4) Typing „Haushalt: Wäsche anstellen, Spülmaschine ausräumen, Bad wischen“ in the field proposes „Bündel · 3 Schritte“ (idea 3's preview); Enter makes one ToDo with a checklist. (5) Panel ⋯ „Als Routine speichern“ sends `routine.requested { name, steps }` (exists) – one handle, toast with the routine's name.

**5b · a bundle of things (the planner owns the bracket).** (1) „Auswählen“ → tick rows → „Bündeln …“ → a name (prefilled with the first title) → one row „Wohnung · 4 Dinge“. (2) Members keep their home and their own „Erledigt“; in the bundle they are its steps, in the order ticked, sortable in the panel. Their own day rows are removed in the same action (they are on the bundle's day now); the undo restores every row. (3) „Auflösen“ puts each member on the bundle's day (or „ohne Tag“). (4) A member done or deleted elsewhere is a done / missing step; a bundle whose steps are all done is ticked off like a done thing. (5) „Los“ as in 5a; a member without a tick (an invoice) shows its panel as the step. (6) „Als Routine speichern“ only when every member is a plain task (titles only travel).

**States.** closed (n steps / k of n) · expanded · in „Los“ (step i) · paused · all done · a step gone · source off (the member is skipped, named in the panel).

**Data.** 5a none. 5b `planner_bundle`, `planner_item.bundle` / `.step` ([CONCEPT §0.1](CONCEPT.md)); the planner's own source lists bundles (kind `bundle`, key `planner:b_<id>`); a member with `bundle` set is not listed on the board while the bundle is alive.

**Risk.** Bundling moves members' rows: it is one named action with one undo (rule 1). Deleting a bundle never deletes a member. Two devices bundling the same thing: later tap wins, the other bundle shows one step fewer.

**Target (script).** C1: rows 5 → 1, creating 5 typed lines → 1, working through 10 handles → 6 („Los“ + 5 × „Fertig · weiter“), `nextStepOnly` ja, `savedAsRoutine` ja in 2 handles.

**Not part of it.** No nesting, no step durations, no automatic bundle, no „Los“ that starts by itself, no change to the start line or the goal line of „Tag kippt“ (they stay; a bundle can be the day's start point like any row).

## Idea 8 – learn the real duration (struck by principle 1, kept ready)

The script shows no fewer rows and no fewer module switches ([ANALYSIS §4](ANALYSIS.md)); it is not in the package order. What it would be, so a „ja“ needs no new design:

**Behaviour.** (1) A focus round or a „Los“ step that ends with „Erledigt“ / „Fertig · weiter“ records the minutes that ran (pauses removed; < 1 min or > 8 h not recorded). (2) A **new** thing without minutes whose key has ≥ 2 records takes the median of the last 5, rounded to 5, instead of 30; the row says „20 Min · wie zuletzt“ until he changes it; the toast of the entry offers „Ändern“. (3) Nothing that exists changes its minutes. (4) „Dauer …“ shows „zuletzt 20, 25, 15 Min“ as a line.

**„The same thing“.** In this order: the source's stable id for things that come back (routine id, list id, a ToDo's series) → else the title as „Plan einfügen“ compares it (case, spaces, quotes, trailing marks ignored; digits kept). No fuzzy match: a near miss gets 30 as today.

**Data.** `planner_measure` (one row per record, pruned to 5 per key inside the recording action); synced with the planner's other rows between his own devices, in no export, no copy text, no assistant path. Switch „Aus gemessener Zeit vorschlagen“ (off deletes nothing; „Gemessene Zeiten löschen“ beside it).

**Risk.** A timer left running teaches nonsense (cap + median). Titles as keys hold what titles hold today – same store, same protection.

**Would lower.** Per-thing „Dauer …“ handles (C1: 5 → 0) and booked minutes (150 → about 40).
