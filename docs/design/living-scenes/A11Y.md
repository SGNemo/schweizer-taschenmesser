Numbers from the mockups (`node docs/design/living-scenes/mockups/measure.mjs`, 2026-11-27, Chromium; contrast from resolved colours and rendered pixels). Each package repeats its rows as tests in the product.

## 2. Text on a sky (clock tiles)

The three lines stand in the sky's ink (light theme: dark ink on day and golden skies, light ink at night; dark theme: light ink everywhere). Measured on the pixels under the text block:

| Band | Light theme | Dark theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| morning, day | 6.42–6.72 | 6.24–8.05 |
| evening | 10.43–10.67 | 7.22 wide · **3.14** on tiles under 300 px (the sun's glow under the text) |
| night | **1.00** wide (a star under a letter) · **3.35** narrow (the moon's glow) | the same |

The failures are all one fault: something bright lies under the text. **Rule (binding for package 4): the text's box is plain sky.** `SkyTile` gets the measured text box and (a) places no star inside it, (b) keeps the body's disc and glow outside it; if the tile is too narrow for that, the body is drawn at 60 % size, and if it still does not fit, it is left out – the sky's colour alone still tells the band. With plain sky under the text the lowest values are those of the gradients: night light ink on `#24356a` ≥ 9, evening dark theme ≥ 7. The acceptance test samples the rendered pixels under the text for every hour and tile width (162, 220, 363 px) and asserts ≥ 4.5.

## 3. Signs and scenes (non-text)

- Signs: the one drawn hue against the toned card ≥ 3:1 on every tone – lowest: Vorräte (fern) 3.82, Budgets (lime) 3.94, Routinen 4.24, Gewohnheiten 4.35 (all light theme, night tone); dark theme ≥ 5.54.
- Scenes carry no text and no control. Every datum of a picture is text in the same card (summary lines, rings, rows), so pictures are `aria-hidden="true"`, `focusable="false"`, and add nothing to the reading order. No information is carried by colour alone: a bloom's state is also its shape (bud, half, open), a done post has a flag.
- Screen reader: nothing new is announced; the new text parts ("2 heute erledigt", the four day parts) are ordinary text.
- Colour mode "Ruhig" / "Modulfarben: Aus": pictures in greys; shapes still tell the state.
- Touch targets, focus order and keyboard paths do not change; a picture is never a link (the card's title stays the link).

## 4. Cost

Ceilings: per scene picture **≤ 220 elements and ≤ 64 KB of markup for what its crop shows**; a sign ≤ 24 elements; a sky tile ≤ 24; first paint of the overview with pictures ≤ 1.25 × without (4× CPU, both windows).

| Picture | As drawn in the mockup (whole landscape) | What the crop shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| garden, wide (344 × 420), leer … viel | 175–246 elements, 71–80 KB | 60–160, 39–61 KB |
| garden, strip (380 × 112) | same | 45–115, 27–35 KB |
| lake, wide (343 × 359), 0 … 7 at night | 167–229, 70–75 KB | 104–167, 43–48 KB |
| lake, strip | same | 72–142, 42–47 KB |
| clock sky tile | 19 elements, 1.6 KB | – |
| weather card (as built in the app: 169 elements, 57 KB) | – | – |

- The mockup draws the whole landscape for every crop and is over the ceilings; the kit's culling (package 1) is what brings pictures under them. A test per spot state counts.
- First paint, 4× CPU, overview Aus → Verspielt in the mockup: 1920: 268 → 344 ms (1.28 ×) · 412: 240 → 380 ms (1.58 ×). Over the ceiling **in the mockup**, with three uncropped landscapes on the page; packages 2–5 measure in the app and must be inside 1.25 ×. Pictures below the fold are drawn when they come near the viewport ([CONCEPT-KIT §6](CONCEPT-KIT.md)).
- At rest a picture costs nothing: no animation, no timer but the shared minute tick, redraw only when its state key changes (the weather card today: 0 running animations, no measurable frame cost).

## 6. Motion

Nothing in a module picture moves by itself (WCAG 2.2.2 does not apply; 2.3.1: nothing flashes). Two transitions exist: a new shape fades in over 300 ms after the user's own tick; the tone changes over 600 ms a few times a day. Both are off with `prefers-reduced-motion` and the app's motion setting.

## 7. Known faults of the mockups (not of the design)

- Stars and the body can lie under the clock text (§2) – the rule fixes it in the product.
- Rain and snow are scaled with the crop (too long in the garden) – the kit draws them in the picture's scale.
- The mock draws whole landscapes (§4).
- The phone mock shows the lake and the garden directly after each other; by the rule ([CONCEPT-HOME §3](CONCEPT-HOME.md)) the second shows its sign.
