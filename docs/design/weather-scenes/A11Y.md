# Weather scenes – accessibility, contrast and render cost

Standard: WCAG 2.1 AA. Measured on the mockups (`mockups/scene.js`, Chromium headless, 2026-11-24): each scene rendered at 1650 × 280 (desktop stage) and 412 × 272 / 312 (phone stage), real pixels sampled in the rows where text sits. **Not measured: the 1280 window** (stage ≈ 1010 px; drawn as situation (8) of `07-kopf`, with `quietX` at the text column) – package 1b adds that width to its contrast run. The product repeats the palette part as a unit test (`ui/weather-scene/contrast.test.ts`) and the page part with axe.

## 1. Contrast of the text on the sky

Ink of text directly on the scene (title, eyebrow, temperature, detail line, rest line): **dark `#171f24` or light `#eceff1`, decided per scene** (`Scene.ink(state)`); never the theme's ink by accident (finds A6–A8 of the [analysis](ANALYSIS.md)).

| Sky key | Light theme: stops · ink | Dark theme: stops · ink |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `night`, `night-full`, `night-overcast`, `night-rain`, `night-snow`, `night-fog`, `dawn`, `dusk`, `eclipse-sky` | dark skies · light | same · light |
| `day`, `overcast`, `drizzle`, `snow`, `fog`, `heat`, `dust`, `golden` | light skies · dark | dimmed skies · light |
| `rain`, `storm` | dark greys · light | darker · light |

Exact stops: `mockups/palette.js` (five stops were darkened because of this measurement: dawn and dusk bottom, dark-theme golden bottom, light-theme rain, rain cloud).

**Result: 45 scene × size combinations; every text row ≥ 4.5 : 1 except one.**

| Scene (ink) | Worst row | Contrast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| heavy snow, dark theme (light ink) | desktop rest line, measured over x 30–1000 px | **4.25** – only right of x ≈ 560 px, where the snowy ridges rise; over the rest line's real width (≤ 36 rem) 4.78 |
| heavy rain, light theme (light) | desktop title row | 4.67 |
| golden hour, dark theme (light) | phone detail line | 4.74 |
| dawn / dusk (light) | desktop rest line | 4.83 / 4.89 |
| drizzle, light theme (dark) | phone detail line | 5.21 |
| overcast, dark theme (light) | phone detail line | 5.31 |
| all others | – | 5.5 – 11.2 |

Added with package 3b's mockups: the aurora scene (light ink) measures 6.2 in its worst desktop row (detail line, strongest curtains) and ≥ 12.8 on the phone; the official pill is opaque like the computed one.

Rules that follow, binding for the product:

1. The rest line never grows wider than 36 rem (it wraps); with that the one row above passes.
2. The **text shelter** is part of the scene, not a hope: nothing with hard contrast left of x = 900, a haze in the sky's bottom colour over the low ridges there, precipitation at 20 % strength, snow caps and rime fading out, stars thinned (single points of ≤ 1.2 units; they may fall behind a letter, which no measurement of text contrast counts).
3. In narrow crops (phone, widget) the quiet zone follows the crop (`quietX`), and the body sits in its own box (`bodyAt`) clear of every text and pill.
4. "Ruhig" / "Modulfarben: Aus": grey scene, ink by the theme; measured ≥ 5.5 in both themes (today: 1.1 at night in the light theme).

**Opaque elements** (chips, the phone's module row, the hint pill) carry their own backing and ink, independent of the scene – worst case over the darkest and lightest scene pixel under them:

| | Light theme | Dark theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chip and module-row text | 14.1 | 10.6 |
| Active module pill | 13.2 | 9.7 |
| Rain icon in a chip (`--info-ink`) | 6.4 | 7.0 |
| Hint text / suffix "berechnet, keine amtliche Warnung" (`--text-muted`) | 13.7 / 5.1 | 11.1 / 6.1 |
| Hint icon (`--warning-ink`) | 5.8 | 7.5 |

Non-text contrast (1.4.11) does not apply to the scene: it is decoration and carries no information of its own (§2). The moon glyph in a day row is paired with its name; its lit part is `currentColor` on the card (≥ 4.5).

## 2. Text alternatives – what the picture shows is also a word

The scene, the porthole and every glyph inside a labelled element are `aria-hidden="true"`; nothing in them is focusable.

| The picture shows | The word on the page |
| --- | --- |
| weather, intensity | detail line: the word of [COPY §1](COPY.md) |
| wind, storm, frost, snow lying, heat | the one addition in the detail line; the hint pill; the wind chip |
| day, night, twilight | no word needed (the time is the user's own); sunrise chip at night |
| moon phase, full moon, supermoon | chip "{Phase} · {n} %", card "Mond & Himmel" |
| moon at a forecast day | name beside the glyph; accessible name "Mond: {Phase}" on the pair |
| eclipse, meteors, rainbow, star night | event chip and the card's "Als Nächstes" block |
| dust | row in "Atemwege & Allergie" |
| the hint of the day | `role="note"` named "Wetterhinweis": lines, then "berechnet, keine amtliche Warnung" |

Reading order in the stage: h1 (place) → temperature → detail line → hint → chips → rest line. The late hint keeps its polite live region under the head. Colour never carries meaning alone: the hint has icon and words, an official warning (package 3b) its own label.

## 3. Motion (2.2.2, 2.3.1, 2.3.3)

- Nothing flashes: a bolt is a still shape; no opacity pulse anywhere.
- Motion **lasts 5 s** from opening the page and then stands still: it stays inside the five seconds that 2.2.2 allows without a pause control. The setting "Standbild" and the device's reduced-motion setting switch it off altogether.
- No motion at all with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `data-motion="reduce"`, "Standbild", "Schlicht", "Ruhig", "Modulfarben: Aus", in widgets, portholes and glyphs, while the page is hidden or the stage is out of view.
- Only `transform` is animated, on whole groups (plus one `opacity` fade of the precipitation layer if the port needs it to land on the still marks); nothing moves in or near the text shelter except precipitation at 20 % strength.
- No parallax, no motion tied to scrolling or the pointer.

## 4. Render cost – measured and the ceiling

| | Measured (heaviest of 67 states) | Ceiling for the product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SVG elements per scene | 169 (typical 81–114) | **≤ 220** |
| SVG markup | 37.4 KB (typical 20–32) | ≤ 48 KB |
| Build time of a scene | 0.5 ms; 2.7 ms at CPU ×4 | ≤ 0.7 ms; ≤ 3.5 ms at CPU ×4 |
| Animated groups | 7 | ≤ 9 |
| Main-thread work during the motion, CPU ×4 (measured over 14 s with the earlier 12 s motion; the 5 s motion does less) | 46 ms in total (≈ 0.05 ms a frame), 0 long tasks, 9–23 paints | ≤ 60 ms, 0 long tasks, ≤ 30 paints |
| After the motion has come to rest | 0 ms, 0 frames, 0 paints | 0 |

Not measured in this round: real GPU raster and layer counts (headless software raster), the Android and Windows WebViews (which may paint SVG transforms every frame – package 4 measures on the phone profile and a real device), battery, memory. If a WebView paints per frame, the fallback is "Standbild" on that platform, not a lighter drawing.
