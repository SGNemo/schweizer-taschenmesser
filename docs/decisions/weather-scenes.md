# Decisions – Weather scenes

The weather picture in the head (round 1: [design/weather-scenes](../design/weather-scenes/README.md); round 2: [design/weather-scenes-2](../design/weather-scenes-2/README.md)). The weather module's other decisions: [weather.md](weather.md).

- **Weather scenes, design round (2026-11-24):** the head's small sky becomes one illustrated scene (layers by weather, time of day, wind, frost, season; landscape "Mittelgebirge", no chooser), the hint of the day moves into the stage (still "berechnet, keine amtliche Warnung"), the word follows the WMO code. Moon and sky events are calculated on the device by an own small module (5.5 KB gzip, measured against astronomy-engine: light ≤ 0.01 pp, rise / set ≤ 0.2 min) – no dependency, no new network destination; lunar eclipses from a shipped table to 2050. Motion answers opening the page: 5 s, then still; on by default with "Standbild" and "Schlicht" one setting away (decided in the round, the maintainer may turn the default). No moon is drawn until it is calculated. Struck for lack of a source: aurora, official warnings, planets. `Sky` is not grown (the music head never used it) and goes with package 4. Why and how: [design/weather-scenes](../design/weather-scenes/README.md); built in five packages (1a, 1b, 2, 3, 4).
- **Weather scenes, the maintainer's answers (2026-11-24):** the overview widget gets the scene as its card background (the one named exception to "a widget is a tone step"; "Ruhig" keeps the plain card); motion stays on by default and "Standbild" switches it off; aurora and official warnings may use new sources – NOAA SWPC's Kp forecast (a global file, no position sent) and the DWD's warning service for places in Germany (position ~1 km), only for a main place inside Germany, from DWD level 2, replacing the computed hint of the same kind; the switch "Amtliche Warnungen" is on by default; built as package 3b with two new DATA-FLOWS rows ([CONCEPT-SOURCES](../design/weather-scenes/CONCEPT-SOURCES.md)).
- **Weather scenes 1a – word by code, data, ice hint (2026-11-24):** the word follows the WMO code (22 words, COPY §1) at every place a word stands; `CodeGroup` keeps nine members (cache validity, frozen languages), the raw `code`, `tz` and `now.snowDepth` sit beside it, all optional, so old caches stay valid and need no refetch. The one extra word in the head's detail line uses limits of the picture only (no rating or hint changes). UV is shown only while the sun is up at the place; at night the third chip is the next sunrise. New hint kind `ice` for freezing drizzle and rain (56, 57, 66, 67), ordered after wind and before rain; frost gets the thermometer. The bar above the head and its sentence stay until package 1b.
- **Weather scenes 1b – the scene in the head (2026-11-25):** the head's `Sky` gives way to one SVG scene (`ui/weather-scene`, a port of `mockups/scene.js` shape by shape; model `core/weather/scene.ts`). Colours are `--scene-*` tokens and `color-mix()` of them, declared once per scene as custom properties on the `<svg>`; "Ruhig" / "Modulfarben: Aus" draw the same shapes through a grey filter and a surface wash instead of a second palette. The ink of text on the stage follows the scene (`data-ink`), chips, pill, module row and gear keep the theme's ink on an opaque backing. The hint is a pill in the stage (no bar above the head); stage 280 / 272 px. Motion 5 s after opening, then rest; setting "Bild im Wetter-Kopf" (motion · still · plain) in the shared `weather` scope. No moon until package 2. One stop changed: dawn bottom `#7d6390` → `#7c628f` (4.48 → 4.55 : 1 with the light ink; the mockup's own measure missed the 4.5 line by rounding).
- **Weather scenes 2 – the moon (2026-11-25):** the moon is calculated on the device by `core/sky/` (a TypeScript port of `reference/astro`, Meeus with all 60 terms; no dependency, no network), checked against `vectors.json` (astronomy-engine 2.1.19) with the tolerances of CONCEPT-MOON §7. Names by rule (ii) on the place's local day (`Forecast.tz`, `Intl`), rise and set only when read. Moonrise / moonset search: the reference's 10-minute grid, but far from the horizon it jumps by |altitude| / 0.3° per minute (the altitude cannot change faster), which finds the same events (test against the plain grid, Tromsø included) and brings `moonOfDay` for seven days + now to 3.7 ms median at CPU ×4 (6.4 ms with the plain grid). The day moon stands at its own place in the sky, not at the mockup's fixed spot (which could sit on the sun), and is left out of the phone's crop. Switch "Mond und Himmelsereignisse" (on by default): off = no moon anywhere, the night chip falls back to the sunrise.
- **Weather scenes 3 – sky events (2026-11-25):** eclipses, meteor nights, seasons, rainbow, star night and dust come from `core/sky/` (`solarEclipse.ts` port of the reference search, `lunarEclipses.ts` table as a typed constant, `meteors.ts`, `events.ts` with the one-event priority of CONCEPT-EVENTS §2); only `dust` is added to the air request. Meteor values were checked against the IMO 2026 calendar through search excerpts (the PDF host is not reachable from the build sandbox); the Quadrantid ZHR is 80 there, not 110. "Within 7 days" counts local days (the eclipse of 2 August is "next" from 26 July). A lunar eclipse of today reads "Heute Nacht". On the phone the safety sentence alone takes two lines, so on an eclipse day the weather hints go under the head (stage ≤ 300 px); desktop keeps one beside it. The meteor cloud sentence uses the 60 % of §1, the eclipses the 80 % of §2. The second full moon stays the moon card's line of package 2 (no second place for it).
- **Weather scenes 3b – official warnings and aurora (2026-11-25):** both sources verified live first (DWD GeoServer: layer `dwd:Warnungen_Gemeinden`, JSON, CORS `*`, point filter `INTERSECTS(THE_GEOM,POINT(lat lon))` – latitude first –, `propertyName` keeps the answer near 1 KB; SWPC: an array of objects, CORS `*`; the old row layout is read too). Germany's border is the VG250 state outline (BKG, via the DWD GeoServer), merged and simplified at 1.5 km, as polyline strings (1.8 KB); Natural Earth was rejected (it puts Konstanz and Görlitz outside). DWD kinds come from `EC_GROUP` with the event name as fallback. The aurora is drawn only in the night phase. The IMO 2026 PDF confirms package 3's six showers.
- **Weather scenes 4 – small versions (2026-11-26):** line and widget draw the scene too (`ScenePorthole`, `cardFit`); the widget shows the full panorama scaled to its width, not the mockup's close crop, and the rain hint ends the word line. `Sky`, `skyKind`, `--sky-*` removed. The sway runs one 5 s iteration (its three boundaries restyled the whole page: 122 → 14 ms). The design documents stay (maintainer's wish).
- **Weather scenes round 2, package A – scene v2 (2026-11-26):** the drawing is `mockups/scene.js` + `events.js` + `motion.js` ported shape by shape into `ui/weather-scene/draw.ts`; round 1's drawing stays as `drawV1.ts` only for the porthole and the overview card until package D. The mockup's hex mixing becomes CSS: `color-mix(in srgb, …)` (the same arithmetic) and precomputed `--scene-grey-*` tokens for the greying (relative colour syntax is missing in older Safari and WebViews, and greying is linear, so only tokens are greyed), each declared once as `--cN` on the `<svg>`, so the themes stay in `tokens.css`. Path data is written relative with one decimal and the leaf, the rain tile's motion copy are `<use>`: the heaviest state (storm 3 with hail and wind 3, moving) lands under 64 KB and 220 elements. Closed the smallest way where the mockup draws no body (sentence 9): under the storm base and in dense fog without a deck the body is drawn pale in its light. Levels are pure functions in `core/weather/scene.ts`; thunder level 1 needs a known amount (an unknown one never makes a storm light), thunder 1 keeps its cover at most 3 (the tower needs sky), from level 2 the cover is the deck. The moon keeps round 1's tilt (`litPath`, `tiltRotation`) instead of the mockup's waxing flag. `dawn-2` stays `#7c628f` (palette.js repeats round 1's typo). Day rows read the thunder word from code and gusts only (a day's sum is no hour's amount). Phone: the stage is the picture (256 px); hint, two chips and the line of further values are the page's first block.
