# Decisions – planner hub („Planer als Steckenpferd“)

Part of [plan](plan.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). Concept: [CONCEPT-DESIGN](../design/planner-hub/CONCEPT-DESIGN.md) (direction A „Der Tag als Blatt“).

- **D2 – sheet, band and context column replace the week rows (2026-11-15, package R1, `feat/planner-sheet`).** The planner page opens on the first day that holds things (`?day=` → the pick stored today on this device → that day → today) and shows it as one sheet („Zuerst“ ≤ 3, „Danach“ five and „n weitere“, folded on the phone); the week is a band of seven tiles on one capacity scale, appointments and the waiting group lie in a context column (`SplitView`, under the sheet below 56 rem). This replaces the seven week rows with the day panel of 0b-4b part 2 ([ui-shell-identity-40-plan](ui-shell-identity-40-plan.md)) and the phone's strip. One row form for every source (lead slot, title opens the thing, meta in a fixed order, a ⋯ that stays in sight); late is said in words, never by colour. Without an appointment and with nothing waiting the main column keeps the width it has beside the context column (no stretched rows; the empty-share target of `planner-hub.spec.ts` is then read on that column). On the phone „Danach“ is folded, so a thing in it costs the fold's tap first (path C1 there: 2 taps, the title itself opens the thing in one; A and B likewise) – unfolding it by default would undo the phone's short first screen. Source: `docs/design/planner-hub/CONCEPT-DESIGN.md` §4, §8; guards `e2e/usecases/planner-hub.spec.ts` (`TARGET`), `modules/planner/__tests__/{page,band,head}.test.*`.
- **D3 – field first on the desktop, the plus opens it on the phone (2026-11-15, package R1).** The capture field is the first thing in the main column and has the focus on arrival on a desktop with a keyboard; „Eintragen“ is the page's one filled button; three example chips fill the field and never submit. On the phone the first screen is band and sheet and the plus of the bottom bar („Ding“) opens the same field as a sheet. This revises UI-audit rule 4 for the planner (the entry was the last row under the week). Deliberate deviation from HANDOFF §2 / §4.7 (accepted for now): the phone also keeps the field as the page's last row, without its examples, because without it every entry there costs one tap and one sheet more than before (path E of `planner-hub.spec.ts`: 0 → 1 tap, 0 → 1 dialog, 8 → 12 s). With it the phone holds 26 interactive elements under overload (`kopf-leer.spec.ts` D, bound 25 → 26: the one raised bound; row titles are controls now). Source: `docs/design/planner-hub/CONCEPT-DESIGN.md` §4.
- **Head: no filled action, the page's ⋯ in the title row (2026-11-15, package R1, HANDOFF §10 Q3).** Identity 4.0 asks for one filled `PageAction` in the desktop head; the planner has none there: the field's „Eintragen“ is the one filled action, the head carries the figure (the next day that asks for something, `modules/planner/head.ts`), the page menu ⋯ and the gear. On the phone `usePageAction` declares the plus („Ding“) and the ⋯ stands beside the sheet's day title (it holds „Tage einteilen“, so the phone's foot shows only „Rest verteilen“, and only on today). The figure takes the `sans` weight (no new variant). Source: `docs/design/planner-hub/CONCEPT-DESIGN.md` §4, §5.
- **D7 – late money leaves the days and the day sums (2026-11-15, package R2, `feat/planner-late-group`).** An invoice (kind `invoice`; also `cancel`, once a source hands a passed cancellation date over) whose hard deadline is before today is „Frist vorbei“: on no day, in no day's sum, bar or overload figure, not among what waits. A late chore (ToDo, routine) stays on its day with „Frist war Di“. The group is the first card of the context column (the column exists whenever there is late money), one unfolding row above the day where there is no column; the danger tone is one icon beside the words (D9), rows are neutral. Head rule 3: only late money → „Heute ist frei.“ · „Frist vorbei: 6 Rechnungen“. Revisable: one rule, `modules/planner/late.ts`.
- **The seam for D7: the planner filters before the engine (2026-11-15, package R2).** `lateMoneyDue` is asked in `run.ts buildInputs` (the thing is no `PlanItem`, so no run, sentence, „Rest verteilen“ or `moveAll` can place or move it; the item list is part of `inputHash`, so the run happens when a deadline passes) and in `board.ts buildBoard` (listed as `board.late`; its stored rows are hidden at once). No change to `core/plan/**` or `core/modules/types.ts`. Stored state goes through the existing paths: the run drops its session rows (`writeRows`), a planner mark on the source is handed back (`writeBackPlan`), a second run writes nothing. A pin stays stored and places nothing while the thing is late; „Nicht planen“ still takes it out (it then waits as „nicht im Plan“). Not shown: the group's sum – `PlannableItem` carries no amount (a contract field, not this package).
- **„Verschieben …“ is the one place that nails (2026-11-15, package R2).** ⋯ leads with „Verschieben …“: the board's seven days as chips; a tap pins the thing to that day with the existing pin action (one undoable run, toast with undo) – three taps to another day. „Festnageln auf <Tag>“ left the menu (the chip of the row's own day does it); „Nicht mehr festnageln“ and „Später“ stay as they were. „Warum?“ is the capacity itself („37 Min frei von 3 h 12 – warum?“, one control, no second link): it opens one line – the day's time, every blocker by name and length, the buffer, the window, the day's end (P80) – and „Zeit ändern“, which opens the day's settings (a day off: the capacity opens them directly).
- **„Nächste Fristen“ fills the context column; two phone bounds rise (2026-11-15, package R2).** The column's card after Termine names the next three things of the plan with a hard deadline inside the board's days that has not passed (title, date, source word; a row opens the source; no rows, no card) – read from the board only (`components/Context.tsx` `nextDeadlinesOf`), on the wide page only (the phone's context stays the late row and the waiting group). With it the Sunday scene of `planner-hub.spec.ts` (now also three upcoming invoices moved to later days) measures 19 % empty at 1920 on the whole content area, so the target ≤ 25 % is asserted there again whenever a context column exists (the main column alone otherwise). The two raised bounds, maintainer to confirm: C2 „Quelle öffnen: Rechnung“ on pixel-7 1 → 2 taps, 8 → 9 s, and F „Überfällige Rechnung begleichen“ on pixel-7 4 → 5 taps, 18 → 19 s – on the phone „Frist vorbei“ is one folded row above the day (mockup a-412), so a late invoice costs the unfolding tap; „Bezahlt …“ in the row (K1b) shortens F again.
