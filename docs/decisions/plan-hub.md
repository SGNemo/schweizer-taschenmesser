# Decisions – planner hub („Planer als Steckenpferd“)

Part of [plan](plan.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). Concept: [CONCEPT-DESIGN](../design/planner-hub/CONCEPT-DESIGN.md) (direction A „Der Tag als Blatt“).

- **D2 – sheet, band and context column replace the week rows (2026-11-15, package R1, `feat/planner-sheet`).** The planner page opens on the first day that holds things (`?day=` → the pick stored today on this device → that day → today) and shows it as one sheet („Zuerst“ ≤ 3, „Danach“ five and „n weitere“, folded on the phone); the week is a band of seven tiles on one capacity scale, appointments and the waiting group lie in a context column (`SplitView`, under the sheet below 56 rem). This replaces the seven week rows with the day panel of 0b-4b part 2 ([ui-shell-identity-40-plan](ui-shell-identity-40-plan.md)) and the phone's strip. One row form for every source (lead slot, title opens the thing, meta in a fixed order, a ⋯ that stays in sight); late is said in words, never by colour. Without an appointment and with nothing waiting the main column keeps the width it has beside the context column (no stretched rows; the empty-share target of `planner-hub.spec.ts` is then read on that column). On the phone „Danach“ is folded, so a thing in it costs the fold's tap first (path C1 there: 2 taps, the title itself opens the thing in one; A and B likewise) – unfolding it by default would undo the phone's short first screen. Source: `docs/design/planner-hub/CONCEPT-DESIGN.md` §4, §8; guards `e2e/usecases/planner-hub.spec.ts` (`TARGET`), `modules/planner/__tests__/{page,band,head}.test.*`.
- **D3 – field first on the desktop, the plus opens it on the phone (2026-11-15, package R1).** The capture field is the first thing in the main column and has the focus on arrival on a desktop with a keyboard; „Eintragen“ is the page's one filled button; three example chips fill the field and never submit. On the phone the first screen is band and sheet and the plus of the bottom bar („Ding“) opens the same field as a sheet. This revises UI-audit rule 4 for the planner (the entry was the last row under the week). Deliberate deviation from HANDOFF §2 / §4.7 (accepted for now): the phone also keeps the field as the page's last row, without its examples, because without it every entry there costs one tap and one sheet more than before (path E of `planner-hub.spec.ts`: 0 → 1 tap, 0 → 1 dialog, 8 → 12 s). With it the phone holds 26 interactive elements under overload (`kopf-leer.spec.ts` D, bound 25 → 26: the one raised bound; row titles are controls now). Source: `docs/design/planner-hub/CONCEPT-DESIGN.md` §4.
- **Head: no filled action, the page's ⋯ in the title row (2026-11-15, package R1, HANDOFF §10 Q3).** Identity 4.0 asks for one filled `PageAction` in the desktop head; the planner has none there: the field's „Eintragen“ is the one filled action, the head carries the figure (the next day that asks for something, `modules/planner/head.ts`), the page menu ⋯ and the gear. On the phone `usePageAction` declares the plus („Ding“) and the ⋯ stands beside the sheet's day title (it holds „Tage einteilen“, so the phone's foot shows only „Rest verteilen“, and only on today). The figure takes the `sans` weight (no new variant). Source: `docs/design/planner-hub/CONCEPT-DESIGN.md` §4, §5.
