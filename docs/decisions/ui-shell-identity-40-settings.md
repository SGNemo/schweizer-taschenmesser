# Decisions – shell – Identity 4.0, settings and tools

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). The module areas: [ui-shell-identity-40-areas](ui-shell-identity-40-areas.md).

- **Identity 4.0 settings (2026-11-12, package 0b-E part E-1, `feat/ui4-settings-tools`; spec and the maintainer's four answers: #757, [PACKAGES](../design/ui-audit-2026-11/PACKAGES.md)).** _Decision:_ a settings tab and a module's settings page have the title line of rule 2 (`pages/settings/components/SettingsTitle`: plate in the hue, eyebrow, one h1, at most two lines below it; 84–108 px on the desktop, 79–103 px on the phone) instead of the tinted head card; the start page keeps its status head (0b-6). The quick switches ("Schnell umschalten") are one named group on `/settings` only – first on the desktop, below the categories on the phone – and no longer stand above every tab. Lists, groups, state card, key caps and the fold are tone steps without a frame; the groups of a tab keep their hairlines on the page (the v3.1 decision "no stack of cards" stands: a raised card per group would bring the stack back). The folded lines of Darstellung open as a whole, "Ändern" is neutral ink (no arrow: the word says the state, and five more icons per tab said nothing new). The list of modules is one column of rows on a `ListSurface` (one line per module on the desktop), not a wall of tiles. _Phone:_ the way back is a 44 px button in the plate's place beside the title (named "Einstellungen" / "Alle Module"), so there is no back row and no hit area over the title; a lone switch stays beside its text; a plain text link is 44 px to hit. _One piece:_ a tab stays out of sight until none of its sections waits for its code, plus 120 ms for the stored values (`TabBody`; a skeleton only after 300 ms, shown after 1.5 s whatever still waits), and is never hidden again afterwards; "Über Nemo" reads its facts before it paints. _Kept as they are:_ every switch, safety text and confirmation of Local API, Sync, LAN hub, remote control and supporter mode; stores and scopes of all settings. _Not a `ui/Tabs`:_ the groups of a tab look like tabs but stay a list of links with `aria-current="location"`, because the protected guard `e2e/seed/ui6-home-settings.seed.spec.ts` pins that markup. _Left as frames:_ the danger zone (red with its words) and the file picker drawn as a button. _Not met as first written:_ "first section from y ≤ 220 px on the phone" – the settings carry their own search field above the head (76 px), so the guard holds 320 px (measured 284–313, before 387–518). Rejected: one loading boundary for the whole tab (a part that loads later hid the whole tab again), the 15 categories as tabs in the head (they run out of the picture). Guard: `e2e/seed/settings-area.seed.spec.ts`. Part E-2 (the tools) follows as its own package.
- **Identity 4.0 tools (2026-11-12, package 0b-E part E-2, `feat/ui4-tools`, #763).** _Decision:_ a tool is one row (`pages/tools/ToolRow`: plate in the hue, name, one line, the switch), the same on `/tools` and in Einstellungen → Werkzeugkasten (the maintainer's answer 3 in #757). What only a tool that is on can do shows only then: "Öffnen" (its name says which tool) and its place in the toolbar ("⋯" menu, and the grip in the settings); a tool that is off carries its switch alone (the settings list had three more buttons on each of them). `/tools` has the title line with "4 aktiv · 16 verfügbar" as its context line instead of the head card; its groups are a heading and one `ListSurface`, no frame and no tint; `LibraryCard` and `library/Library.module.css` stay with the module library (0b-6). "Braucht Internet" is said only where it is true (the badge "Auch ohne Internet" on every other tool is gone). _The tools themselves:_ keys, cards, mats, the plot and the diff and regex boxes are tone steps (a recess in the panel: `--surface-sunken`), no shadow and no ring; the calculator's keys keep their press movement. _Kept:_ the tint of a display or mat in the tool's hue, the die's ring in the tool's colour and the persona's stamp (a mark, not a surface); `tools/isolation.test.ts` and `tools/noEval.test.ts` are untouched. _Two lists, one row, not one list:_ the tool page keeps search, filter and groups, the settings keep the toolbar's order with dragging; the tests of both keep their hooks (`tool-<id>`, `tools--<id>`, "<Name> einschalten"). On `/tools` a move changes places with the next tool that is on (before, it swapped with the neighbour in the list of all tools, which showed nothing when that one was off) and a toast says the new place, because the page is grouped and not in the toolbar's order; the row's menu is named for its tool and the focus returns to it after a move. Shapes keep an invisible edge that forced colours draw. _Also in this PR, a fix to E-1:_ a waiting tab is hidden, `inert` and `aria-hidden` but laid out again (it was `display: none`, and the protected guard `mobile-layout.spec.ts` "Big monitor" found no box for a field). Guard: `e2e/seed/tools-area.seed.spec.ts`.
