# Decisions – Day and week planning – use case „Wochenende retten“

Part of [plan](plan.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). Split from [plan-contract](plan-contract.md) (budget); use case decisions outside the planner: [features-wochenende](features-wochenende.md).

- **„Rest verteilen“ is the op list of `moveAll(today, tomorrow)`, asked for over the bus (2026-11-14, use case „Wochenende retten“ F3):** `restToTomorrow` in `core/plan/instructions.ts` sets today to 0 and gives each open thing of today `earliest = tomorrow`; a thing nailed to today keeps its day, a hard due of today gets no op (with no time left the run places it on the next day and marks it late). Always a preview first, one undo. The ToDo widget's „Tag abschließen“ emits `plan.restRequested` with an `open(path)` callback while the planner is on; the planner names its own page (`/planner?rest=1`), no module import. While the planner is on, „Auf morgen schieben“ gives way to it: a ToDo the planner does not hold (excluded) keeps its own date. The optional 20:00 notification is `focus.restReminder` (off by default), soft, device-local, once a day, never repeated, left out inside the quiet hours and a „Stumm“ pause. No count of what was left anywhere.
- **„Als Pause …“ converts, through an additive `PlannableSource.onRemove?(id)` (2026-11-15, use case „Wochenende retten“ package 4a, REVIEW-FABLE R2/R3/R8):** the row's thing becomes a blocker of its day (1/2/3 h) and the source removes it (a ToDo with its subtasks, soft delete; a recurring ToDo first creates its next instance without a tick – no `todos.task.completed`, so the series goes on) and returns an idempotent restore (or `undefined`: nothing removed, no blocker), which the planner records into its own `undoable`, so one undo brings back the thing, its plan rows and takes the blocker out. No `asBreakOn` field, no engine case: a blocker is what the engine already knows, and leisure never stays an open task (morning digest, „Erledigt heute“). A line that starts with „Pause“/„Freizeit“ (quick capture with the planner on, „Tage einteilen“, the field) is the quick-capture target `break`: a blocker of its day (typed date ?? today, typed span or length ?? 1 h, times only when typed), never a ToDo; a plain „Zocken“ stays a ToDo. Known limit: `blockers` is one LWW field of the day row, so two devices adding a break to the same day offline keep one (as before for any blocker). Rejected: an `asBreakOn` row field (left the ToDo open), one menu row per length (R8: one entry opens the choice).
- **Day parts are planner-own and only order the day (2026-11-15, „Wochenende retten“ 4b, REVIEW-FABLE R2/R8):** `part?` on the planner item row (v3, empty step) and `PlanItem`, never in `plannable`; order tier → part (none first) → old keys, days as before. Window: fixed borders 12:00/18:00 cut to it; a session starts in or after its part (may run past the border), else no time; a part outside the window only orders; a pinned or deadline unit leads the list but keeps its part's time. Field words „vormittags/nachmittags/abends“, „am …“ only. Known limit: an app below v3 in the sync group drops `part` when it writes the item row (as v2 windows). Rejected: window thirds, a part that moves the day.
- **A deadline may carry its hour (2026-11-15, „Wochenende retten“ 5b, Q4 a):** additive `due.time?` ('HH:mm', local) on `PlannableItem` and the item row (v4), ToDo `dueTime?`/`dueRemind?` (v6), empty steps; the run strips it, the engine places by day. „bis/spätestens <Tag> <Uhrzeit>“ writes `dueTime`, never a note line. Row „Frist So 18:00“, then „Frist war …“, never red. „Erinnern: 1 Std vorher“ is opt-in, sent by the ToDos (once, not soft, without `todoDigest`). Rejected: hour-exact planning, an automatic reminder.
