# Decisions – shell – Identity 4.0, knowledge and system (package 0b-6)

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md), continues [ui-shell-identity-40-areas](ui-shell-identity-40-areas.md) (money, plan), which reached its size budget. Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md).

- **Identity 4.0 knowledge area, first half (2026-11-12, package 0b-6a, `feat/ui4-0b-6-knowledge-system`; the package is split, 0b-6b brings Browser, Dieser PC, Downloads and the status line of the settings).** _Decision:_ Merkliste, Notizen, Chat, Feeds, Mail and Musik lie on tone steps (no frame, no shadow in `main`), each has one entry. _What left the page under the head:_ every signature block (pinboard cards, note paper, bubble pair, masthead, stamp and envelope) and the feeds' "Zuletzt hinzugefügt" card (it repeated a row, B2); the marks stay as the empty states' motifs and on the widgets. _Per page:_ Merkliste – "Merkliste" and "Lesezeichen" are `Tabs`, the search is the first row with the "⋯" beside it on the phone, an empty page offers one verb; Notizen – "Neue Seite" is the head's `PageAction` (plus: "Notiz eintragen"), the list's row holds view switch, search and menu, the open page is a recess and a weight, the sheets of the cards view are neutral (the notebook is its dot and name); Chat – threads are `ItemRow`s, your message is a recess and the answer a raised surface (side and tone, no accent); Feeds – groups are `ListSurface`s in one column instead of newspaper columns; Mail – the unread count is the head's figure ("2 ungelesen", none at zero), "Neue Mail" the one filled action, "Suchen" neutral, refresh is an icon button beside the search, the mail settings stay in the head as a button with its name instead of a second gear (the panel alone clears the mail cache and has to open without an account too), a row is sender and time on one line and two lines of text; Musik – the views are `Tabs` under the stage, not in the head: inside it the stage with a song playing would be 362 px (limit 320). _`layout: 'split'` for Notizen and Mail:_ both keep their own columns (an editor and a three-column reader are no `DetailPanel`); `split` only gives them the page width of the other master–detail pages. _Reading measure:_ Chat and Feeds are `layout: 'reading'` (one column of rows; at `content` a row ran 1 586 px wide on a 1920 window, rule 5). _Module library:_ an area is a heading and its cards on the page (a card is the only level), the card's head has no tint (`LibraryCard plainHead`: a prop of the shared card, because the tool library keeps the tinted head until package 0b-E), the drawn widget sketch is neutral ink on a recess, the 27 switches are `tone="neutral"`; the module's colour stays on the plate beside its name. The tool library keeps its look until its own package. _Rejected:_ a "⋯" menu named "Mail-Einstellungen" (the protected `mail.seed` guard opens the panel by a button of that name), tabs "Chats · Archiv" (the archive is rare, a unit test pins the checkbox), deleting `mail/components/InboxHead.module.css` (the protected `ui/tokens.test.ts` reads it by name: an empty file until 0b-8). _Open for the sweep 0b-8:_ the notebook `FolderTabs` (shared `ui`: hairline and the notebook's colour, which names the notebook), the shortcut hint of "Neue Seite" (`PageAction` takes no `title`; `notes.shortcutNew` is unused), the unused texts `feeds.lead` and `mail.head.unread`, the test id `mail-stamp` (the protected guard reads it), the fixed values the design-system pass found in these modules (design check F6). _Answered by the maintainer (2026-11-12, the three questions of #759, each as recommended):_ while Chat has no AI the plus (and the head's "Neuer Chat") is disabled, so the set-up hint is the one filled button (a small fix PR after #759, a behaviour change); the empty `InboxHead.module.css` stays until 0b-8; Chat and Feeds keep the reading measure. Guard: `e2e/seed/knowledge-area.seed.spec.ts`.
