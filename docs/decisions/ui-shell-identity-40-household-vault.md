# Decisions – shell – Identity 4.0, household and vault

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). The other area packages: [ui-shell-identity-40-areas](ui-shell-identity-40-areas.md).

- **Identity 4.0 household and vault (2026-11-12, package 0b-5, `feat/ui4-0b-5-household-vault`).** _Decision:_ Listen, Vorräte, Fitness, Unterlagen and Passwörter follow the money area: tone steps, `Tabs` for the views, one filled action, a "⋯" menu for everything occasional, content from the first screen of the phone. _Views:_ Listen – one tab per list; Vorräte – Alle, Läuft bald ab, Nachkaufen (they were a filter); Fitness – Training, Essen, Woche; Unterlagen – Alle and the three states with their counts (they were folder tabs in the old signature slot); categories of Unterlagen and groups of Passwörter are a `Segmented` filter under the search. _Entry (rule 4):_ the add field of Listen is the last row of the list (`?new=1` scrolls to it and focuses it); the head's action and the phone's plus stay "Neue Liste" (`mobile-layout.spec.ts` pins the plus); "Aus Vorlage", editing, resetting, copying and sharing are in the menu, and so are "Eintrag hinzufügen" (it scrolls to the field of a long list) and "Gekauftes entfernen", which also stays one tap under the list while something is in the basket; the page's first line says what the head's figure does not (sums, or what is in the basket; packed and the trip for a packing list), an empty list has none. Vorräte: "Mehr Felder anzeigen" is a menu entry (`useAdvancedMenuItem`). Fitness: "Wie letztes Mal" and the calorie goal are menu entries. _Passwörter:_ the entry is a plain button, never the page action of the other modules, so the phone's plus stays the plain capture there (`exclusion.test.ts`); generator, check, import and export, emergency sheet and the browser extension are the menu; "Sperren" stays in sight beside the state it changes, never inside a menu, and while the browser extension is connected the same line says so (it is a channel into the open vault). _Master–detail:_ Unterlagen is `layout: 'split'`: on a wide page a picked folder shows in `DetailPanel` (none before a pick, and a pick ends when its folder leaves the list: the note and the full detail are not shown to whoever looks at the screen when the page opens, unlike Rechnungen), below that width it opens its dialog; the panel holds "Bearbeiten", renewing and downloading stay the folder's own buttons. _Signature blocks:_ `vault-doc` and `pantry-label` keep their hairline; the amount stepper inside a label lost its frame, so `pantry-label.framedInside` is gone from `signature-blocks.json`. _What left the old signature slot:_ the shelf, the receipt, the suitcase (its text is the page's first line, `ListSummary`) and the state tabs; the week plates moved into the tab "Woche"; the door of Passwörter stays, with the state and the lock button. _Rejected:_ the lock button inside the menu (two clicks to lock), a page action on Passwörter (breaks the exclusion), removing the "Jetzt" row of Vorräte (`now-strip.spec.ts` promises it). Guard: `e2e/seed/household-vault.seed.spec.ts`. _Answered by the maintainer (2026-11-12, the four questions of #756, each as recommended):_ the page's "⋯" stays beside the first group heading on the phone (as on Rechnungen); "Wie letztes Mal" stays a menu entry; the panel of Unterlagen stays empty until a pick; the six ratchet hits are to be approved (three raised values of Unterlagen, `a11y.spec.ts`, `money-narrow.seed.spec.ts`, `pantry-label`).
- 2026-11-13 **Unterlagen: Fristen (module package T-a of UI audit 2026-11, `feat/ui4-t-a-deadlines`; plan: [PACKAGES](../design/ui-audit-2026-11/PACKAGES.md) "Modul-Pakete").** _Views:_ the tabs of Unterlagen are "Ablage" and "Fristen"; the three counted state tabs are gone (FOCUS checklist 9: what goes instead) and with them the filter by state: what needs a look is the one line under the head figure and the upper part of the rail. _Fristen:_ every document with a date on one rail (`DatedTimeline`, the timeline of the widget catalogue over days, in `ui/widgets/Timeline.tsx`; only on the module page), oldest first, "Heute" between what has run out and what comes; the date of a row is the cancellation deadline while it lies ahead, otherwise the end. Expired = a badge in the warning tone, a deadline this week = accent, never red (red stays for overdue money; the folders of "Ablage" follow: their "Abgelaufen" badge is warning now, it was danger); what is near says so in its own words ("Kündbar bis · diese Woche", "Kündbar bis · bald", "Läuft bald ab"), never by the colour alone; the rail names the deadline ("Kündbar bis · diese Woche"), not an order to cancel. The badges of "Ablage" keep their older words ("Jetzt kündigen", "Bald", "Frist vorbei"): aligning them is a follow-up. All dated documents ahead are listed, also those years ahead: the rail is the household's calendar of papers, not a to-do list. The past does not grow for years: what ran out more than 90 days ago is one counted line above the rail, whatever its category. _Verlängert:_ one button (rail and folder) for every category but Garantien and Steuer, from the day the cancellation deadline is a week away or the end has come until 90 days after the end (what ran out long ago is history, not a call to act; its end date is changed in its dialog); a contract whose deadline has passed runs on and offers it only in the last 30 days of its term (until now for the whole rest of the term: a standing call to act, also for whoever cancelled in time). It opens a dialog that proposes the new end and writes on "Verlängerung speichern" (undo in the toast); the proposal is one more term of the same length, in whole months when the term is that (also from a day the last month lacks: 29 Feb 2024 – 28 Feb 2025 is a year, 31 Jan – 28 Feb a month), otherwise in days; without `startDate` nothing is proposed. The renewal also moves `startDate` to the new term's first day (otherwise the second renewal would propose two terms); the dialog says so before it happens, and "Rückgängig" gives the old term back. The dialog works on the row as stored now, not on a copy from the click. Until now the button added twelve months at once for contracts and insurances only. _Datei-Vorschau:_ pictures only (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF), after a tap, in the panel and in the dialog; an object URL in memory, revoked when hidden or left, inside `NoReadAid`; nothing is written (test: no table, no storage key changes; and the negative list of the plan is `core/diagnostics/previewNegativeList.test.tsx`: while a preview is shown, a report holds neither title, file name nor object URL, and the URL is in no table, backup, stored key or recent search; `diagnostics.test.ts` is unchanged). What decides is the type of the file that lies on this device, not the synced `fileType`; left before the file was read, no URL is made; one read at a time. PDFs get no preview: the app's content security policy allows no frames or objects, and it is not weakened for this. _Promise C04 holds:_ no title of a document on the overview (`e2e/seed/vault-deadlines.seed.spec.ts`); widget, calendar, attention and reminders are unchanged. _Not in it:_ a filter by state, a preview of PDFs, a reminder change.
- 2026-11-13 **Unterlagen: one vocabulary for dates (`fix/vault-deadline-words`, follow-up of T-a).** _Answered by the maintainer (the three questions of #781, each as recommended):_ the filter by state stays gone; a renewal keeps moving "Beginn" (no field for the original start); the badges of "Ablage" get the rail's words. _Words:_ `DeadlineKind` is the one source for both views: "Kündigungsfrist endet in 3 Tagen" (accent, within a week: the day is said), "Kündigungsfrist endet bald", "Läuft bald ab", "Abgelaufen" (warning), "Kündigungsfrist endet", "Läuft ab" (plain, rail only). They name the deadline, never an order ("Jetzt kündigen" is gone) and no dangling "bis" ("Kündbar bis · bald" read as "bis bald"). A folder says nothing for a passed cancellation deadline: its small print already says "Frist vorbei am …". The folder's small print keeps the label "Kündigen bis" with the date itself ("Kündigen bis: 2. Okt. 2026", as in the panel); the day count is the badge's, so nothing is said twice. _Empty rail:_ when every dated document ran out more than 90 days ago the title is "Gerade steht keine Frist an.", not "Keine Unterlage hat ein Datum.". _Dialog "Verlängert":_ a proposal that can be confirmed as it stands keeps the first focus on "Verlängerung speichern" (decided in #781), and the button is described by the old end, the move of the start and the proposed date, so a screen reader hears what is confirmed; a proposal that still lies in the past, or none, puts the first focus on the date. _Preview:_ the button keeps the keyboard focus while the file is read (`aria-disabled`, not `disabled`) and the state is said ("Vorschau wird geladen …"). _Late council round on #781 (after the merge):_ Architect 8 and Guardian 9 approved, Advocate 7 asked for the wording and focus points above. _Not in it:_ "Verlängert" inside the opened document; another word than "Abgelaufen" for a contract that renewed by itself.
- 2026-11-13 **Unterlagen: "Verlängert" before the end, one write per renewal (`fix/ui4-t-a-followup`, follow-up of T-a after the council over all of T-a).** The words of both views are `DeadlineKind` (bullet above); this adds only behaviour. _"Verlängert" before the end:_ a paper without a cancellation deadline (passport, ID card, vehicle inspection, an insurance without notice) offers the button as long as it reads "Läuft bald ab" (`EXPIRY_SOON_DAYS`, 60 days), because such papers are renewed before they run out; until now only from the end day on. Such a renewal before the end is a new paper, not the old term going on: nothing is proposed (the new end is on the paper; the first focus is the date) and the new term starts today, which the dialog says (`renewedEarly`); on or after the end it goes on from the end as before. Contracts with a deadline are unchanged and go on from their end whenever they are renewed. _Order:_ the folders sort as their badges read: a deadline this week, run out, soon, then a passed deadline (it has no badge), then the rest. _One write per dialog:_ the renew dialog holds the lock itself (the write changes the term and the form starts afresh for it: a second Enter must not renew the next term on top); the lock returns once the dialog is closed, or when the write fails. _Focus:_ after a renewal the row's "Verlängert" is gone with the new term, so the focus goes to the row's own button right after the dialog has closed (one announcement, not the gone button first): on the rail its title (`data-doc-id`), in a folder the row the click came from (`[data-row]`); once the list has changed, it is set again only if a row drawn anew lost it. Ids are compared as values, never put into a selector (an imported id may hold any character). _Not in it, follow-up:_ keeping the chosen view in the address.
