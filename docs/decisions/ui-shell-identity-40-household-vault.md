# Decisions – shell – Identity 4.0, household and vault

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). The other area packages: [ui-shell-identity-40-areas](ui-shell-identity-40-areas.md).

- **Identity 4.0 household and vault (2026-11-12, package 0b-5, `feat/ui4-0b-5-household-vault`).** _Decision:_ Listen, Vorräte, Fitness, Unterlagen and Passwörter follow the money area: tone steps, `Tabs` for the views, one filled action, a "⋯" menu for everything occasional, content from the first screen of the phone. _Views:_ Listen – one tab per list; Vorräte – Alle, Läuft bald ab, Nachkaufen (they were a filter); Fitness – Training, Essen, Woche; Unterlagen – Alle and the three states with their counts (they were folder tabs in the old signature slot); categories of Unterlagen and groups of Passwörter are a `Segmented` filter under the search. _Entry (rule 4):_ the add field of Listen is the last row of the list (`?new=1` scrolls to it and focuses it); the head's action and the phone's plus stay "Neue Liste" (`mobile-layout.spec.ts` pins the plus); "Aus Vorlage", editing, resetting, copying and sharing are in the menu, and so are "Eintrag hinzufügen" (it scrolls to the field of a long list) and "Gekauftes entfernen", which also stays one tap under the list while something is in the basket; the page's first line says what the head's figure does not (sums, or what is in the basket; packed and the trip for a packing list), an empty list has none. Vorräte: "Mehr Felder anzeigen" is a menu entry (`useAdvancedMenuItem`). Fitness: "Wie letztes Mal" and the calorie goal are menu entries. _Passwörter:_ the entry is a plain button, never the page action of the other modules, so the phone's plus stays the plain capture there (`exclusion.test.ts`); generator, check, import and export, emergency sheet and the browser extension are the menu; "Sperren" stays in sight beside the state it changes, never inside a menu, and while the browser extension is connected the same line says so (it is a channel into the open vault). _Master–detail:_ Unterlagen is `layout: 'split'`: on a wide page a picked folder shows in `DetailPanel` (none before a pick, and a pick ends when its folder leaves the list: the note and the full detail are not shown to whoever looks at the screen when the page opens, unlike Rechnungen), below that width it opens its dialog; the panel holds "Bearbeiten", renewing and downloading stay the folder's own buttons. _Signature blocks:_ `vault-doc` and `pantry-label` keep their hairline; the amount stepper inside a label lost its frame, so `pantry-label.framedInside` is gone from `signature-blocks.json`. _What left the old signature slot:_ the shelf, the receipt, the suitcase (its text is the page's first line, `ListSummary`) and the state tabs; the week plates moved into the tab "Woche"; the door of Passwörter stays, with the state and the lock button. _Rejected:_ the lock button inside the menu (two clicks to lock), a page action on Passwörter (breaks the exclusion), removing the "Jetzt" row of Vorräte (`now-strip.spec.ts` promises it). Guard: `e2e/seed/household-vault.seed.spec.ts`. _Answered by the maintainer (2026-11-12, the four questions of #756, each as recommended):_ the page's "⋯" stays beside the first group heading on the phone (as on Rechnungen); "Wie letztes Mal" stays a menu entry; the panel of Unterlagen stays empty until a pick; the six ratchet hits are to be approved (three raised values of Unterlagen, `a11y.spec.ts`, `money-narrow.seed.spec.ts`, `pantry-label`).
- 2026-11-13 **Unterlagen: Fristen (module package T-a of UI audit 2026-11, `feat/ui4-t-a-deadlines`; plan: [PACKAGES](../design/ui-audit-2026-11/PACKAGES.md) "Modul-Pakete").** _Views:_ the tabs of Unterlagen are "Ablage" and "Fristen"; the three counted state tabs are gone (FOCUS checklist 9: what goes instead) and with them the filter by state: what needs a look is the one line under the head figure and the upper part of the rail. _Fristen:_ every document with a date on one rail (`DatedTimeline`, the timeline of the widget catalogue over days, in `ui/widgets/Timeline.tsx`; only on the module page), oldest first, "Heute" between what has run out and what comes; the date of a row is the cancellation deadline while it lies ahead, otherwise the end. Expired = a badge in the warning tone, a deadline this week = accent, never red (red stays for overdue money; the folders of "Ablage" follow: their "Abgelaufen" badge is warning now, it was danger); what is near says so in its own words ("Jetzt kündigen", "Bald kündigen", "Läuft bald ab"), never by the colour alone; the rail names the deadline ("Kündbar bis · diese Woche"), not an order to cancel. The badges of "Ablage" keep their older words ("Jetzt kündigen", "Bald", "Frist vorbei"): aligning them is a follow-up. All dated documents ahead are listed, also those years ahead: the rail is the household's calendar of papers, not a to-do list. The past does not grow for years: what ran out more than 90 days ago is one counted line above the rail, whatever its category. _Verlängert:_ one button (rail and folder) for every category but Garantien and Steuer, from the day the cancellation deadline is a week away or the end has come until 90 days after the end (what ran out long ago is history, not a call to act; its end date is changed in its dialog); a contract whose deadline has passed runs on and offers it only in the last 30 days of its term (until now for the whole rest of the term: a standing call to act, also for whoever cancelled in time). It opens a dialog that proposes the new end and writes on "Verlängerung speichern" (undo in the toast); the proposal is one more term of the same length, in whole months when the term is that (also from a day the last month lacks: 29 Feb 2024 – 28 Feb 2025 is a year, 31 Jan – 28 Feb a month), otherwise in days; without `startDate` nothing is proposed. The renewal also moves `startDate` to the new term's first day (otherwise the second renewal would propose two terms); the dialog says so before it happens, and "Rückgängig" gives the old term back. The dialog works on the row as stored now, not on a copy from the click. Until now the button added twelve months at once for contracts and insurances only. _Datei-Vorschau:_ pictures only (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF), after a tap, in the panel and in the dialog; an object URL in memory, revoked when hidden or left, inside `NoReadAid`; nothing is written (test: no table, no storage key changes; and the negative list of the plan is `vault.preview.diagnostics.test.tsx`: a report written while a preview is shown holds neither title, file name nor object URL; `diagnostics.test.ts` is unchanged). What decides is the type of the file that lies on this device, not the synced `fileType`; left before the file was read, no URL is made; one read at a time. PDFs get no preview: the app's content security policy allows no frames or objects, and it is not weakened for this. _Promise C04 holds:_ no title of a document on the overview (`e2e/seed/vault-deadlines.seed.spec.ts`); widget, calendar, attention and reminders are unchanged. _Not in it:_ a filter by state, a preview of PDFs, a reminder change.
