# Decisions – shell – Identity 4.0, area packages

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). Foundation (0b-1) and head/navigation (0b-2): [ui-shell-identity-v31](ui-shell-identity-v31.md). Household and vault (0b-5): [ui-shell-identity-40-household-vault](ui-shell-identity-40-household-vault.md).

- **Identity 4.0 money area (2026-11-11, package 0b-3, `feat/ui4-0b-3-money`; plan: [PACKAGES](../design/ui-audit-2026-11/PACKAGES.md)).** _Decision:_ Finanzen, Rechnungen, Abos and Budgets lie on tone steps (no frame, no shadow in `main`), their views are `Tabs`, and each page has one entry: the `PageAction` and a "⋯" menu that holds "Mehr Felder anzeigen" (`useAdvancedMenuItem`); no switch and no second button in the head. _What left the old signature slot:_ all of it is gone – the account cards (the tab "Konten" lists every account with its balance), the three budget envelopes, the subscriptions ring with "Nächste Abbuchungen" and "Nächste bezahlen" – because the lists, the cards (sorted by the next charge) and the first row's own pay button already say it (finding B2: no number twice). _Head figures:_ the standard head draws no label, so the figure is named beside it: Finanzen "Verfügbar · 3 Konten" (or "Kontostand · …" while nothing is deducted; red below zero, as the overview's figure was before) with one line below the month naming its parts; Abos the monthly sum of the main currency with the unit "/ Monat", the year and further currencies in one line; Budgets "Frei · 1 darüber"; context lines lead with what needs a decision ("2 überfällig · 7 Rechnungen") and carry no amount, so nothing is cut on the phone. The income figure of the overview is neutral ink (green stays with the "+" amounts of the list). _Master–detail:_ Finanzen ("Buchungen") and Rechnungen are `layout: 'split'`: on a wide page a row shows the entry in `DetailPanel` (`ItemRow current`; Rechnungen shows the first row from the start, Finanzen the booking that was picked, because its list is searched and filtered inside the tab), below that width the row opens the dialog; the panel never repeats a row's own button or badge (it holds "Bearbeiten"; paying, deleting and copying stay with the row and the dialog), and on wide pages "Bearbeiten" is also in the row's menu (below that width the row itself opens the dialog). The head figures carry no hidden label: the context line or the unit names them. Abos: the row's switch is neutral, and on the phone the row has none ("Pausieren" is in its menu). _Dialogs (B10):_ `FormActions` is the fixed foot of a sheet on the phone; `DayField`/`TimeField` write dates and times in the app's format (text that is no date blocks the form, it never saves the last readable prefix); a new booking shows amount, payee and category, the rest folds behind "Mehr Felder" with its current values beside the button ("Heute · Girokonto"). _Rejected:_ hiding the category instead of the date (uncategorised bookings would pile up unseen; a hidden date costs one visible click), a pay button in the detail panel (the same button twice on one screen), the envelopes as a signature block (a new entry in `signature-blocks.json` for a second copy of the cards). _Phone:_ `PageHeader besideContent` lets the "⋯" menu sit at the right edge of the first row, so content starts at 218 px. Guard: `e2e/seed/money-area.seed.spec.ts`.
- **Net worth "Vermögen" (2026-11-11, module package G-b, `feat/ui4-g-b-net-worth`, only `finance`).** _Decision:_ net worth is the signed sum of all balances (`netWorth()` in `finance/netWorth.ts`, on top of `balances()`), per currency and split by the account's `kind`; it is the head's figure on the tab "Verlauf" ("Vermögen · 3 Konten", no red below zero), where the head showed "Verfügbar" beside the curve's own end value before. The tab's first line says what is still open as counts, invoices first ("Noch nicht abgezogen: 2 offene Rechnungen · 4 Abos bis Monatsende", so nobody reads the figure as already reduced: every open invoice whatever its due date, subscriptions with a charge until the month's end, behind the same two settings as "Verfügbar"); it wraps and carries no amount. Rows by kind show only when there is more than one kind or another currency (one row would repeat the figure, B2); another currency is one row "Konten in USD · nicht umgerechnet"; the foot of the curve names the last month without its amount. The balance widget's figure is the same number, so its context line names it ("Vermögen, davon … verfügbar") instead of printing it again; "Frei bis Monatsende" stays the liquidity figure of the other widget. Every new amount follows "Werte ausblenden", the amounts around the curve included (the line keeps its shape: a trend without a number). _No write:_ an account without `kind` reads as `checking` (`kindOf`); a migration would run on every device with a new HLC and a late device would overwrite a chosen kind by LWW. _Not in it:_ savings goals (they live in Budgets, which `finance` may not read, and lie on an account already: never added), a notion of debt, a kind `investment` (an older device could not parse it: own package with a version rule). Guard: `e2e/seed/net-worth.seed.spec.ts`. _Answered by the maintainer (2026-11-11, the four questions of #736, each as recommended):_ the widget keeps its name "Kontostand" (renaming it to "Vermögen" is a decision of its own: it changes the generated module tables); the counts stay the first line of the tab, not the head's context line (the head cuts a long line); the head's figure changes with the tab ("Verfügbar" on Übersicht, "Vermögen" on Verlauf, both named); savings goals stay out of the net worth (showing them needs Budgets data in `finance`: an own package that settles the module boundary first). _Design review after the merge (2026-11-11):_ the curve of the net worth is a line without a filled area, because its scale does not start at zero and a fill down to the lower edge said it did.
- **One verb for a page's main action: "<Ding> eintragen" (2026-11-11, answer to question 2 of the design check #750; scheduled 2026-11-12).** _Decision:_ the action that adds the page's thing is named "<Ding> eintragen" everywhere it shows: the head's button, the plus on the phone (already so since 0b-2), the dialog's title, the empty state's button, the dialog's save button; English "Add <thing>" stays one verb too. "Hinzufügen" stays where something joins an existing entry (a part payment, an attachment, a place, a calendar). _Why:_ the same act carried two verbs on one page (plus "Buchung eintragen", head "Rechnung hinzufügen", dialog "Buchung hinzufügen"; design check K5). _When:_ not page by page: changing only the money pages would leave four modules on "eintragen" and about fifteen on "hinzufügen". The texts change in one wording pass in the sweep 0b-8 ([PACKAGES](../design/ui-audit-2026-11/PACKAGES.md)); from now on a package names a new main action this way.
