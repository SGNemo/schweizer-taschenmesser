# Decisions – shell – Identity 4.0, area packages

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). Foundation (0b-1) and head/navigation (0b-2): [ui-shell-identity-v31](ui-shell-identity-v31.md).

- **Identity 4.0 money area (2026-11-11, package 0b-3, `feat/ui4-0b-3-money`; plan: [PACKAGES](../design/ui-audit-2026-11/PACKAGES.md)).** _Decision:_ Finanzen, Rechnungen, Abos and Budgets lie on tone steps (no frame, no shadow in `main`), their views are `Tabs`, and each page has one entry: the `PageAction` and a "⋯" menu that holds "Mehr Felder anzeigen" (`useAdvancedMenuItem`); no switch and no second button in the head. _What left the old signature slot:_ the account cards head the tab "Konten"; the three budget envelopes, the subscriptions ring with "Nächste Abbuchungen" and "Nächste bezahlen" are gone, because the cards, the list (sorted by the next charge) and the first row's own pay button already say it (finding B2: no number twice). _Head figures:_ Finanzen shows "Verfügbar" (the balance while nothing is deducted) and one line below the month names its parts; Abos shows the monthly sum of the main currency, the year and further currencies in one line; context lines lead with what needs a decision ("1 von 6 darüber", "2 überfällig · 7 Rechnungen") and carry no amount, so nothing is cut on the phone. _Master–detail:_ Finanzen ("Buchungen") and Rechnungen are `layout: 'split'`: on a wide page a row shows the entry in `DetailPanel` (the first entry from the start, `ItemRow current`), below that width the row opens the dialog; the panel never repeats a row's own button. _Dialogs (B10):_ `FormActions` is the fixed foot of a sheet on the phone; `DayField`/`TimeField` write dates and times in the app's format; a new booking shows amount, payee and category, the rest folds behind "Mehr Felder" with its current values beside the button ("Heute · Girokonto"). _Rejected:_ hiding the category instead of the date (uncategorised bookings would pile up unseen; a hidden date costs one visible click), a pay button in the detail panel (the same button twice on one screen), the envelopes as a signature block (a new entry in `signature-blocks.json` for a second copy of the cards). _Phone:_ `PageHeader besideContent` lets the "⋯" menu sit at the right edge of the first row, so content starts at 218 px. Guard: `e2e/seed/money-area.seed.spec.ts`.
