# Decisions – shell – Identity 4.0, area packages

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). Foundation (0b-1) and head/navigation (0b-2): [ui-shell-identity-v31](ui-shell-identity-v31.md).

- **Identity 4.0 money area (2026-11-11, package 0b-3, `feat/ui4-0b-3-money`; plan: [PACKAGES](../design/ui-audit-2026-11/PACKAGES.md)).** _Decision:_ Finanzen, Rechnungen, Abos and Budgets lie on tone steps (no frame, no shadow in `main`), their views are `Tabs`, and each page has one entry: the `PageAction` and a "⋯" menu that holds "Mehr Felder anzeigen" (`useAdvancedMenuItem`); no switch and no second button in the head. _What left the old signature slot:_ all of it is gone – the account cards (the tab "Konten" lists every account with its balance), the three budget envelopes, the subscriptions ring with "Nächste Abbuchungen" and "Nächste bezahlen" – because the lists, the cards (sorted by the next charge) and the first row's own pay button already say it (finding B2: no number twice). _Head figures:_ the standard head draws no label, so the figure is named beside it: Finanzen "Verfügbar · 3 Konten" (or "Kontostand · …" while nothing is deducted; red below zero, as the overview's figure was before) with one line below the month naming its parts; Abos the monthly sum of the main currency with the unit "/ Monat", the year and further currencies in one line; Budgets "Frei · 1 darüber"; context lines lead with what needs a decision ("2 überfällig · 7 Rechnungen") and carry no amount, so nothing is cut on the phone. The income figure of the overview is neutral ink (green stays with the "+" amounts of the list). _Master–detail:_ Finanzen ("Buchungen") and Rechnungen are `layout: 'split'`: on a wide page a row shows the entry in `DetailPanel` (`ItemRow current`; Rechnungen shows the first row from the start, Finanzen the booking that was picked, because its list is searched and filtered inside the tab), below that width the row opens the dialog; the panel never repeats a row's own button or badge (it holds "Bearbeiten"; paying, deleting and copying stay with the row and the dialog), and on wide pages "Bearbeiten" is also in the row's menu (below that width the row itself opens the dialog). The head figures carry no hidden label: the context line or the unit names them. Abos: the row's switch is neutral, and on the phone the row has none ("Pausieren" is in its menu). _Dialogs (B10):_ `FormActions` is the fixed foot of a sheet on the phone; `DayField`/`TimeField` write dates and times in the app's format (text that is no date blocks the form, it never saves the last readable prefix); a new booking shows amount, payee and category, the rest folds behind "Mehr Felder" with its current values beside the button ("Heute · Girokonto"). _Rejected:_ hiding the category instead of the date (uncategorised bookings would pile up unseen; a hidden date costs one visible click), a pay button in the detail panel (the same button twice on one screen), the envelopes as a signature block (a new entry in `signature-blocks.json` for a second copy of the cards). _Phone:_ `PageHeader besideContent` lets the "⋯" menu sit at the right edge of the first row, so content starts at 218 px. Guard: `e2e/seed/money-area.seed.spec.ts`.
- **Net worth "Vermögen" (2026-11-11, module package G-b, `feat/ui4-g-b-net-worth`, only `finance`).** _Decision:_ net worth is the signed sum of all balances (`netWorth()` in `finance/netWorth.ts`, on top of `balances()`), per currency and split by the account's `kind`; it is the head's figure on the tab "Verlauf" ("Vermögen · 3 Konten", no red below zero), where the head showed "Verfügbar" beside the curve's own end value before. The tab's first line says what is still open as counts, invoices first ("Noch nicht abgezogen: 2 offene Rechnungen · 4 Abos bis Monatsende", so nobody reads the figure as already reduced: every open invoice whatever its due date, subscriptions with a charge until the month's end, behind the same two settings as "Verfügbar"); it wraps and carries no amount. Rows by kind show only when there is more than one kind or another currency (one row would repeat the figure, B2); another currency is one row "Konten in USD · nicht umgerechnet"; the foot of the curve names the last month without its amount. The balance widget's figure is the same number, so its context line names it ("Vermögen, davon … verfügbar") instead of printing it again; "Frei bis Monatsende" stays the liquidity figure of the other widget. Every new amount follows "Werte ausblenden", the amounts around the curve included (the line keeps its shape: a trend without a number). _No write:_ an account without `kind` reads as `checking` (`kindOf`); a migration would run on every device with a new HLC and a late device would overwrite a chosen kind by LWW. _Not in it:_ savings goals (they live in Budgets, which `finance` may not read, and lie on an account already: never added), a notion of debt, a kind `investment` (an older device could not parse it: own package with a version rule). Guard: `e2e/seed/net-worth.seed.spec.ts`. _Answered by the maintainer (2026-11-11, the four questions of #736, each as recommended):_ the widget keeps its name "Kontostand" (renaming it to "Vermögen" is a decision of its own: it changes the generated module tables); the counts stay the first line of the tab, not the head's context line (the head cuts a long line); the head's figure changes with the tab ("Verfügbar" on Übersicht, "Vermögen" on Verlauf, both named); savings goals stay out of the net worth (showing them needs Budgets data in `finance`: an own package that settles the module boundary first). _Design review after the merge (2026-11-11):_ the curve of the net worth is a line without a filled area, because its scale does not start at zero and a fill down to the lower edge said it did.
- **Identity 4.0 plan area, first half: Kalender and ToDos (2026-11-11, package 0b-4a, `feat/ui4-0b-4-plan`; the package is split, 0b-4b brings Gewohnheiten, Zeiterfassung, Wetter, Karten, Personen, Planer, Uhr).** _Decision:_ both pages lie on tone steps (no frame, no shadow in `main`), their views are `Tabs`, each has one entry. _Kalender:_ the month is one raised surface, its cells are parted by hairlines at the top and start edge; a line is 24 px high (WCAG 2.5.8); chips, week cards, hour grid, agenda panel and year tiles lost their frames. _Colour only with meaning (B7):_ a mark is `--calm-ink` unless the entry belongs to a named calendar, then it is that calendar's colour; kind badges are neutral chips with the kind in words (a nearing deadline keeps its quiet warning, red only once overdue); an own appointment is told by weight, not by a tint; a month line names its kind and its calendar in words (tooltip and hidden text), so neither hangs on colour or on a tooltip alone. `.lineTime` stays on `--text-on-tint` (today's cell is a tint). _Views:_ "Kalender" and "Erinnerungen" are the tabs; month · week · day · agenda · year stays a `Segmented` in the toolbar beside the calendar chips, because the protected guard `calendar-views.seed.spec.ts` pins that row on the phone and a tab row of seven would scroll "Erinnerungen" out of sight. _Entry:_ "Neuer Termin" (reminders tab: "Erinnerung hinzufügen") is the head's action and the plus on the phone; the reminders tab lost its in-page button and its repeated heading; an empty day and an empty agenda offer one verb, a day with entries no second button. _Dialog (B10):_ a new appointment shows title, all-day, day, start, end; place, calendar, repeat, person, reminder, end day and note fold behind "Mehr Felder" with "Einmalig · Erinnerung: …" beside it; a remembered lead time (`focus.eventLead`) applies while folded; an existing entry shows everything; the foot is `FormActions` (delete, cancel, save), what else a saved entry can do is a row above it. _ToDos:_ no add field on the page: "Neue Aufgabe" opens a one-field dialog of the same name (the title reads date and effort as before), Enter saves and closes it; the count of today is the head's figure, named by its context line ("3", "heute · etwa 30 Min"; no figure and no zero on an empty day), the next task is the page's first row (on the phone without a card, so the page's "⋯" beside it does not read as the task's menu) with "Anfangen" (no longer filled: the head owns the one filled button) and "Später"; master–detail (`layout: 'split'`, `TaskDetail` in `DetailPanel`, `ItemRow current`; the first row is shown from the start, "Bearbeiten" is in the panel and in the row's menu; below the split width the row opens the dialog; the board has no panel and takes the page); "Auf heute" is the first entry of a row's menu for every task not due today; board columns, cards and tag chips lost frames, fills and tints; subtasks are rows of the same surface. _Rejected:_ the calendar's ranges as tabs (see above); marks drawn as SVG so the measurement does not count them (the count would lie); a second add row at the end of the ToDo list (the planner keeps its row, ToDos has the dialog); keeping the dialog open for the next task (Enter would no longer end the path; the plus is one tap away). _Not reached:_ "coloured above the fold ≤ 10" for the calendar's month: 94 → about 20 on the desktop, 82 → about 16 on the phone, all of it today's cell and the marks and chips of named calendars. _Shared blocks touched:_ `Tabs` fades its edge only while the row overflows; after "Mehr Felder" the focus moves to the first revealed field (`AdvancedFields`); a sheet keeps a focused field clear of its fixed foot (`scroll-padding`). _Reviews:_ three design passes (critique, copy, accessibility) and the council; what they found and what was declined stands in the PR text of #746. Guard: `e2e/seed/plan-area.seed.spec.ts` with its helper `planArea.ts` (0b-4b adds its pages in a new spec file). _Answered by the maintainer (2026-11-11):_ ToDos at the reading measure left too much of a wide window empty: it is master–detail instead (the list keeps short rows, the picked task fills the rest); the calendar keeps its calendar colours in the month, so "coloured above the fold ≤ 10" does not apply to it (0b-8 measures again); a new appointment keeps five visible fields, repeat stays behind "Mehr Felder" (who repeats often switches "Mehr Felder anzeigen" on); month lines on coarse pointers (24 px against the project's 44 px) get a small package of their own (the phone's marks layout on touch desktops and tablets).
- **One verb for a page's main action: "<Ding> eintragen" (2026-11-11, answer to question 2 of the design check #750; scheduled 2026-11-12).** _Decision:_ the action that adds the page's thing is named "<Ding> eintragen" everywhere it shows: the head's button, the plus on the phone (already so since 0b-2), the dialog's title, the empty state's button, the dialog's save button; English "Add <thing>" stays one verb too. "Hinzufügen" stays where something joins an existing entry (a part payment, an attachment, a place, a calendar). _Why:_ the same act carried two verbs on one page (plus "Buchung eintragen", head "Rechnung hinzufügen", dialog "Buchung hinzufügen"; design check K5). _When:_ not page by page: changing only the money pages would leave four modules on "eintragen" and about fifteen on "hinzufügen". The texts change in one wording pass in the sweep 0b-8 ([PACKAGES](../design/ui-audit-2026-11/PACKAGES.md)); from now on a package names a new main action this way.
- **Identity 4.0 household and vault (2026-11-12, package 0b-5, `feat/ui4-0b-5-household-vault`).** _Decision:_ Listen, Vorräte, Fitness, Unterlagen and Passwörter follow the money area: tone steps, `Tabs` for the views, one filled action, a "⋯" menu for everything occasional, content from the first screen of the phone. _Views:_ Listen – one tab per list; Vorräte – Alle, Läuft bald ab, Nachkaufen (they were a filter); Fitness – Training, Essen, Woche; Unterlagen – Alle and the three states with their counts (they were folder tabs in the old signature slot); categories of Unterlagen and groups of Passwörter are a `Segmented` filter under the search. _Entry (rule 4):_ the add field of Listen is the last row of the list (`?new=1` scrolls to it and focuses it); the head's action and the phone's plus stay "Neue Liste" (`mobile-layout.spec.ts` pins the plus); "Aus Vorlage", editing, resetting, copying and sharing are in the menu, and so are "Eintrag hinzufügen" (it scrolls to the field of a long list) and "Gekauftes entfernen", which also stays one tap under the list while something is in the basket; the page's first line says what the head's figure does not (sums, or what is in the basket; packed and the trip for a packing list), an empty list has none. Vorräte: "Mehr Felder anzeigen" is a menu entry (`useAdvancedMenuItem`). Fitness: "Wie letztes Mal" and the calorie goal are menu entries. _Passwörter:_ the entry is a plain button, never the page action of the other modules, so the phone's plus stays the plain capture there (`exclusion.test.ts`); generator, check, import and export, emergency sheet and the browser extension are the menu; "Sperren" stays in sight beside the state it changes, never inside a menu, and while the browser extension is connected the same line says so (it is a channel into the open vault). _Master–detail:_ Unterlagen is `layout: 'split'`: on a wide page a picked folder shows in `DetailPanel` (none before a pick: provider, dates and note are not shown to whoever looks at the screen when the page opens, unlike Rechnungen), below that width it opens its dialog; the panel holds "Bearbeiten", renewing and downloading stay the folder's own buttons. _Signature blocks:_ `vault-doc` and `pantry-label` keep their hairline; the amount stepper inside a label lost its frame, so `pantry-label.framedInside` is gone from `signature-blocks.json`. _What left the old signature slot:_ the shelf, the receipt, the suitcase (its text is the page's first line, `ListSummary`) and the state tabs; the week plates moved into the tab "Woche"; the door of Passwörter stays, with the state and the lock button. _Rejected:_ the lock button inside the menu (two clicks to lock), a page action on Passwörter (breaks the exclusion), removing the "Jetzt" row of Vorräte (`now-strip.spec.ts` promises it). Guard: `e2e/seed/household-vault.seed.spec.ts`.
