# Decisions – planner rethink („a plan that stays put“)

Part of [plan](plan.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). Concept: [design/planner-rethink](../design/planner-rethink/README.md). Supersedes the run and engine rules of [plan-contract](plan-contract.md) and „Verschieben … is the one place that nails“ in [plan-hub](plan-hub.md) as the packages land.

- **The plan is laid, not computed (2026-11-21, maintainer's brief + answers).** Nothing changes its day without an action that names the thing or its day; measured before: 5 of 10 hand-laid things on another day after two missed days, 17 taps to repair (`web/e2e/usecases/planner-rethink.spec.ts`). The engine becomes a proposer behind buttons with a preview. Why: the automaton's only stable state was „everything pinned“.
- **One row holds day and lock (2026-11-21, council).** The placement (`planner_assignment`) carries `date`, `order`, `locked`; states (no day, laid, fest, missed) are derived at read time, „proposed“ is never stored. Why: a lock date in a second row could disagree with the day after a sync merge.
- **Missed things live in a tab „Noch offen“ (2026-11-21, maintainer's own answer).** Not a group on today's sheet as proposed. Things that were fest land there too, marked and first. A date passing is not a write.
- **Sources with a deadline only get a proposal chip under „Noch ohne Tag“ (2026-11-21, answer 2);** a dated turn (training, routine) shows on its day; a date set in ToDos shows as a muted row „In ToDos: Do“ that one tap lays. No background adoption.
- **One week with paging is the standard view, „4 Wochen“ a switch; direction A „Blatt und Band“ (2026-11-21, answers 3 and 4).** Why: stimulus budget and the same surface on the phone ([CONCEPT-DIRECTIONS](../design/planner-rethink/CONCEPT-DIRECTIONS.md)).
- **Words (2026-11-21):** the lock is „fest“ (not „gesperrt“), the tab „Noch offen“ (not „Verpasst“), an overbooked day „2 h 10 mehr als planbar“; fixed blocks show their time only ([CONCEPT-COPY](../design/planner-rethink/CONCEPT-COPY.md)).
- **Rows are hidden, never tidied (2026-11-21, council).** A row disappears only by „Vom Tag nehmen“ / „Nicht planen“ on that thing; a finished thing's row is hidden by one selector. Why: a failing or switched-off source must never cost a placement.
- **Migration runs once per device (2026-11-21, council):** rows stay; a pin equal to its row becomes `locked`; a pin of today or later moves the thing there, locked; a passed pin is dropped; `autoReplan` is set off once (stops old-version devices' background runs) and a restore clears the flag. Known limit: an old-version device still reshuffles on its own actions – both devices get the release together.
- **Package 2: lock on the row, chips derived (2026-11-22, autonomous chat, council).** The lock is one tap on the row (on the phone only on rows that are „fest“ – the phone bound of `kopf-leer.spec.ts` D holds, locking an open row there stays in ⋯); lock words lock only next to a day. Chips „Vorschlag“ come from one dry `plan()` over all deadline things, „In ToDos“ rows from the ToDo's own date – both computed at read time, never stored; a dated turn (`PlannableItem.day`) shows on its day without a row. „Rest verteilen“ of an overbooked day hands over that day's unlocked things, nothing before that day.
- **Package 3: „Noch offen“ in one move (2026-11-22, autonomous chat, review loop).** The tab's rows carry their steps (tick, lock, move button, no ⋯); „Alle auf heute“, „Verteilen …“ (preview, today first, fixed things named) and „Auswählen“ with a day group's „Alle“ sit above the groups. Measured: U1 repair 16 → 9 taps in one pass, „Alle auf heute“ 1 tap. The tab shows a dot and names the days in its accessible name; today's line can be switched off („Fokus & Aufmerksamkeit“).
- **Package 4: more than a week (2026-11-22, autonomous chat, review loop).** The band stays a rolling week from today (date range, not „KW“) and pages forward only; „4 Wochen“ starts on this week's Monday so passed days say „offen“ and lead to the tab. The board reads 28 days; the stretch in view is built from the same reads, sources up to its last day, `horizonDays` only for proposals. Measured: U5 possible in 3 taps (day visible in the planner); board + grid with 200 placements 3.8 ms.
- **Package 5: moving by drag, menu, keyboard, selection (2026-11-22, autonomous chat, review loop).** Own pointer gesture instead of HTML drag-and-drop (one code path for mouse and touch, no new handle: the whole row drags, its controls keep their taps); a source's action word („Bezahlen“) stays ahead of „Verschieben …“ in ⋯ (K1b). No reordering inside a day by dragging (it would need a handle). Measured: U1 laying the week 31 → 11 taps.
- **Package 6: proposals (2026-11-22, autonomous chat, council).** „Woche vorschlagen“ replaces the menu's „Neu verteilen“ and stands at „Noch ohne Tag“ (on the phone inside the open group – the stimulus bound of `kopf-leer.spec.ts` D). Chips without a deadline come from a second pass after the deadline things (the deadline days lie when it runs), so the setting can never change a deadline chip. „In Teile aufteilen …“ is a plan op stored on the item (`splittable`), one undo with the proposal. Counter-scenarios as e2e: first morning ≤ 7 taps, „Kopf leer“ +2, reminder chain +1.
- **Package 7: measured against the target (2026-11-22, autonomous chat, review loop).** The measuring script asserts `TARGET` on the desktop and the phone: every target reached, the phone's lock is 2 taps (its control shows only on rows that are „fest“ – stimulus bound). Hidden rows of done things: 516 B per planner row (rows only – without the sync log and the source's own row), ≈ 1.8 MB a year at 10 a day – no clean-up built; one that asks the source about exact ids stays a possible package. The round's working documents (packages, hand-off) were removed; decisions stay here. The mockups stay: the page still looks like the old planner, and a follow-up package brings it closer to direction A.
- **Look of direction A (2026-11-23, the maintainer: „nah an den Mockup“, rules may follow).** The band is the calendar week from Monday (passed days „offen“, „KW 47“, ‹ › at its ends, „Woche | 4 Wochen“ on the right); on the weekend it starts with today. One card per day: the open line on top, rows on one line (no source word), „fest“ as a pill, „Noch ohne Tag“ inside the card. Rules changed for it: an overbooked day is a warm box (was: no tone); the tick is a circle. Kept from the rules: on the phone this week's week view has no band row (the field stays whole on arrival, planner-hub M-3) – „4 Wochen“ in ⋯ there.
- **ToDos show the plan state (2026-11-23, autonomous chat, review loop).** The mark comes from the ToDo's own fields (`plannedFor` + `planMark` agree), so a field-LWW mix reads „noch nicht im Plan“; „fest“ and „Nicht planen“ are read from the planner's rows in core (`planFacts`, like `isRequested`), no new contract. Subtasks carry no mark (only the main task is a plan thing). „In den Plan …“ from ToDos is `plan.requested` with `refs` + `day` (`null` = „Noch ohne Tag“; a named thing that lay elsewhere moves there, keeping its lock; „Nicht planen“ is lifted) – one undo, one toast. `?item=` brings the row into view in the planner.
- **„Plan einfügen“ (2026-11-23, use case „Tag kippt“, autonomous chat, council).** A finished plan from outside – text with day headings or the ToDos' JSON – is read locally (`modules/planner/pastePlan.ts`, no model) and applied as one undoable action; a block pasted into the planner's field opens the preview by itself, ⋯ has „Plan einfügen …“. The same title (case, spaces, quotes, trailing marks ignored) is the same thing, also when kept for later: it moves, nothing is doubled; things the paste does not name stay and are only listed. A passed day becomes „ohne Tag“ unless he picks „Auf heute“. „Nächste Wochen“ is the new optional source step `onLater`/`listLater` (ToDos: „Irgendwann“) – the planner never edits a ToDo itself. Measured: plan from outside 12 handles in two modules and 11 duplicates → 3 handles, 0 duplicates, also on the second paste (`web/e2e/usecases/tag-kippt.spec.ts` T4). Why title and not an id: a chat returns titles only; a JSON with ids would let a model address things it never saw.
- **„Nur das hier …“ and „Nächste Wochen“ (2026-11-23, use case „Tag kippt“, autonomous chat, review loop).** Shrinking a day is one move: in the page's ⋯ he ticks what **stays** (not what goes), then picks where the rest goes – „Morgen“, „Diese Woche, ohne Tag“ or „Nächste Wochen“; „fest“ things stay and are named; one undo. The first thing that stays becomes the day's start line („Anfangen mit: … · Fokus starten“, ✕ hides it; device-local, one per day). „Nächste Wochen“ is a folded group in the planner (source step `listLater`), back only by his tap („Diese Woche“). While choosing, the day shows all its rows. Measured: shrinking Saturday 9 handles (4 per thing) → 8 (0); 5 / 6 / 9 over the weekend 31 handles in two modules (23 per thing) → 21 in the planner (0). Rejected: a sentence as the door (fuzzy names, no target for „ohne Tag“), re-spreading the rest (that is „Rest verteilen“). Known limits: an undo keeps the start line (✕ hides it); a thing its source dates (training) stays on its day for „ohne Tag“ / „Nächste Wochen“.
- **„Kleiner machen …“ (2026-11-23, use case „Tag kippt“ P1, autonomous chat, review loop).** Row ⋯ → „Kleiner machen …“: the field „Heute nur“ and where the rest goes („Diese Woche, ohne Tag“ default, „Nächste Wochen“); one undo. The thing keeps its id, so its day, lock and order stay with the first step; the rest is a new thing of the old title. The titles are the source's: new optional step `onSplitOff(id, todayTitle)` (ToDos: rename, plus a new task with list, priority, estimate, deadline and its reminder, tags, links, person and the series – a tick on the step never turns next week's turn into the step; checklist, note and subtasks stay with the original). Measured: T1 one thing smaller in one move, 4 handles (was not possible).
- **Goal line of days (2026-11-23, use case „Tag kippt“ P2, autonomous chat, review loop).** „Ziel: Wohnung frei bekommen“ stands as one quiet line above the day's sheet – text only, no figure, no bar, not on the widget or in the calendar. It is the day's existing `note` (no new collection); „Plan einfügen“ takes the first „Ziel …:“ line onto the days the paste names (a ticked line in the preview, same undo), „Tag einstellen“ edits it per day, ✕ on the line removes it from every day in view that carries it.
- **„Plan als Text kopieren“ (2026-11-23, use case „Tag kippt“ P3, the maintainer's answer „ja“ to TARGET §5 question 1, autonomous chat, council).** The page's ⋯ copies the plan from today on to the clipboard – only on his tap, nothing is sent anywhere, no share target, no model. The text is exactly what „Plan einfügen“ reads (goal line, one heading per day, „Diese Woche, ohne Tag:“, „Nächste Wochen:“, `- title (30 Min)`), so the round trip through a chat needs no retyping; pasted back unchanged it changes nothing (measured, T5). It holds titles, days, minutes and the goal only – no ids, notes, links, blocker labels or appointments (`planAsText`, pinned by a unit test). The planner's `dataApi: false` and missing `aiSchema` stay: this is his copy, not a read path.
