# Decisions in full – Focus and attention aids – principles, defaults, settings, setup

Part of [focus](focus.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md).

- **Opt-out per aid, sensible defaults:** every aid (next-one-thing, day plan, calm attention strip, focus mode, time-to-next) is a setting in the core section "Fokus & Aufmerksamkeit", synced scope `focus`; device-local state (focus timer, skipped suggestions) lives in `_meta`. Alternative rejected: a single "focus mode" master switch (too coarse, hides what each aid does).
- **No pressure by design:** overdue ToDos read "Wartet noch" with no daily counter; red stays for money deadlines and exceeded budgets; "Neu planen" spreads ToDos over the next days with undo. Streaks (package 4) get rest days and never show a loss.
- **Order of work:** package 1 "Anfangen" (next-one-thing, day plan, calm strip, focus mode, estimates), 2 "Erinnerungen", 3 "Erfassen/Ruhe/Wiederfinden", 4 "Fortschritt"; one PR each. Details, mockups and scope: [../features/focus-aids.md](../features/focus-aids.md); rules: [../design/FOCUS-GUIDELINES.md](../design/FOCUS-GUIDELINES.md).
- **Doc budgets raised** (start context 2 600 instead of 2 000, STATUS 5 000, CHATS and PROMPT-TEMPLATES 2 000): small rule additions no longer force cuts elsewhere; the target stays ≈ 2 000 (`docs/meta/DOCS-GUIDE.md`).
- **Display aids stay device-local (2026-10-03):** text size, line spacing, in-app motion and the calm home view live in `localStorage` like the other display choices, so a phone and a desktop can differ; they are applied before first paint.
- **Calm defaults for new installations (2026-10-14, focus audit 2, package 1):** the home view starts as "Nur das Wichtigste" (`HOME_VIEWS` order: calm first), `streaks`, `weekReview` and `moduleNow` are off by default. Why: the default home was ~20 widgets / 6598 px on the phone and every extra counter or repeated strip is stimulus without a diary-proven benefit. Nothing is removed; each is one switch away. An installation that already existed keeps its behaviour: at the first start of this version `core/focus/defaults.ts` writes the old values explicitly (only where nothing was stored) and sets the home view to "all" if none was chosen; a fresh app (no setup state, no data) is detected before `ensureSetupState` and gets the new defaults. Marker `_meta` `focus.defaultsEra`. Risk: a second device that syncs an account before the first one started the new version may briefly show the new defaults.
- **Focus settings: 14 visible rows → 6 + "Weitere Einstellungen" (2026-10-14):** the rarely changed ones (plan limit, timer length, sound, indicator, module strips, streaks, week review, search history) sit in a folded block; stored keys and search anchors are unchanged (a deep link opens the block first).
- **Second round of improvements before the week of use (2026-10-17):** five parallel chats (voice key, AI reminders, quiet ping, reminder repeat, focus package 2) run before the diary week, prompts in `docs/product/focus/13-parallel-prompts.md` (removed 2026-11-10, in the git history). Stimulus numbers are measured with `npm run measure:stimulus` before and after each package.
- **Darstellung = five folded groups (2026-10-19):** Thema, Farben, Schrift, Bewegung, Sonstiges; one line with the current choice, "Ändern" opens the pickers (`FoldedGroup.tsx`, native `<details>`; deep links open it). Nothing removed or changed; no dialogs. Round 4: Lesen, Fokus, Favoriten, Erinnerungen, Module alike; "Basis oder Erweitert" stays open. Module settings: one "Felder" line each.
- **Setup step „Fokus-Hilfen“ and toasts that wait (2026-11-08, Erstkontakt-Runde, demo-a):** `core.focus` (order 35, `since: 1`, so a finished setup is not reopened) is the third first-run question: five switches over existing settings (motion `reduce`; `todoDigest` + `staggered` off; `streaks` + `weekReview` off; new device-local `tm-toast-stay`; reading aid offered, not preselected), one complete sentence each, preselected „Ruhig“, written only on „Weiter“ and only what changed (`core/setup/focusAids.ts`). No toast hides itself while the pointer or the keyboard focus is in it; `toastStay` removes the clock. Why: people who take texts literally or read slowly lost „Rückgängig“; the switches existed but were spread over four settings pages. Removed in exchange: the reading-aid offer on the welcome card (one question at a time).
- **Erstkontakt person A – Sven's answers (2026-11-08, all four recommendations):** (1) the Dev-Preview no longer fills an empty app at start-up; the welcome card offers „Mit Testdaten ansehen“ next to „Einrichtung starten“ (`layout/setup/TestDataOffer.tsx`, dev-only via `layout/devTools.ts`; a tap fills even after „Alles zurücksetzen“, never over real data); (2) „Fokus-Hilfen“ stays question 3 of 4; (3) the planner stays off by default and in no profile; (4) „Hinweise bleiben stehen“ stays off by default, the hold while touched/focused applies to everyone.
- **Accents and decisions (2026-11-14, `docs/accents-decisions`; 0b-8 follow-up 11, M4 + „Fewer decisions“; status: proposal, waiting for the maintainer's answers in the PR).** _Measured_ on `develop` 68eb5ba (seeded and empty Dev-Preview, 1440 × 900 and 412 × 915, light): (1) **Schnellerfassung** shows one type chip per available target: 6 on a fresh install, 14 with every module (the audit's 16 = these 14 + „Satz eintragen“ + „Oder mit dem ganzen Formular“); on the phone the chip block is 200 px of 915 (four rows), 20 controls in the sheet; the parser already picks the type while one types („Zahnarzt morgen 10 Uhr“ → Termin), so the chips correct a guess, they are no required choice; one filled accent („Speichern“). (2) **Einrichtung „Schritt 13 von 13“ = Übersicht**: 33 switches (phone 28), all on, every one a filled accent track; the same pattern in step 3 „Profil und Module“ (29), step 5 Werkzeuge (14, 4 on) and step 4 Fokus-Hilfen (5); the Home „Karten“ sheet lists the same rows. design-rules already says a `Switch` that repeats in every row takes `tone="neutral"` (precedent: Bibliothek 0b-6). (3) **Übersicht am Handy**: two fills – the plus (y 846) and „Anfangen · 30 Min“ in „Jetzt dran“ (`NextWidget`, y 933, one scroll); empty: the plus and „Einrichtung starten“; desktop has one (the top bar's „Neu“ is neutral). _Proposal (recommendation first):_ (1) at most six type chips – the picked one, then the order of `available` – and „Mehr …“ unfolds the rest in place (Tab still walks all); a fresh install sees no change; „Satz eintragen“ and the form fold stay as they are (quiet, no fill); alternatives: chips only after typing, or as today. (2) `tone="neutral"` for the repeated switches in steps 3, 4, 5, 13 and the „Karten“ sheet (rule fix, no new rule); settings pages stay a follow-up of their own. (3) below 900 px no filled button in the overview content – „Anfangen“, „Weiter dort“ and „Einrichtung starten“ become `secondary` (same place and words), the plus stays the one accent as on module pages; desktop unchanged; alternatives: `secondary` on every device, or keep both and name „Jetzt dran“ an exception. FOCUS checklist: less stimulus, no new switch, the next step keeps its place and words.
