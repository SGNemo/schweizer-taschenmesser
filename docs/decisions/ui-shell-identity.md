# Decisions – shell – module identity v1–v3

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md).

## Module identity (2026-11-02, PR `feat/module-identity`)

- **Chosen look: "Plättchen" plus edge.** The maintainer picked variant A (icon chip in the module hue) and added the 4 px left edge of variant C: both on widget cards, the module frame (`AreaFrame`) and the calendar's today column; the active navigation icon takes the hue, the text keeps the accent. Area colours stay only on group heads. No tinted header bands, no per-module motifs.
- **Contract** (superseded by v3.1 below: `widgetType` dropped): `ModuleManifest.identity: { hue, widgetType }` is required (`validateManifest`, `check:modules`, generator template). `hue` comes from the fixed ten-hue palette `--mod-*` (light + dark; ≥ 3:1 on the surfaces, ≥ 25° of hue away from danger/accent/warning/success so a module colour can never read as a state; slate is the grey). `widgetType` names the catalogue base type of the main widget. No two modules of one area share a hue (registry test).
- **Application through tokens only:** `data-hue` on a widget card, a module page (`PageContainer`) and a nav item sets `--module-color`; inside it the first chart series follows the module. Semantic colours keep priority: urgency stripes and badges are unchanged and sit inside the card, the edge sits on its border.
- **Off switch:** Einstellungen → Darstellung → Farben → "Modulfarben" (`tm-module-colors`, `data-module-colors='off'`) and the "Ruhig" mode both turn every hue into `--text-3`; chip, edge and icon stay, so the module is still recognisable without colour.
- **Cockpit figures from modules:** `contributions.headline` (one formatted figure per module; finance = balance) feeds the cockpit's "Großanzeige" with the module's chip and edge.

## Module identity v2 (2026-11-03, PR `feat/module-shell-identity`)

- **Supersedes v1's "no tinted header bands, no per-module motifs".** The maintainer picked variant **B** of three (A subtle, B pronounced, C between; mockups were in `docs/design/identity-v2/`, removed 2026-11-10, git history): a tinted head with a gradient in the module hue, the content stays neutral. v1's chip and edge remain the fallback for the "Ruhig" mode.
- **One frame for every module** (`layout/AreaFrame.tsx`): the head carries chip, area, tabs, the page's h1 (`PageHeader`/`PageTitle` portal into it; no module has an own head), headline figure or signature and the gear; slots `notice`, `backdrop`, `figure`, `extra` (`@/ui/identity` `HeroSlot`). Dialogs only for short tasks. On the phone the head is sticky and folds to one line after 96 px (`core/shell/heroCollapse.ts`).
- **Contract:** `identity` gains required `head` (`figure|cover|compact`), `surface`, `signature` and optional `hue2`, `type`, `shape`, `feedback`; validated by `validateManifest` + `check:modules`. The empty-state motif is the signature in grey (no own field). Hues stay ten (the planned extra hues were not needed: weather = indigo, music = orchid).
- **Colour rules:** text on a tint is always `--text` (AA for every hue and theme, `tokens.test.ts`); the tint is 40 % → 10 % of the hue over `--surface` (`--head-tint-*`); "Ruhig" and "Modulfarben: Aus" flatten it to 12 % / 5 % and grey the hue; a dynamic colour (cover) goes through `safeTint` at run time; the sky has its own tokens and a night ink.
- **Navigation:** the active sidebar entry is tinted in the hue (icon in the hue, text `--text`), a 3 px stripe in the hue runs along the sidebar edge; widgets get a tinted head band (65 % of the head tint) with the icon chip.
- **Weather and music are modules** (`weather` in Planen, `music` in Wissen), no longer features of the overview: the dialogs, the overview line "Wetter", the "Läuft gerade" card and the `core:nowplaying` switch are gone; the widgets show only today / what plays, the page has the rest. `music` is `preview` like its connector and off by default, but the connector (`ConnectorDef.enablesModule`) switches it on once when Spotify is first connected, never against an explicit choice (`enableModuleIfUnset`); `weather` is on by default. No collections, `seed.none: 'live-data'`, `dataApi: false`.
- **Weather hints** (`modules/weather/warnings.ts`) are computed from the forecast days (thunderstorm, gusts ≥ 70 km/h, ≥ 20 mm, ≥ 32 °C, ≤ −5 °C), not official warnings, and say so. **More places:** settings `weather.extra` (≤ 2, optional, older stored settings keep working), forecast of an extra place kept per session only.
- **Music without a device:** the head offers the devices ("Hier abspielen"), the Spotify app (`spotify:` link; in the desktop app the platform opens it through a scoped opener grant, PR `feat/spotify-app-link`; in browsers and on Android the OS handles it) and the web player (`open.spotify.com` through `openUrl`); Premium note only without "Steuern".

## Module identity v3 (2026-11-03, PR #414, branch `feat/identity-v3-foundation`)

- **Chosen after two rounds (diagnosis, concept with mockups A/B/C):** variant **D** = C (full-bleed tinted head with the area pattern, tinted sidebar groups, solid icon plates) with the widgets of B (white body, tinted head zone, tinted plate). Rules and the module mapping: `docs/design/MODULE-IDENTITY.md` (v3, built in round 3; the concept stays in `docs/design/identity-v3/`).
- **Supersedes v2** where they differ: no identity stripes anywhere (accent only as plate, tinted head, hairline, surface tint, filled chip), area tabs as a sub-navigation under the head instead of inside it, two head heights by variant (nine variants), one frame width per area with container-query breakpoints and an overflow E2E, palette v3 (ten hues ≥ 25° apart, also between hues), widget shapes bound to the head variant with an adjacency rule, sidebar v3 (favourites bar, capsule, no stars), thin overlay scrollbars with fade masks in every scroll area.
- **Built as decided, with four deviations:** (1) master–detail opens from **56 rem** of measured page container, not 72 rem (`ui/pageWidth.ts`, `useSplitView`), so 1280–1440 px laptops keep the panel; (2) the area pattern is not a manifest field, it follows `manifest.area` (`data-area`); (3) the sidebar list is the only custom scroller (`ui/OverlayScroll`), every other scroll area gets thin scrollbars that show on hover/scroll (native scrollbars cannot be overlays in CSS); (4) seven head variants changed from the first mapping so neighbours in an area never share one (enforced by `check:modules`).
- **Guards:** `ui/noStripes.test.ts` (no coloured one-sided edge or thin bar anywhere in the CSS), `check:modules` (all fields, hue distance ≥ 25° inside an area, neighbours differ in head variant), `e2e/seed/layout-overflow.seed.spec.ts` (no horizontal overflow, centred page, wrapping sub-navigation, six viewports × both themes × every module).
- **Overview:** the greeting is by the hour with no name; the weather short line shows only with the weather module on and a place chosen (no new request, same hook as the widget). Default widget order avoids neighbouring cards of different modules with the same shape (`spreadVariants`; dropped in v3.1, the manifest rules keep shapes apart); saved positions and the front widgets never move.
- **Existing modules** got the v3 fields in their manifests only; rebuilding their pages around their head and widget variant is each module chat's job (list in `scribble/feat-identity-v3-foundation.md`).
- **Identity v3 pages, plan + money (2026-11-04, `feat/identity-v3-planen-geld`):** calendar entries are capsules in the calendar colour with the kind as an icon; ToDos get a focus head (count box, next task, "Anfangen"), round ticks and priority icons; habits a progress figure and a ring empty state; time tracking an hour strip with Start/Stopp in the head; maps a shelf head with category counts; subscriptions a month-cycle ring and tiles; budgets envelope cards with fill levels. In-module view switches live in the head (`PageHeader views`). Missing signature parts (round tick, ring empty state, envelope) are module-local; lifting them to `ui/identity` is a proposal. ToDos keeps its own 44 px chip bar: the shared `Segmented` is 32 px tall.
