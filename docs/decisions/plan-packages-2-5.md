# Decisions – Day and week planning – packages 2–5 and follow-ups

Part of [plan](plan.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md).

- **One field for entering and saying; one chip per day (2026-11-05, package 2):** the planner page keeps one field: German sentences („Donnerstag frei“, „schieb Steuer auf Fr“, „alles von Do nach Fr“) are read first by stage 0 (`core/plan/instructions.ts`, quick-capture date/end-time extractors, `pickTarget`/`withinOneEdit`), everything else stays a ToDo; a sentence never writes directly – it ends in `PlanPreview` (moved with cause, new, waits) and „Übernehmen“ is one `undoable`. Two equal matches ask, a planning verb with an unreadable rest explains, a thing is named without a verb only word for word („Müll raus“ stays a ToDo). Other languages keep „Eintragen“ and the ⋯ actions. The calendar gets one all-day chip per planned day (never one per session), switchable; the „Heute“ card, the agenda block and the head line skip it. Quick capture `p`/„plan:“ saves in two stages (ToDo through the `todo` target, then `planner_item` `todos:<id>` through the manifest repo; undo removes both). The lists' „Wochenplanung“ routine becomes a link to „Woche prüfen“ while the planner is on (focus checklist 9).
- **More sources without write-back (2026-11-06, package 3):** invoices (open, due within the horizon: „<payee> bezahlen“, 10 min, prio 2) and subscriptions (next cancellation deadline via `cancelDeadlinesInRange`/`core/deadline/notice.ts`: „<Abo> kündigen“, 15 min, prio 3) are `plannable` sources with a hard due and **no** `onDone`/`onAssign`/`onEstimate` – paying and cancelling stay money actions in their modules, a paid or cancelled thing simply leaves the list; the board hides tick, „Später“ and the deadline actions for them; one switch `showInPlan` per source (default on); no new fields on invoices or subscriptions. Notes put things in through `plan.requested` (the quick-capture target `plan` with an additive `CaptureFields.link`). The overload line is the second line of the run's one toast (the toast store keeps one message; a toast of its own would push the undo away; `.toastText` keeps line breaks); the week of the last line is device-local `_meta` `planner.overload.toastWeek`; `planner.overloaded` is reported by a detector from the stored last run, key per ISO week.
- **Heute follows the plan, nothing disappears (2026-11-06, sweep N02):** while the planner is on, a later day it gave a ToDo (`planMark.date === plannedFor`) wins over an older due date – the ToDo page (list, board, head count, „Als Nächstes“) and the „Heute“ card's „Jetzt dran“ treat it as „Demnächst“ until that day (`viewOf(…, planner)`, `isLaterInPlan`); a user's own date and an overdue thing the planner has not placed keep the plain rule, so the counts can differ by the unplaced ones; in „Alle“ and „Demnächst“ such a thing stays grouped by its due date („Wartet noch“, with „im Plan: Tag“ in Demnächst). „Alle auf heute“ steps back like „Neu planen“. Board: seven columns only from 95rem of page width (a column keeps ~210 px; titles never break inside a word).
- **Stage 1 in the planner's field with its own prompt (2026-11-06, package 4):** `core/ai/local/prompts/plan.ts` is the planner's own prompt and grammar – a static system part (ops, rules, examples; the KV cache keeps it) and a user part with today, a 14-day day list, the compact plan (≤ 30 things: `k<n>`, title, minutes, priority, hard due, first day; waiting things last; never blocker labels or day notes) and the sentence; the GBNF grammar allows ≤ 8 ops of six kinds (`pin`, `exclude`, `priority`, `duration`, `available`, `block`) with keys enumerated from the plan, every repetition bounded. `modules/planner/localStage.ts` walks the steps of `core/ai/local/stage.ts` itself (`findModelByFile`, `ensureLoaded`, `formatChat`, `getPlatform().localModel.generate`), never `aiModules()`, `writableModules`, `core/ai/write`, the router or `fetch`. Order: the rules first (0 tokens, also with „KI aus“); the model only for a sentence the rules could not read (`explain`/`missing`, or `none` while it names a thing of the plan word for word), only with `(await isAiOn()) && localStageReady()`; an empty, invalid or partly invalid answer (one bad key, date or range spoils the whole answer – nothing is guessed) leaves the rules' result standing; a non-empty answer ends in the same `PlanPreview`, headed by „Vom lokalen Modell gelesen – bitte prüfen.“; „Übernehmen“ applies the ops through `applyOps` in one `undoable`. The field waits with one calm line while the model reads („Einen Moment – das lokale Modell liest den Satz.“); Android and the PWA never show either line. Pins: `modules/planner/__tests__/privacy.test.ts` (1) „KI aus“ → rules only, 0 requests, `generate` never called; (2) the stage and the page never `fetch` (spy and sources); `core/ai/privacy.test.ts` (3) every adapter with cloud writes allowed and no local stage: a tool call naming `planner` → `unknown-module`, the body carries no `planner`, no fixture text, `schemaHash` and the prompt unchanged with the planner enabled; (4) a chat with planner data on the local engine and then every adapter through the router carries no fixture text, and the planner is never a context choice. ToDos: `estimateMin` is an AI field with the new parse role `estimate` (read by the quick-capture estimate extractor, now also number words: „zwei Stunden“), so „Steuer zwei Stunden bis Donnerstag“ in the bar keeps its estimate – the one sanctioned touch of `core/ai/write/rules/parse.ts` in this package (the role has to be consumed where roles are consumed; it changes the ToDo schema text, so cached cloud answers are keyed anew – expected). Rejected: asking the model for every ToDo sentence (seconds of waiting for the common case, and a ToDo could turn into an op), the model's keys as item ids (long, and an unknown id could be spelled), a `question` field in the answer (the rules' questions stay the only questions).
- **Windows are optional, minutes stay the truth (2026-11-06, package 5):** a day's window (`day.from/to`, else the weekday text setting, '' = none) never changes capacity, minutes or order – `core/plan/windows.ts` only lays the day's sessions, in the engine's order, first-fit on a 5-minute grid with a 15-minute pause, around blockers that have `from`/`to` (stored ones and timed calendar items), whole and never shortened, without a clock (today too: the day as planned); what does not fit keeps no time and stays in the day chip. Times are stored on the assignment rows (same input → same rows on every device; hash and row signature include them only when set, so plans without windows hash as before); manifest v2 is a no-op step (optional fields only). Templates in „Woche prüfen“ only fill the sheet; „Passt so“ writes the shown days (Arbeitswoche hands them back to the settings), one sentence, one undo.
- **Folded waiting group, count with its calming sentence (2026-11-08, use case „Kopf leer“ Q4):** „Wartet auf freie Zeit“ is `defaultClosed`; its head reads „Wartet auf freie Zeit · <Anzahl> · Nichts davon muss heute sein.“, the overload sentence (hours) shows when the group is opened and in the once-a-week toast as before; the per-row reason moves behind „Warum hier?“; the head's detail line names today's first thing („Heute: … Das reicht für heute.“) or the next free day. Refines „Calm by construction“ (2026-11-05): the figure stays one tap away, honesty is kept. Rejected: count and overload sentence in the folded head (Q4 b) – the first screen would carry the load figure while the person is unloading.
