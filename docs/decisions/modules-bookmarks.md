# Decisions – modules and tools – Merkliste

Part of [modules](modules.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). Earlier bullets (link preview opt-in, `gift.requested`): [modules-household-knowledge](modules-household-knowledge.md). Plan of the round: [bookmarks-deep/PLAN](../design/bookmarks-deep/PLAN.md).

- 2026-11-15 **Role (`feat/bookmarks-purpose-p1`).** _Decision:_ the Merkliste holds what a person still wants to look at, read, buy, try out or visit; every Merkzettel has an end (done or gone), the tab "Lesezeichen" holds the few permanent links and says it does not replace the browser's. Words: Merkliste = module, Merkzettel = entry, Lesezeichen = permanent link. Kinds `buy` and `try` (`manifest.version` 3, no data change); the grouping is a stored setting (`groupBy`). _Not built:_ tag chips on the page (focus audit 2 keeps filters in the menu), an own collection entity (tags group already).
- 2026-11-15 **Capture (`feat/bookmarks-purpose-p2`).** _Decision:_ the same page counts as saved whatever its scheme, "www.", trailing slash, fragment or tracking parameters (`urlKey`, compare only, never stored); the editor says "Schon gemerkt" and never blocks, `bookmark.requested` skips it. The preview is fetched on an explicit act only: the row action "Vorschau von der Seite holen", or a shared link with the tick in the form (on by default, the person just came from that page); the setting "Linkvorschau laden" (off) still means "on every save". The quick capture reads the kind from the first word (`m film Dune`), German like its parser. _Left out:_ no duplicate check in the quick capture (its target builds the record without reading the table); a captured address keeps kind `link`.
- 2026-11-15 **Plan and widget (`feat/bookmarks-purpose-p3`).** _Decision:_ the Merkliste is a planner source (`contributions.plannable`) only for entries the person marked with "In den Plan" (`inPlan`, optional; `manifest.version` 4): no date, nothing hard, default minutes by kind (`PLAN_MINUTES`), an estimate edited in the plan is kept (`planMinutes`), ticked there = "Erledigt"; a whole list offered by itself would flood the plan. The off switch is "Aus dem Plan nehmen" per entry. The widget `recent` shows three things to pick (`pickNext`: in the plan, started, newest; never kind `link`) under "Als Nächstes" / from Friday "Fürs Wochenende", and no count of what waits. _Left out:_ a reminder per entry; seed rows with `inPlan` (the planner seed lists its sources by hand).
