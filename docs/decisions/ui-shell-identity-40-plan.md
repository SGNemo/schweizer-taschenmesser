# Decisions – shell – Identity 4.0, plan area

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). The other area packages: [ui-shell-identity-40-areas](ui-shell-identity-40-areas.md).

- **Identity 4.0 plan area, first half: Kalender and ToDos (2026-11-11, package 0b-4a, `feat/ui4-0b-4-plan`; the package is split, 0b-4b brings Gewohnheiten, Zeiterfassung, Wetter, Karten, Personen, Planer, Uhr).** _Decision:_ both pages lie on tone steps (no frame, no shadow in `main`), their views are `Tabs`, each has one entry. _Kalender:_ the month is one raised surface, its cells are parted by hairlines at the top and start edge; a line is 24 px high (WCAG 2.5.8); chips, week cards, hour grid, agenda panel and year tiles lost their frames. _Colour only with meaning (B7):_ a mark is `--calm-ink` unless the entry belongs to a named calendar, then it is that calendar's colour; kind badges are neutral chips with the kind in words (a nearing deadline keeps its quiet warning, red only once overdue); an own appointment is told by weight, not by a tint; a month line names its kind and its calendar in words (tooltip and hidden text), so neither hangs on colour or on a tooltip alone. `.lineTime` stays on `--text-on-tint` (today's cell is a tint). _Views:_ "Kalender" and "Erinnerungen" are the tabs; month · week · day · agenda · year stays a `Segmented` in the toolbar beside the calendar chips, because the protected guard `calendar-views.seed.spec.ts` pins that row on the phone and a tab row of seven would scroll "Erinnerungen" out of sight. _Entry:_ "Neuer Termin" (reminders tab: "Erinnerung hinzufügen") is the head's action and the plus on the phone; the reminders tab lost its in-page button and its repeated heading; an empty day and an empty agenda offer one verb, a day with entries no second button. _Dialog (B10):_ a new appointment shows title, all-day, day, start, end; place, calendar, repeat, person, reminder, end day and note fold behind "Mehr Felder" with "Einmalig · Erinnerung: …" beside it; a remembered lead time (`focus.eventLead`) applies while folded; an existing entry shows everything; the foot is `FormActions` (delete, cancel, save), what else a saved entry can do is a row above it. _ToDos:_ no add field on the page: "Neue Aufgabe" opens a one-field dialog of the same name (the title reads date and effort as before), Enter saves and closes it; the count of today is the head's figure, named by its context line ("3", "heute · etwa 30 Min"; no figure and no zero on an empty day), the next task is the page's first row (on the phone without a card, so the page's "⋯" beside it does not read as the task's menu) with "Anfangen" (no longer filled: the head owns the one filled button) and "Später"; master–detail (`layout: 'split'`, `TaskDetail` in `DetailPanel`, `ItemRow current`; the first row is shown from the start, "Bearbeiten" is in the panel and in the row's menu; below the split width the row opens the dialog; the board has no panel and takes the page); "Auf heute" is the first entry of a row's menu for every task not due today; board columns, cards and tag chips lost frames, fills and tints; subtasks are rows of the same surface. _Rejected:_ the calendar's ranges as tabs (see above); marks drawn as SVG so the measurement does not count them (the count would lie); a second add row at the end of the ToDo list (the planner keeps its row, ToDos has the dialog); keeping the dialog open for the next task (Enter would no longer end the path; the plus is one tap away). _Not reached:_ "coloured above the fold ≤ 10" for the calendar's month: 94 → about 20 on the desktop, 82 → about 16 on the phone, all of it today's cell and the marks and chips of named calendars. _Shared blocks touched:_ `Tabs` fades its edge only while the row overflows; after "Mehr Felder" the focus moves to the first revealed field (`AdvancedFields`); a sheet keeps a focused field clear of its fixed foot (`scroll-padding`). _Reviews:_ three design passes (critique, copy, accessibility) and the council; what they found and what was declined stands in the PR text of #746. Guard: `e2e/seed/plan-area.seed.spec.ts` with its helper `planArea.ts` (0b-4b adds its pages in a new spec file). _Answered by the maintainer (2026-11-11):_ ToDos at the reading measure left too much of a wide window empty: it is master–detail instead (the list keeps short rows, the picked task fills the rest); the calendar keeps its calendar colours in the month, so "coloured above the fold ≤ 10" does not apply to it (0b-8 measures again); a new appointment keeps five visible fields, repeat stays behind "Mehr Felder" (who repeats often switches "Mehr Felder anzeigen" on); month lines on coarse pointers (24 px against the project's 44 px) get a small package of their own (the phone's marks layout on touch desktops and tablets).
- **Identity 4.0 plan area, list pages: Personen, Gewohnheiten, Zeiterfassung, Uhr (2026-11-12, package 0b-4b part 1, `feat/ui4-0b-4b-plan`; part 2 brings Planer, Karten, Wetter).** _Asked first (the maintainer, 2026-11-12):_ Gewohnheiten and Zeiterfassung are master–detail, not the reading measure of PACKAGES; 0b-4b is two PRs with a stop. _Decision:_ the four pages lie on tone steps, their views are `Tabs`, each has one entry. _Personen:_ `layout: 'split'`: rows on a `ListSurface` (no tiles), the picked person in a `DetailPanel`, below the split width a person stays a page of its own; "Mehr Felder anzeigen" is in the "⋯" menu. _Gewohnheiten:_ today's goals are rows of one surface under the day's date; on a wide page the goal's name is the row's button and its last 30 days and weekdays lie in the panel (the year grid stays on "Verlauf": 117 coloured cells); the rings row under the head is gone. _Zeiterfassung:_ "Start" stays the page's one filled button (C29), so the head's entry "Zeit eintragen" is a `PageAction` that is not filled and the phone's plus is "Zeit eintragen"; the picked entry of today lies in the panel with its time, break, note and what its project took today. _Uhr:_ the places are rows of one surface. _Empty states:_ one sentence and one verb. _From the design check (heads-up 2026-11-12):_ day and time in the four dialogs are `DayField`/`TimeField`; the main action is "Person / Gewohnheit / Zeit eintragen" (head, dialog title, empty state; Uhr keeps "Ort hinzufügen", a place joins a list); in the narrow panel a gift's actions lie under its text (the text was 69 px wide). _From the design skills and the council:_ a row of "Offene Geschenke" leads to its person; a person's page below the split width keeps the tabs (they lead back); row surfaces carry no `overflow: hidden` (it cut the "⋯" menu); Zeiterfassung has one entry per view ("Projekt eintragen" on "Projekte"), its dialog folds break and end day behind "Mehr Felder", and a row beside the panel keeps an edit button (one click, key E); Gewohnheiten and Zeiterfassung have "Mehr Felder anzeigen" in the "⋯" menu. Guards: `e2e/seed/plan-area-lists.seed.spec.ts`, and the three pages in `money-narrow.seed.spec.ts`. K3 and K7 (weather stage) go to part 2.
- **Identity 4.0 plan area, last part: Planer, Karten & Orte, Wetter (2026-11-12, package 0b-4b part 2, `feat/ui4-0b-4b2-plan`).** _Answered (the maintainer, 2026-11-12, all as recommended):_ the panel of Gewohnheiten is open from the start; the lost focus when a `SplitView` panel opens or closes goes to 0b-8 (shared block); "Start" of Zeiterfassung stays at the dial's top edge on the phone. _Planer:_ `layout: 'split'`: on a wide page the week is a list of seven days (what is free, how full) and the picked day lies in the panel beside it; below the split width the strip of chips above the day stays. The seven-column board is gone (rows are whole again: no two-line titles, swipes everywhere). The inline field is the page's last row with the planner's answers right under it (rule 4); it is built with the board, once and at its place (no jump, no lost focus while the board is read), the week bars left the signature slot (each day's row carries its bar). Day cards and chips lie on tone steps: no shadow, no outline for today (the row says "Heute" in words), the picked day a step lower; a chosen chip and the current hour carry the shared chip's line besides the tone (B7). _Karten & Orte:_ views are `Tabs`; the pin plate and the category tiles left the signature slot (the category chips filter by the same categories); chips are tone steps without frames; "Ort / Route eintragen"; the empty list of routes has its one button. _Wetter (design check K3, K7):_ on the phone the stage with weather data is 314 px (was 394, limit 320): the long line of further values lies under the head there, sun and moon sit small in the free end of the title row (the module row no longer crosses them); the stage draws its default signature as the bare mark, without surface and ring, until the page fills the slot (the tile looked like a button); hour cards and the air card are tone steps, the current hour a step lower. Times in the day sheet are `TimeField`. _Rejected:_ keeping the board beside a day panel (two pictures of the same week). Guards: `e2e/seed/plan-area-rest.seed.spec.ts`; appended to the protected `head-height.seed.spec.ts` (the stage with a forecast) and `money-narrow.seed.spec.ts` (Planer).
