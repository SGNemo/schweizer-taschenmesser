# Decisions – shell – Identity 4.0, overview

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). Area packages: [ui-shell-identity-40-areas](ui-shell-identity-40-areas.md).

- **Identity 4.0 overview (2026-11-13, package 0b-7, `feat/ui4-0b-7-overview`; plan: [PACKAGES](../design/ui-audit-2026-11/PACKAGES.md)).** _One fold rule for "Jetzt wichtig":_ `danger` and entries with `due: 'today'` are cards, however many; everything else waits behind "Kann warten · n" (n = entries, not things), nothing is filled up, nothing is cut (`rankAttention` has no cap; `home/attentionFold.ts`). The tone does not say "today", so the contract carries it: `AttentionItem.due`, set by ToDos, Kalender, Rechnungen, Fitness, the `core:` entries and `vault:act-today` (a deadline that ends today, split from `vault:act`). `budgets:warn`, the two price notices and `vault:act` wear `warning` (accent = act today, DESIGN-SPEC § 4a). The folded line names at most two phrases; `AttentionItem.foldSummary` counts entries of one kind ("2 Budgets fast aufgebraucht"). With `focus.calmAttention` off every entry is a card, as before. _Nothing twice:_ `AttentionItem.coveredBy` names the blocks that list the thing themselves; `coveredIds` leaves the entry out while such a block is on the page (the agenda, or the "Heute" card where the agenda is not on the page). One rule decides which block lists what (`activeCovers`: the agenda when shown, else the "Heute" card when it lists something), read by the strip and by the card (`core/modules/overviewBlocks.ts`). It knows visibility only, not what a block cuts off its list (the named price). `todos:today` is not covered: the card lists planned ToDos, not every ToDo due today (deviation from the plan, council round 1). The weather stands in the head only while no weather card is on the page. _"Ruhig" = strip + "Heute" card + pins_ in one reading column (`--page-reading`); the agenda shows there only when pinned (`core:agenda` in `pinned`, switch in the card sheet), and the "Heute" card then lists the day's appointments instead (`core/modules/overviewBlocks.ts`: a widget reads which blocks are on the page), also when there is no ToDo at all. Older devices ignore the pin and keep the agenda in "Ruhig" (the synced layout keeps the key). _Werte ausblenden:_ amounts inside a line (an invoice's entry, a card's context, a new price) become dots on the overview (`core/focus/maskAmounts.ts`, the tray's pattern). _Surfaces:_ strip, day cards and widgets are tone steps; no shadow, no tinted widget head, no pattern or wash behind text; a card is as high as its content (no minimum height per size class). _One paint:_ strip, agenda and cards appear together once their data is read, and the cards of the first screen show once (`data-settling`, cap 1.5 s), so nothing pushes anything (CLS 0 on the seed). _Head:_ the greeting has the title's measure (`--text-h1`, 600); on the phone the tools are one row. _Empty states of the overview:_ motif, one sentence, one verb ("Module auswählen", "Termin eintragen"). _Rejected:_ a new tone for "not today" (three tones stay), the agenda's cut-off known to `home/` (it would import a module), keeping the minimum height while loading (cards would shrink after loading instead of growing). _Not in it:_ the wording pass over the widgets' own empty states and the entrance animations `pageIn`/`itemIn` (`ui/`, `layout/`) – both with 0b-8; the Budgets page's own "fast aufgebraucht" (design check G5). Guard: `e2e/seed/overview.seed.spec.ts`, `home/attentionFold.test.ts`.
- **Overview builds up from the top (2026-11-14, `docs/overview-build-up`; status: proposed, waits for the maintainer).** _Proposal (variant K):_ the head at once; "Jetzt wichtig" and the agenda as today (together, once read); then the cards in grid order in two steps: the first row of the grid (as many cards as it has columns) shows once those cards have their content, then every other card mounts at once, hidden, and each shows once it and every card before it in the grid have theirs (no motion, no placeholder). _Why nothing can shift:_ grid placement puts a card by the cards before it only, so a card that finishes later never moves one already shown. No stored heights: the same on the first visit, on phone and desktop, in "Ruhig", "Ausführlich" and the cockpit. _Measured_ (prototype, seed `medium`, CPU 4×, median of 5, desktop "Ausführlich"): first card 1.0 s instead of 2.2 s, done 2.4 s (today 2.1–2.4 s), CLS under throttling 0.006 (today up to 0.072). "Ruhig" and the phone show their first card at about 1.0 s already today. _Rejected:_ A, remembered heights (CLS up to 0.83 at 6× on the phone when the content differs from last time; the first visit stays as today); B, strip and agenda after the cards (first content 1.4–1.9 s instead of 0.8 s; "Jetzt wichtig" leaves the first screen); C, a fixed one-line strip and agenda (cuts what 0b-7 never cuts); D, cards of known height first (since 0b-7 no card has one, `effectiveSize` is a width); cards loading beside the blocks (the page is main-thread bound: "Jetzt wichtig" then comes at 2.1–2.6 s); batches of three (done 6 s). Numbers and sketches: the PR text.
