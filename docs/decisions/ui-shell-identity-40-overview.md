# Decisions – shell – Identity 4.0, overview

Part of [ui-shell](ui-shell.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). Area packages: [ui-shell-identity-40-areas](ui-shell-identity-40-areas.md).

- **Identity 4.0 overview (2026-11-13, package 0b-7, `feat/ui4-0b-7-overview`; plan: [PACKAGES](../design/ui-audit-2026-11/PACKAGES.md)).** _One fold rule for "Jetzt wichtig":_ `danger` and entries with `due: 'today'` are cards, however many; everything else waits behind "Kann warten · n" (n = entries, not things), nothing is filled up, nothing is cut (`rankAttention` has no cap; `home/attentionFold.ts`). The tone does not say "today", so the contract carries it: `AttentionItem.due`, set by ToDos, Kalender, Rechnungen, Fitness, the `core:` entries and `vault:act-today` (a deadline that ends today, split from `vault:act`). `budgets:warn`, the two price notices and `vault:act` wear `warning` (accent = act today, DESIGN-SPEC § 4a). The folded line names at most two phrases; `AttentionItem.foldSummary` counts entries of one kind ("2 Budgets fast aufgebraucht"). With `focus.calmAttention` off every entry is a card, as before. _Nothing twice:_ `AttentionItem.coveredBy` names the blocks that list the thing themselves; `coveredIds` leaves the entry out while such a block is on the page (agenda: both views when shown; "Heute" card: the calm view). It knows visibility only, not what a block cuts off its list (the named price). The weather stands in the head only while no weather card is on the page. _"Ruhig" = strip + "Heute" card + pins_ in one reading column (`--page-reading`); the agenda shows there only when pinned (`core:agenda` in `pinned`, switch in the card sheet), and the "Heute" card then lists the day's appointments instead (`core/modules/overviewBlocks.ts`: a widget reads which blocks are on the page), also when there is no ToDo at all. _Surfaces:_ strip, day cards and widgets are tone steps; no shadow, no tinted widget head, no pattern or wash behind text; a card is as high as its content (no minimum height per size class). _One paint:_ strip, agenda and cards appear together once their data is read, and the cards of the first screen show once (`data-settling`, cap 1.5 s), so nothing pushes anything (CLS 0 on the seed). _Head:_ the greeting has the title's measure (`--text-h1`, 600); on the phone the tools are one row. _Empty states of the overview:_ motif, one sentence, one verb ("Module auswählen", "Termin eintragen"). _Rejected:_ a new tone for "not today" (three tones stay), the agenda's cut-off known to `home/` (it would import a module), keeping the minimum height while loading (cards would shrink after loading instead of growing). _Not in it:_ the wording pass over the widgets' own empty states and the entrance animations `pageIn`/`itemIn` (`ui/`, `layout/`) – both with 0b-8; the Budgets page's own "fast aufgebraucht" (design check G5). Guard: `e2e/seed/overview.seed.spec.ts`, `home/attentionFold.test.ts`.
