# Decisions – Day and week planning – calendar chip, calm design, package 1

Part of [plan](plan.md). Index of areas: [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md).

- **Plan in the calendar = one chip per day plus a link, not a tab (2026-11-05, answer 2):** the planner contributes one summary `calendarItems` chip per day (`kind: 'plan'`, switchable), the „Heute“ card and the home agenda skip it, and the calendar's view switcher gets a „Plan“ entry that links to the planner. Rejected: a tab (modules never import each other), a sixth calendar view, one chip per assignment (crowds month cells, duplicates the card).
- **Calm by construction (2026-11-05, council rounds 1–4):** the board head carries one figure (today; „Heute ist frei.“ on a day with no time), the week lives in the widget („Bis So“ over the shown rows); rows show title and minutes, the reason sits behind „Warum hier?“; a toast names at most two moves plus „n weitere“; the waiting group is „Wartet auf freie Zeit“ (never „freier Tag“, which means a day off); a toast names at most two moves plus „n mehr“ under 68 characters per line; the field is „Eintragen“ in package 1 and „Eintragen oder sagen“ from package 2, German only, and one user-doc line says plan sentences go there, not into the global bar; the „Woche prüfen“ sheet is always reachable via ⋯, a quiet text link on Mondays from package 2 (switch `focus.weekCheck`, off by default, × dismisses per ISO week), never a dialog that opens by itself; the widget line is labelled with the last shown day; the overload figure lives in the head of the waiting group and is toasted only when it first goes above zero, at most once per week (switch) – no attention item, and the „Neu planen möglich“ hint there goes while the planner is on; no automation action and no `watch` on the planner (only the `planner.overloaded` event; `decisions/automation.md`: modules opt in to `create` only); „Neu planen“ of the ToDo page is hidden while the planner is enabled (it rewrites due dates); the widget uses `ProgressList` (`key`, `title`, `value`, `max`, `detail`) without `overText`; `formatMinutes` moves the timetrack duration helper into `core/i18n/format` and adds a compact form for boards; duration steps 5/15/30/60 plus free entry; learning from time tracking, energy profile, habit blocks and the week review are a later concept of their own.
- **Package 1 = the minimum to plan a week (2026-11-05, answer 4):** engine, contract, module with week board, ToDos source (plus the optional ToDo field `planMark`, the core `ignoreFields`, the `module.disabling` event and the `syncSettled()` helper), available time and blockers per day, write-back, widget, undo; starts after `feat/identity-v3-frame` is merged (hotspot `core/modules/types.ts`); packages 2–5 follow in sequence. Identity: `indigo`/`cobalt`, head `kennzahl`, widget `fortschritt` (week load), signature `weekBars`, `order: 15`, `maturity: 'preview'` until package 3; the 26° distance to cobalt and violet is re-checked after the frame merge.
- **Package 1 as built (2026-11-05):** rolling week from today on board, strip and widget; available minutes = three settings (weekday/Sa/So), per-date values in `day` rows; a background run skips while hash, row signature and the `item.earliest` copies are in step (source dates are not hashed); „Später“ on the board = `onAssign(id, tomorrow)` without order; excluded things end the waiting group; `PlannableItem.createdAt` is the age tie-break; `PlannableSource.onRelease` hands a date back on switch-off (also a past `prior` – the same rule as the ToDo side's clean-up), while `onAssign(id, undefined)` in a run keeps a past date off today's card; the data API drops `internal` fields instead of rejecting; `schema-history.json` lists released versions only. Council: `RunDeps.aborted` (a run that outlives the switch-off writes nothing more); planner undo entries carry a scope and are forgotten on `module.disabling`; the ToDo clean-up runs from a live query (stored planner state, gate, marks), so a late mark is cleaned without a restart; `syncSettled()` awaits the running round; a stale page's action ends in one toast (`PlannerOffError`); the seed's `afterSeed` runs the planner once and registers the rows it made for the todos seed's tasks (`ctx.seeded`; a person's own rows and marks stay theirs), the loader purges the outbox of registered rows, `beforeRemove`/`afterRemove` suspend and resume the runs around the removal; the board shows four days a row and seven from 73rem of page width (layout `full`), the minutes lead the meta line, so no row runs past a column; the gate waits for a running sync round only for background runs.
