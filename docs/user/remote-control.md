# Remote: control your PC from your phone

With the Android app you control your Windows PC from the sofa: playback and volume and, if you choose, the mouse pointer and the keyboard. It works only at home on the same Wi-Fi, and only for a phone you allowed on the PC.

The remote is still work in progress. To see it, turn on _Settings → Appearance → Show work in progress_ **on the PC and on the phone**.

## What you need

- Nemo on a Windows PC and on an Android phone.
- Both are paired on your home network: [Sync → On your home network with your PC](sync.md#on-your-home-network-with-your-pc-no-server-needed).
- Nemo runs on the PC, and phone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi.

## Switching it on

1. **PC:** turn on _Settings → Connections → Remote control → Allow remote control_.
2. **PC:** under _Paired devices_ choose what the phone may do (see below). Windows asks once more in a window of its own.
3. **Phone:** turn on _Remote_ in the module library and open it.

Without steps 1 and 2 no device can control your PC. Pairing alone is not enough.

## What the phone may do

| Chosen on the PC          | What works on the phone                                                                                           |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nothing                   | nothing                                                                                                           |
| Media and volume          | play / pause, next and previous track, louder, quieter, mute, lock the PC                                         |
| Media, mouse and keyboard | also the views _Touchpad_ (pointer, clicks, scrolling) and _Keyboard_ (text and single keys such as Enter or Esc) |

On the PC, next to the device, you can set the phone to ask for fingerprint, face or PIN before mouse and keyboard.

While the PC is locked, only media and volume work. The phone never sees your screen, your clipboard or your files.

## On the phone

- **Media:** big buttons for playback and volume. If you like, the phone's volume keys control the PC too (a setting of the module).
- **Touchpad:** swiping moves the pointer, a tap clicks, two fingers scroll.
- **Keyboard:** type text on the phone, read it, then send it. The text is stored nowhere.
- **On the overview:** the card _Remote_ has one button, _Play / Pause_. One tap connects and sends. If connecting takes longer, the card says so; once it is connected, tap once more. The card never connects by itself.

## Ending it and switching it on again

- The PC always shows when a device is in control: at the top of the window and at the icon next to the clock.
- **End it at once:** _End remote control_ there. The phone is paused afterwards and cannot control anything.
- **Switch it on again:** on the PC under _Settings → Connections → Remote control_, _Switch on again_ next to the device.
- **Off altogether:** turn off _Allow remote control_. Home-network sync keeps running unchanged.

## Good to know

- Away from home or on someone else's Wi-Fi the remote does not work. That is on purpose.
- Programs that run with administrator rights take no input from the remote. Windows prevents that.
- If the phone says "Your PC cannot be reached right now": is Nemo running on the PC, and are both devices on the same Wi-Fi?
- Your phone's keyboard learns the words you type. For a password use its incognito mode.
