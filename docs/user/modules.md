# Modules and tools

## Overview

In the **module library** you turn modules on and off (when turning one off: keep or delete its data).

| On by default | Optional (off, enable in the library) |
|---|---|
| Calendar (with reminders and notification before events), To-dos (also recurring, with "Someday"), Finances, Invoices, Subscriptions | **Saved** (links, reading, watching, places, ideas with tags; on the phone via "Share"), **Notes** (with a pinned **scratchpad** at the top for quick jots), **Lists** (shopping with "2 milk", packing lists with progress and templates, checklists), **People** (birthdays yearly in the calendar, with age, reminder and WhatsApp greeting; gift ideas per person with price, link and status), **Budgets & savings goals** (monthly limits per finance category, savings goals with deposits), **Documents** (IDs, contracts, insurance, warranties with end date, notice period in the calendar + reminder, and a file), **Bookmarks** (a view of Saved: tiles grouped by first tag, e.g. parcel tracking, trains, maps …), **Accounts** (password vault) |

- **Documents:** title, category, provider, start, end, notice period and note are synced like everything else; the **files stay only on the
  device** where they were added (not synced, not in the backup, max. 10 MB per file).
- **Share (Android, installed as a PWA):** "Nemo" appears in the share menu; the "Share" page asks where the content should go
  (Saved, note or to-do; only enabled modules are offered). The native Android app (APK) does not have the share target yet.
- **Tools:** the "Tools" button at the top (or **Ctrl+.**) opens the calculator (with percent/VAT and split modes), currency, timer and QR code;
  more (units, date calculator, time zones, dice, text, images, PDF and **Developer** with Base64/URL, JSON, UUID, hash) can be turned on
  under "Manage tools". In the command palette (Ctrl+K) a calculation like "240 + 19 %" is computed right away, "Tool: …" opens a
  tool. The former scratch note now lives as the **scratchpad** at the top of Notes (your old text is carried over once).
- **This PC** (Windows app only): analyse and clean up drives; the "System" tab shows processor, memory, battery, graphics and network.
- **Retired (0.4.0):** Messages, habit tracker and time tracking are no longer visible. Their data stays stored and is still part of every backup.
- **Map & WhatsApp:** events with a location have "Show on map"; for people with a birthday the button opens WhatsApp with a greeting
  (you pick the contact in WhatsApp).
